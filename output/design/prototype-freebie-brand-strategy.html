03 — Your Brand Voice

Your Tone

Warm and direct. Like a friend who's been through it and isn't afraid to say the real thing. Not a therapist, not a motivational speaker — just honest.

✓ Do This

Short sentences. Questions that land. Start with feeling before advice. Use "you" not "women". Say the uncomfortable thing first.

✗ Avoid This

Toxic positivity. Hustle language. "Empower" and "transform". Overly formatted lists. Perfect grammar at the expense of realness.

Phrases That Sound Like You
  • "Here's what nobody told me about..."
  • "Let me be honest for a second."
  • "This is the part where most people give up."
  • "I know because I lived it."
  • "You don't need to have it figured out."
