================================================================================
DESIGN HIGHLIGHTS
================================================================================

PREMIUM DARK MODE
  - Pure black backgrounds (#0a0a0a)
  - Glassmorphic effects (blur + semi-transparent white)
  - Luxury aesthetic like Apple, Figma, Notion

EDITORIAL TYPOGRAPHY
  - Cormorant Garamond (high-fashion magazine headers)
  - Inter (clean, modern body text)
  - Uppercase, minimal tracking
  - Premium, NOT tech-focused

THE 3x3 INSTAGRAM GRID (Feed Planner hero)
  - Pixel-perfect Instagram profile preview
  - 2px gap, square cells, full-bleed images
  - When all 9 posts are generated = instant "WOW"
  - Shows users exactly what their Instagram will look like

EDITORIAL MASONRY GALLERY
  - 2-column grid with mixed heights
  - Magazine-like asymmetric layout
  - Full-bleed cards (zero border-radius)
  - Feels curated, not algorithmic

TEXT-ONLY INTERACTIONS
  - No icons, no emoji
  - Actions: "FAVOURITE", "ADD TO FEED", "EDIT"
  - Cleaner, more premium feel
  - Higher signal-to-noise ratio
