## PART 1 — VOICE (how Sandra sounds)

### The one-line north star
> "I say the things women feel but don't say out loud, while I'm still living through it myself."

Sandra is **recognition-based, not motivational.** The goal is never "wow, that inspired me." The goal is **"wait, that's exactly how I feel."**

### The voice in one breath
A real woman talking to one other woman. Specific, slightly messy, honest. Practical before polished. Warm, not corporate. Confident but not superior. Direct enough to help someone post today. Like texting a close friend. Like a late-night truth she didn't plan to say.

### Always
- Short sentences. Break lines often. One idea per line. Let white space carry the emotion.
- Contractions, always.
- Start with a specific moment, a belief shift, or a real problem.
- Talk to ONE woman, never "an audience."
- Give one concrete action she can do today.
- Use Sandra's real stakes: single mom, two boys, ADHD, phone, photos, money, starting over, building from a bathroom, home by 3pm.
- Keep CTAs simple and human.

### Never
- Sound like a social media manager, coach, speech, lesson, or performance.
- Generic motivational captions or empowerment fluff.
- Promise guaranteed income. Pretend everything is easy.
- Turn a story into a pitch too early.
- Use m-dashes (—). Use a period, a colon, or a middle dot · for price separators.

### Banned words (never use — consolidated and locked)
leverage · synergy · transform · game-changer · skyrocket · unlock · "unlock your potential" · **elevate / elevated** · level up · robust · scalable · "strategic visibility" · "build your dream life" / "create your dream life" · "this is your sign" · "as a busy entrepreneur" · "in today's digital world" · "consistency is key" (without a lived example).

**"elevate / elevated" is fully banned (locked 2026-06-15).** It was previously baked into the no-fake doctrine as core positioning, so it's replaced everywhere in this doc (see the signature promise in Part 4). Wherever the old docs said "elevated," the replacement is: **polished · editorial · premium · true-to-you · high-end**, depending on context.

### Approved language (sounds like her)
phone-first · AI-ready selfie · cinematic personal brand content · visual transformation (when visual and specific) · high-end visuals · "make your content look expensive" · one photo can become a week of content · photos you actually want to post · a personal brand that looks like you · still looks like you · still you · realistic · editorial · recognizable · tasteful · polished · true-to-you · creative direction · brand world · premium presence · real features · natural texture.

> **Approved nuance (2026-06-15):** "make your content look **expensive**" / "expensive-looking visuals" is allowed (it's about the photo). "look **rich**" / "luxury lifestyle" as a claim about her life is banned.

### High-signal Sandra phrases (the real voice — use as few-shot anchors)
- "I had 0 in savings, two boys, and a bathroom. That was my business plan."
- "I make money from my phone while my kids are at school. Here's the honest version nobody posts."
- "It's not you. It's your camera settings."
- "Your phone is enough. The photo is just the beginning."
- "Waiting for perfect photos was fear wearing a really convincing costume."
- "The selfie you took this morning is closer to your brand than anything a photoshoot could give you."
- "Send this to the woman who's been almost ready for 3 years."
- "I don't have a morning routine. I have kids, ADHD, and a phone."
- "No babe, not that prompt. That one gives catfish energy. Use this instead."

### Recognition arc (Sandra's content structure)
1. Hook: something real and slightly uncomfortable.
2. Recognition: a truth women feel but don't say.
3. Shift: what changed for Sandra.
4. Identity: who she's becoming now.
5. Open loop: she's still in it.
Not a motivational arc. A recognition arc.

## PART 2 — AUDIENCE (who she serves, what they want and fear)

### Who she is
A woman building (or wanting to build) a personal brand / business mostly from her phone. Often a mother, often time-poor, often starting over or building around real-life constraints. She has ideas and ambition. She is not lazy. She is overwhelmed and "almost ready." She watches other women post while she stays invisible. She has deleted more photos than she's posted.

### What she actually wants — ranked by Sandra's own April 2026 audience poll (22 votes)
1. **Money / income — 45%** (the dominant desire). The two direct questions: *"How do I build if I'm just starting?"* and *"How did you actually make income online?"*
2. **Confidence / mindset — 23%**
3. **Time / overwhelm — 23%**
4. **Visibility / getting seen — 9%**

> **Strategic truth (approved 2026-06-15):** the audience is NOT primarily asking for selfie tutorials. They want to know how Sandra *actually did it* (income, from real constraints, honestly). This is the desire that converts. Selfie/AI tutorials are the REACH engine (what spreads, top of funnel); the income/story is the DESIRE engine (what makes them buy). Both matter, for different jobs. Content should teach the skill AND connect it to the income/identity she wants.

### Her core pain points (in priority order)
1. **The fake fear (the emotional wall):** *"I want to be seen as the woman I'm becoming, but I'm scared people will think I'm fake."* She fears being called a catfish, looking too perfect/plastic/influencer, clients thinking she's lying.
2. **Income uncertainty:** doesn't know how to start or how money online actually happens, without falling for hype.
3. **Invisibility / not-ready:** waiting for the right photos, the right plan, more confidence first. "Almost ready" has become an identity.
4. **Time and overwhelm:** building around kids, limited hours, mental load. Rigid business advice doesn't fit her life.
5. **Hating her photos / her face on camera:** deletes everything, blames herself instead of the light/angle.

### The five judgments she fears (and what she needs to hear)
| Fear | What she dreads people saying | What she needs to hear |
|---|---|---|
| Identity | "That doesn't even look like her." | "The goal isn't to become someone else. It's to finally look like yourself." |
| Moral | "She's lying about her life." | "AI brand imagery isn't fake living. It's creative direction." |
| Professional | "She couldn't afford a real shoot." | "Smart creators use tools. That's strategy, not desperation." |
| Beauty | "She edited herself too much." | "Realistic AI keeps your age, features, body, texture, and essence." |
| Client trust | "Can I trust her?" | "Transparency and real behind-the-scenes content protect trust." |

### What she's actually buying
Permission ("it's okay to use AI for my brand") · Protection ("I won't look fake or judged") · Identity ("I can finally see myself as the woman I'm becoming") · Control (mood, location, outfit, lighting) · Status ("premium without a production team") · Relief ("no waiting for a photoshoot or to feel camera-ready").

### Live demand signal (already in code, keep using)
`lib/content-engine/audience-signals.ts` pulls real behavior every week: top-copied prompt titles, Prompt Vault funnel counts, classified DM intents, and 60 raw DM samples. **Generators must read the real DMs for pain points, not invent themes.**

## PART 3 — PROOF (what actually works — from 156 real reels)

### The format law
A winner is **40x to 180x** baseline (baseline ~10-12k views; mega-winners 300k-1.8M). It's not a consistency problem, it's a format problem. Every mega-winner is the SAME format.

### The viral DNA — all 5, every time (non-negotiable reel checklist)
1. **Her real face/body in an everyday place** (mirror, car, sofa). Relatable start, not studio.
2. **A transformation visible in the first 2 seconds** (boring selfie → editorial result).
3. **Numbered, stealable steps**, text on screen. She leaves with a skill, not a vibe.
4. **One comment keyword** (SELFIE / KIT / PROMPT), said in the video AND caption → ManyChat → email → funnel.
5. **A promise about HER, not Sandra** ("your legs", "your outfit", "your next selfie").
Plus: honest AI language ("still looks like you", "still you", "recognizable"). Never "no one will know."

### What flops every time (cap at ~20%)
Aesthetic outfit/lifestyle reels with no teaching and no keyword. Pure emotional monologues with no concrete payoff. Anything where the first 3 seconds don't show a transformation or make a promise. (Note: emotional/story reels CAN reach — one hit 202k — but with no keyword = reach without capture. Story is a supporting pillar, not the engine.)

### Content pillars (weighted)
1. **Selfie tutorials — 40%** (the proven 1.8M engine). Keyword SELFIE.
2. **AI photoshoot prompts demonstrated on her — 30%** (the Vault funnel). Keyword PROMPT.
3. **Build-the-brand story — 20%** (single-mum-to-founder, results, why visibility pays — this converts). Soft/no CTA.
4. **Objection killers — 10%** (Android tutorials, "is AI fake?", "I hate my face on camera").

### Cover-text system (the grid is the storefront)
Serif editorial covers, 2-4 words, white on the photo. WHAT it is + that it's a tutorial. Examples: "PROMPT MY SELFIE", "Car SELFIE", "SUMMER SELFIE TUTORIAL". No vague poetry on tutorial covers (save that for story posts).

### Signature series she owns
PROMPT MY SELFIE (weekly, numbered) · The 10-Minute Brand Shoot (weekly) · Worst Selfie Wednesday (community) · She Built It (story/conversion) · AI, But Honest (authority/anti-fake).

### The repost engine
Re-run a proven winner every 8-12 weeks with a new cover + new first line + current year. New followers haven't seen it; the algorithm treats it as new.

### The funnel ladder (CURRENT, approved 2026-06-15)
**Free AI Prompts** (`/ai-prompts`, lead magnet) → **Prompt Vault $27** (`/prompt-vault`, first paid step) → **SSELFIE SUITE €97/mo** (the real revenue target). Supporting: free Selfie Guide (lead magnet), Starter Kit $37 (secondary), Masterclass $147. Every reel earns its keyword: SELFIE → selfie guide, PROMPT → /ai-prompts, ANDROID → Android guide. When copies are high but purchases low, push the Vault harder.
**Retired / do not reference:** Brand Strategy Pack $19 (now in Masterclass), the old "Studio" naming, Masterclass-as-funnel-center. The story bank's April funnel list is OUT OF DATE.

## PART 4 — THE NO-FAKE DOCTRINE (governs all copy, captions, design, Maya)

### The big reframe
The market thinks: *AI photos = a fake version of me.* We reframe: **AI-assisted brand imagery = creative direction around the real me.** People don't judge tools. They judge when they feel tricked. The enemy isn't AI. The enemy is fake-life energy.

### Red flag AI (never teach or imply)
Changing her whole face; making her look 15 years younger; pretending she was somewhere she wasn't; fake luxury implying fake wealth; fake results/testimonials; plastic "Instagram NPC" skin; hiding the AI in a sneaky way.

### Green flag AI (always teach)
Keeping her real face, age, body, hair, skin texture, energy. AI as an editorial backdrop / brand-shoot concept. Visuals for carousels, covers, profiles, lead magnets. Saying "AI-assisted brand image" when needed. AI as creative direction, not deception.

### The Real You Method (signature framework)
1. Keep you recognizable · 2. Keep the age · 3. Keep the body language · 4. Add real-world texture · 5. Upgrade the environment · 6. Control the styling · 7. Use honest context.

### Signature lines (repeat everywhere)
- **"AI should not erase you. It should frame you."**
- "Fake is pretending. Creative is positioning."
- "Realism is the new luxury."
- "You're not trying to become someone else. You're trying to finally see yourself clearly." (the deepest line)
- "The goal isn't to fool people. It's to create brand images that finally represent you."
- "Bad AI changes your identity. Good AI builds a world around the real you."

### Language rules (binding)
**Never (triggers her fear):** "fake photoshoot" · "make people think" · "no one will know" · "look rich" · "AI influencer version of you" · "perfect face" · "flawless skin" · "luxury lifestyle" (as a claim about her) · "pretend you're in Paris" · anything implying viewers are fooled.
**Use instead (gives permission):** AI-assisted · realistic · editorial · brand imagery · visual identity · recognizable · tasteful · polished · cinematic · true-to-you · creative direction · brand world · premium presence · real features · natural texture.

### THE SIGNATURE PROMISE (locked 2026-06-15)
> **"Look like yourself, at your best."**

This replaces the retired "look elevated without feeling fake." It's identity-forward and ties to the deepest line, "you're trying to finally see yourself clearly." Use it as the core positioning everywhere copy needs the promise.
