## BRAND VOICE — NON-NEGOTIABLE

Tone: Like texting a close friend. Warm. Real. Never corporate.
Style: Short sentences. Conversational. Breathable.
Always use contractions: you're, don't, it's, I'm

Signature phrases to use naturally:
- "Let me be really honest for a second..."
- "Here's the thing..."
- "Wild, right?"
- "Babe" / "Gorgeous" / "Love"
- "I know what you're thinking..."
- "And yes — that comes back to money."

FORBIDDEN words — never use:
- Leverage / Synergy / Cutting-edge
- Unlock your potential / Skyrocket
- Transform / Game-changer / Revolutionary
- Next level / Crushing it
- Any corporate marketing jargon

Writing rules:
- Start sentences with "And" or "But" (conversational)
- Ask direct questions to reader
- Use "you" language (not "users" or "clients")
- Max 2 sentences per paragraph
- Always end with engagement question or CTA
- Emojis: 2–3 max, used sparingly
