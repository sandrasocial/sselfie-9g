# Sandra Voice Bible

Source restored on 2026-04-23 from `docs/_archive/brand/VOICE_BIBLE.md`.

## Purpose

Use this voice system for:

1. SSELFIE landing pages and product copy.
2. Lifecycle emails, nurture sequences, and DM drafts.
3. Agent-authored outward-facing drafts across the repo.

## Brand Promise

SSELFIE helps women build visibility, clarity, and cashflow with simple systems, starting with the selfie and expanding into content and AI support without burnout and without pretending to be someone else.

## Voice Identity

Five words:

1. Warm
2. Real
3. Clear
4. Encouraging
5. Actionable

## Tone Rules

Always:

1. Speak directly to one person.
2. Use short sentences and short paragraphs.
3. Stay grounded and honest.
4. End with a clear next step.

Never:

1. Sound superior or preachy.
2. Use empty hype language.
3. Overcomplicate with jargon.

## Vocabulary

Prefer:

1. simple
2. quick win
3. next step
4. no stress
5. this works

Avoid:

1. unlock
2. game-changer
3. level up
4. transform
5. elevate
6. synergy
7. robust solution

## Core Messaging Truths

1. Visibility is the first transaction.
2. One good selfie can fuel weeks of content.
3. You do not need a perfect plan; you need a system.
4. AI is the layer that reduces repeated work, not the starting point.
5. Consistency beats perfection.

## Format Rules

1. One to two sentence paragraphs.
2. Bullets over long blocks when instructing.
3. Numbered steps when clarity matters.
4. Bold only key phrases.

## QA Gate

Before publishing any outward draft, check:

1. Voice match
2. Clarity
3. Emotional truth
4. Action clarity
5. Offer fit

If the draft sounds like marketing before it sounds like Sandra, rewrite it.
