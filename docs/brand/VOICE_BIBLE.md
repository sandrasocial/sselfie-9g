# SSELFIE Voice Bible

Last updated: 2026-05-21

For visual design decisions, follow `docs/SSELFIE_DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`. For outward-facing copy, follow this Voice Bible.

## Purpose

Use this voice system for:

1. Landing pages and product copy.
2. Lifecycle emails, nurture sequences, and DM drafts.
3. Ad hooks, Instagram captions, ManyChat copy, and agent-authored outward-facing drafts.

## Current Category Language

SSELFIE teaches women how to create cinematic, high-end personal brand content using their phone + AI.

Short version:

**Cinematic personal brand content using your phone + AI.**

## Brand Role

Sandra is the real human face, guide, and proof.

AI is the enhancer, not the identity.

SSELFIE does not sell random prompts, generic AI tools, or vague confidence. It helps women turn their face, phone photos, story, and existing life into visuals that feel expensive, real, and usable.

## Product Ecosystem

1. **Starter Kit** is the first paid step. It connects selfies, editing, posing, captions, and AI-ready visuals.
2. **Selfie education** is the foundation. Better input photos create better AI results.
3. **AI-ready selfies** are the bridge between phone photos and cinematic AI visuals.
4. **Email nurture and DM copy** are revenue-critical. They should feel clear, personal, and action-focused.
5. **Studio** is the future ongoing workspace and execution layer. It is not a pile of random AI features.

## Voice Identity

The SSELFIE voice is:

1. Warm
2. Real
3. Clear
4. Direct
5. Feminine but not fluffy
6. Premium but simple
7. Emotionally specific
8. Action-focused

## Copy Style

Use:

1. Short sentences.
2. Short paragraphs.
3. Everyday language.
4. Visual details.
5. Concrete outcomes.
6. A clear next step.

Avoid:

1. Corporate SaaS tone.
2. Generic coaching language.
3. Fake hype.
4. Over-explaining.
5. Provider-heavy AI language.
6. Long abstract claims.

If it sounds like a software company wrote it, rewrite it.

If it sounds like Sandra said it to one woman who wants better photos and clearer content, it is closer.

## Approved Language

Use language like:

1. phone-first
2. AI-ready selfie
3. cinematic personal brand content
4. visual transformation
5. high-end visuals
6. selfie system
7. simple content system
8. your phone is enough to start
9. better selfies create better AI results
10. show up with what you already have
11. one photo can become a week of content
12. make your content look expensive
13. photos you actually want to post
14. turn your phone photos into content
15. a personal brand that looks like you

Use `transformation` when it is visual and specific. Do not use it as empty hype.

## Language To Avoid

Avoid:

1. generic AI creator language
2. generic empowerment fluff
3. unlock
4. game-changer
5. level up
6. synergy
7. robust solution
8. scalable creator operating system
9. strategic visibility
10. dark SaaS language
11. fake urgency
12. vague build your dream life claims
13. provider names as benefits
14. technical model language in public copy

## Funnel Copy Rules

1. PROMPT content should create curiosity and action.
2. Starter Kit copy should connect selfies, editing, posing, captions, and AI-ready visuals.
3. Email copy should feel like a short editorial note from Sandra, not a corporate newsletter.
4. Sales copy should sell the visual outcome, not just the tool.
5. Studio copy should position the app as the ongoing execution layer, not as random AI features.

## Core Messaging Truths

1. Your phone is enough to start.
2. Better selfies create better AI results.
3. One good photo can become a week of content.
4. The original image matters. AI cannot fully fix a weak starting point.
5. Sandra is the proof that this can look high-end without a studio.
6. Visibility is easier when the visuals finally feel like you.
7. The system should make posting feel lighter, not more complicated.

## Voice Examples

Bad:

> Unlock your next level with AI-powered content tools.

Good:

> You do not need more random tools. You need one simple way to turn your phone photos into content you actually want to post.

Bad:

> Transform your brand with a robust creator operating system.

Good:

> Your personal brand starts looking different when your photos finally match the woman you are becoming.

Bad:

> Use our AI solution to generate scalable content assets.

Good:

> Upload the selfie. Choose the look. Let Maya help you turn it into a photo, caption, and post idea you can actually use.

Bad:

> Level up your visibility strategy with premium content prompts.

Good:

> Start with one strong photo. Then turn it into the caption, post, and next step your audience can understand.

Bad:

> Build your dream life with AI.

Good:

> Make your content look more like the business you are already building.

## Format Rules

1. One to two sentence paragraphs.
2. Bullets over long blocks when instructing.
3. Numbered steps when clarity matters.
4. One primary CTA.
5. Bold only key phrases.
6. No emojis unless Sandra explicitly asks.

## QA Gate

Before publishing any outward draft, check:

1. Voice match
2. Clarity
3. Emotional truth
4. Visual specificity
5. Action clarity
6. Offer fit

If the draft sells the tool before it sells the visual outcome, rewrite it.

If the draft sounds like marketing before it sounds like Sandra, rewrite it.
