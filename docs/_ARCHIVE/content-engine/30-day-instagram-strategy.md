# 30-DAY INSTAGRAM STRATEGY
## Brand Engine Sales Campaign | Feb 16 - Mar 17, 2026

**Sandra "The Selfie Queen" - SSELFIE Studio**

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**Primary Goal:** Drive qualified Brand Engine purchases through Instagram content
**Target Audience:** Female entrepreneurs 32-44, recently divorced/separated, $0-$80K income
**Core Message:** Visibility = wealth. Your selfies are your most powerful marketing tool.
**Recommended Cadence:** 5x/week (Mon-Fri) to protect family time while maintaining momentum
**Success Metrics:** Saves >5%, Profile visits +30%, DM inquiries +50%, Brand Engine sales tracking

**Strategic Approach:**
This 30-day plan prioritizes *qualified leads* over vanity metrics. Every post ties to business objectives: awareness, consideration, or conversion. Content is designed for low production overhead using your existing selfie assets while maintaining Scandinavian luxury aesthetic.

## AUDIENCE INTELLIGENCE

### Primary Avatar: "The Rebuilding Entrepreneur"
- **Age:** 32-44
- **Status:** Recently divorced/separated or rebuilding after major life change
- **Income:** $0-$80K (feeling financially trapped)
- **Business:** Early-stage solopreneur or side hustler
- **Pain Points:**
  - Can't afford €1,500 brand photoshoots
  - Inconsistent online presence (lack of content)
  - Imposter syndrome about showing face
  - Tech overwhelm (knows AI exists, intimidated)
  - Time poverty (business + kids + life)

### What They're Scrolling For
- Proof it's possible (they need hope)
- Simple, actionable steps (not overwhelmed)
- Real talk about money and struggle
- Permission to start imperfectly
- Community of women like them

### Content They Save
- Before/after transformations
- Step-by-step tutorials they can recreate
- Money transparency (costs, earnings, investments)
- Relatable struggles + solutions
- "Swipe file" content they can reference later

## CONTENT PILLARS (Weekly Mix)

### 1. EDUCATIONAL (40% - 2 posts/week)
**Purpose:** Position as expert, drive saves
**Format:** Carousels, tutorial reels
**Topics:** Selfie techniques, AI workflows, personal branding basics
**CTA Focus:** "Save this for later" + follow

### 2. PERSONAL STORY (30% - 1-2 posts/week)
**Purpose:** Build trust, emotional connection
**Format:** Single photo + long caption, vulnerable reels
**Topics:** €12 beginning, divorce/rebuilding, single mom life, money truth
**CTA Focus:** DM engagement, shares

### 3. SOCIAL PROOF (20% - 1 post/week)
**Purpose:** Convert followers to customers
**Format:** Before/after, testimonial graphics, results screenshots
**Topics:** Your results, customer wins, platform growth, income transparency
**CTA Focus:** "Link in bio" or "Comment BRAND for details"

### 4. ENGAGEMENT (10% - Stories daily)
**Purpose:** Algorithm boost, audience insights
**Format:** Stories with polls, questions, BTS moments
**Topics:** Daily life, quick tips, audience questions
**CTA Focus:** DM replies, poll responses

---

## BRAND ENGINE SALES FUNNEL

### Awareness → Consideration → Conversion

**Week 1-2: AWARENESS**
- Hook with transformation stories
- Establish credibility (your journey)
- Demonstrate expertise (tutorials)
- Build trust (vulnerability)

**Week 3: CONSIDERATION**
- Showcase results (yours + clients)
- Address objections ("I'm not tech-savvy")
- Compare alternatives (photoshoots vs. AI)
- Tease Brand Engine value

**Week 4: CONVERSION**
- Direct Brand Engine promotion
- Limited-time offer or bonus
- Testimonials and case studies
- Strong CTAs with urgency
