## PRODUCTION EFFICIENCY TIPS

### Batch Filming Strategy
**Single 2-Hour Session Can Produce:**
- 5-7 talking-to-camera reels (different topics)
- 100+ selfies for transformation demonstrations
- 3-4 tutorial screen recordings
- All B-roll for the week

### Equipment Needed
- iPhone (any recent model)
- Natural window light
- Tripod or phone stand (€15-30)
- CapCut or InShot for editing (free)

### Editing Shortcuts
- Use CapCut templates (90% of work done)
- Add captions with auto-caption feature (accessibility + watch-time)
- Apply same color grade across all reels (brand consistency)
- Keep transitions simple (cuts work best)

### Reels That Perform Best
✅ Face visible in first frame
✅ Hook text on screen (not just voiceover)
✅ Captions for sound-off viewing
✅ 9:16 vertical format (full-screen)
✅ 30-60 second length (sweet spot)
✅ Clear value delivered quickly

---

## THUMBNAIL BEST PRACTICES

### Elements of High-CTR Thumbnails
1. **Your Face:** Close-up, expression matches content
2. **Text Overlay:** 3-5 words max, Cormorant Garamond font
3. **High Contrast:** Obsidian text on Porcelain background OR vice versa
4. **Emotion/Intrigue:** Curiosity, surprise, or direct promise
5. **Brand Consistency:** Recognizable as YOUR content

### Thumbnail Testing
- Create 2-3 thumbnail options
- Post reel, test thumbnails in first hour
- Keep best performer
- Note what works for future content
