## Stage 1: Free User (Just Signed Up, 2 Bonus Credits)

**Entry point:** Email link → `/studio` or `/blueprint` → app authenticated with 2 bonus credits
**Audience:** 14 new users/day (2026-02-25 cohort); 20 on 2026-02-23; 21 on 2026-02-22
**Evidence:** Funnel digest shows **0/14, 0/20, 0/21 first-output activation** across three recent daily cohorts. Free credits are granted but never spent (0 credit-spend events from bonus users in windows measured).

### Current State

Free users land in `/studio` or receive an email directing them to the app. They see:
- A working app interface with **2 bonus credits** in their balance (visible in nav or profile).
- **No onboarding prompt** specific to free users (unlike paid blueprint users who see a welcome wizard).
- **Maya tab accessible** but with no guidance on how to start (Classic vs Pro mode unclear; no "ask me for..." example).
- **Feed Planner tab accessible** but entry requires clicking past a main screen; limited preview only (1-post instead of 9-post).
- **Academy tab gated** by Studio membership; non-members see upgrade CTA.
- **Gallery tab visible** but image generation requires paid subscription.

**UX experience:** The app feels open but the next action is unclear. Free users have credits but no visible "start here" path.

### Desired Next Step in-App

Free users should land in a **guided first-generation flow** that leads them to:
1. **Maya with example prompt** (e.g. "Ask me for a casual style photo — I'll generate one with your 2 bonus credits").
2. **Or Feed Planner with a "quick-start 1-post preview"** CTA.
3. **Clear cost display** before generation (e.g. "This will use 2 of your 2 bonus credits").

After first generation (if successful), the next step should be:
- **In-app banner or modal:** "You generated your first photo! Next: [Ask Maya for more styles] or [Plan a full 9-post feed]" (with Paid Blueprint CTA).

### Gaps

1. **No welcome/first-time flow:** Free users receive credits but no "start here" guidance. Unlike paid blueprint users (who see a welcome wizard), free users have no on-ramp.
2. **Zero activation:** 0% of free-credit users generate anything (0/14, 0/20, 0/21). The barrier to first output is too high.
3. **Mode confusion:** Maya has two modes (Classic vs Pro) with no default or explanation visible on first visit.
4. **No in-app next-best-action:** After signup, no modal, banner, or CTA surfaces "Generate your first photo" or "Explore Feed Planner."
5. **No re-engagement trigger:** If a user leaves without generating, there's no in-app nudge or email follow-up within the first 24h.

### Suggested Surface

1. **Welcome modal on first `/studio` load:**
   - **Header:** "You have 2 free credits. Let's create your first photo!"
   - **Two CTAs:**
     - **"Generate with Maya"** → deep link to Maya with inline prompt (e.g. "Try asking: 'casual style photo'").
     - **"See a Preview"** → deep link to Feed Planner, 1-post preview.
   - **Dismiss option:** "Skip for now" (but re-surface in sidebar or nav badge).

2. **Maya tab first-message placeholder (if Classic mode is default):**
   - **Text:** "Hi! I'm Maya. You have **2 free credits**. Ask me for any style (casual, luxury, editorial, etc.) and I'll generate a photo for you. Ready?"
   - **Cost disclaimer:** Below chat input: "Generating one photo costs 2 credits. You have 2 available."

3. **Feed Planner "Quick Start" card (if user clicks Feed Planner):**
   - **Title:** "Your First Feed Preview"
   - **Description:** "Generate a 1-post preview with your 2 free credits. (Full 9-post feeds available with Paid Blueprint.)"
   - **CTA:** "Generate Preview"

4. **In-app banner post-generation (after first output succeeds):**
   - **Location:** Top of Maya or Feed Planner screen.
   - **Message:** "Great! Your photo is ready. [Explore more styles] or [Plan a full feed for €47] →"

## Stage 2: Paid Blueprint Buyer (€47 One-Time, 60 Credits)

**Entry point:** User buys Paid Blueprint from `/checkout/blueprint` → webhook grants 60 credits and `paid-blueprint-buyer` tag → redirects to `/checkout/success` → then to `/studio` or `/feed-planner`.
**Audience:** 13 active paid-blueprint subscriptions (subscription audit); 0 new subscriptions in last 24h (funnel digest).
**Evidence:** Feed Planner pain doc (§7) shows **"Zero activation conversion"**: 0/21, 0/20, 0/14 bonus users generated credits in Feb 22–25 windows. Only **1 "Continue" click** across three days (activation-continue link to first generation is a drop-off cliff).

### Current State

Paid blueprint buyers are sent to `/studio` or are redirected to `/feed-planner` after purchase. They see:
- **60 credits** in balance (enough for ~30 9-post feeds at 2 credits per scene × 9 scenes, or 18 credits per full grid).
- **Feed Planner welcome wizard** (Step: onboarding if not completed, then welcome wizard saying "You're all set!" + suggestion to use credits in Maya).
- **Feed Planner tab active** by default; shows feed list and "Create Feed" button.
- **Maya tab available** but the welcome wizard mentions "Use your credits in Maya..." which is confusing because Maya Feed tab is disabled.
- **No clear "Create your first feed" CTA** after wizard completes; users must click "Create Feed" manually.
- **Activation checklist** shown, with "Continue" button (link to first generation). But **0 clicks on this button** across recent cohorts = wizard is the blocker, not the checklist.

**UX experience:** User completes wizard and sees the app, but the next action is ambiguous. The wizard says "you're all set" but doesn't say "click here to generate." The Feed Planner creation flow is perceived as complex (multiple steps to set strategy, then generate).

### Desired Next Step in-App

Paid blueprint buyers should land in **Feed Planner with a clear "Create your first 9-post feed" flow** that:
1. **Skips or simplifies the onboarding/welcome wizard** (currently a drop-off cliff).
2. **Shows a "Quick-Start Feed" template** or one-click "Generate Sample Feed" to accelerate first output.
3. **After first feed generates, surfaces next actions:** "Next: [Ask Maya to plan more weeks] or [Upgrade to unlimited with Creator Studio]."

### Gaps

1. **Wizard is a drop-off cliff:** Almost no users reach first feed generation. The onboarding and welcome wizards block the funnel. Only 1 "Continue" click in 3 days means users are abandoning after wizard, not after first generation (evidence from activation-continue button).
2. **Feed Planner workflow is over-engineered:** V1/V2 iterations left the product complex (set strategy, pick scenes, wait for generation, etc.). Free users get limited preview; the jump to "full 9-post feed" feels overwhelming.
3. **Maya confusion:** Welcome wizard says "use credits in Maya" but Maya Feed tab is disabled and Feed Planner is the actual "Blueprint funnel" surface. Users are directed to the wrong place.
4. **No "first feed" template or quick-start:** Unlike the "quick-start 1-post preview" free users might need, paid blueprint users need a "generate sample 9-post feed in 2 clicks" path. This is missing.
5. **No in-app post-purchase moment:** User is sent to app after purchase, but there's no modal or banner saying "Your 60 credits are ready — here's what to do next."

### Suggested Surface

1. **Post-purchase redirect and modal (at `/studio` or `/feed-planner`):**
   - **Header:** "Welcome! Your 60 credits are here."
   - **Message:** "You're ready to create a full 9-post Instagram feed. We'll handle the strategy and photos. Let's go."
   - **Two CTAs:**
     - **"Create My First Feed"** → skip wizard, go straight to "Feed Template Picker" or preset strategy.
     - **"Learn More"** → link to Academy or FAQ (low friction).

2. **Feed Planner "Quick-Start Template" (simplify wizard):**
   - **Replace multi-step wizard with single "Pick a Template" screen.**
   - **Pre-populated templates** (e.g. "Luxury Personal Brand," "Service Provider Showcase," "Content Creator") with one-click "Generate" button.
   - **After template selected, auto-generate a sample 9-post feed** using preset scenes and user's brand profile (if captured in signup).
   - **Cost display:** "This feed uses 18 credits. You have 60 available."

3. **In-app banner post-first-generation (Feed Planner):**
   - **Location:** Above feed grid or in a card.
   - **Message:** "Your first feed is ready! [Regenerate] or [Plan another week with Maya (€97/mo)] →"
   - **CTA:** Links to Maya or Membership checkout.

4. **Feed Planner nav badge (if user hasn't generated):**
   - **Badge color/style:** Attention-getting (e.g. gold or accent color).
   - **Label:** "New: Generate your first feed"
   - **Click:** Leads to Quick-Start Template.

## Stage 3: Creator Studio Member (€97/Month, 200 Credits/Month)

**Entry point:** User buys Creator Studio from `/checkout/membership` → webhook grants 200 monthly credits + `studio-member` tag → redirects to `/checkout/success` → then to `/studio` with full app access.
**Audience:** 17 active sselfie_studio_membership subscriptions (subscription audit); 0 new subscriptions in last 24h (funnel digest).
**Evidence:** New Studio users (14 in last 24h, funnel digest) have full app access, but **0% generated any image** (0/14 first-output activation). Maya pain doc (§7) shows **3.1% overall conversion rate** (17 active members of 547 total users).

### Current State

Creator Studio members land in `/studio` with:
- **200 monthly credits** visible in nav.
- **Brand onboarding wizard** (if brand profile not completed): captures business info, target audience, content pillars, aesthetic, voice/style.
- **Full Maya access** including **Training tab** (custom model training, membership-only).
- **Full Feed Planner access** (unlimited 9-post grids, 200 monthly credits).
- **Academy tab** showing courses, templates, monthly drops, flatlay images (all Studio membership benefits).
- **Gallery tab** for AI photo generation (if not using Maya).
- **No indication of Academy mini-products purchased externally** (What To Say, Show Up, Get Paid, etc. bought on `/academy` are not surfaced in-app).

**UX experience:** Member has access to everything but the brand profile is often incomplete, which degraded Maya output quality. Academy mini-products are siloed on public `/academy`; there's no in-app awareness that the user has purchased them or where to use them.

### Gaps

1. **Brand profile incompleteness:** Members often skip or partially complete the brand onboarding wizard. Incomplete brand data → Maya lacks context → lower quality outputs.
2. **Academy mini-products invisible in-app:** A Studio member may have purchased "What To Say" (€17) on `/academy` but has no in-app signal that they own it. The purchase is isolated from the app experience. (Academy pain doc §7.)
3. **No in-app product activation:** Even if a member has an Academy product, there's no "You have What To Say — [start using it in Feed Planner]" card or badge in the Academy tab.
4. **Post-purchase email + in-app mismatch:** Post-purchase email for Academy products links back to `/academy` or a static success page, not into the app.
5. **No funnel-stage-aware Maya system prompt:** Maya doesn't know which products the user has purchased; she can't suggest "You have Caption Pack — want to generate caption-friendly styles?"

### Suggested Surface

1. **In-app post-purchase modal (if member buys Academy product while in-app):**
   - **Header:** "You now have [Product Name]!"
   - **Message:** "Great! Here's how to use it:"
   - **Contextual CTA:** Based on product:
     - **"What To Say" → "Generate captions in Feed Planner"** (links to Feed Planner with a note/banner).
     - **"Show Up" → "Plan your brand positioning with Maya"** (links to Maya with a system-prompt note).
     - **"Get Paid" → "Monetization strategy in Academy"** (links to Academy course).

2. **Academy tab "You own X products" badges (for non-member Academy buyers):**
   - **Location:** Top of Academy tab or as cards in overview.
   - **Design:** Small badge or card showing product name + "Start using" CTA.
   - **Example:** Card says "What To Say — [Start in Feed Planner] or [Ask Maya]".
   - **Data source:** Check `user_tags` or `academy_course_purchases` for products user owns.

3. **Maya system-prompt injection (product-aware context):**
   - **When member has purchased products, include in system context:**
     - "User owns: [What To Say, Show Up, Get Paid]. Mention relevant products in suggestions."
   - **First message adaptation:**
     - Standard (no products): "Hi, I'm Maya. What style would you like to generate?"
     - With products: "I see you have [Caption Pack]. Want me to help you generate caption-friendly styles or plan a week of posts?"

4. **Academy tab enhancement (mini-product awareness for non-members):**
   - **If user is not a Studio member but has purchased on `/academy`, show:**
     - "You own: [What To Say]" badge with "[Start here]" link to product detail or Feed Planner.
   - **If user is a Studio member, add subsection:**
     - "Your purchases: [What To Say, Show Up]" with quick-links to use them.

5. **Brand profile incomplete reminder:**
   - **In-app banner/card:** "Completing your brand profile helps Maya generate better photos. [Finish now] (5 min)"
   - **Location:** Top of Maya tab or in onboarding checklist.

## Stage 4: Academy Mini-Product Buyer (What To Say / Show Up / Get Paid / AI Photo Prompt Pack)

**Entry point:** User buys an Academy mini-product on `/academy` → Stripe checkout → webhook grants product access → redirects to `/academy/success` (static success page).
**Audience:** Unknown specific cohort count; funnel digest shows 0 new subscriptions (Creator Studio upsell) in last 24h, indicating mini-product → membership conversion is broken. (Academy pain doc §7: "Path from public Academy mini-product purchase → Creator Studio €97/mo subscription is broken.")
**Evidence:** Academy pain doc (§7) lists "Post-purchase dead end: After buying an Academy mini-product, user receives success page (`/academy/success`) but no in-app entry point or deep link to next-best-action."

### Current State

Academy mini-product buyers:
- **Receive a purchase confirmation email** from Stripe or Resend.
- **Land on `/academy/success`**, a static page with links to their products (e.g. "View What To Say").
- **Can view their product** on `/academy/products/[productId]` if logged in (HTML course viewer or content page).
- **Have no in-app deep link** to the Studio app or to a contextual feature (Feed Planner, Maya, etc.).
- **Do not receive an in-app notification** that they now own the product.
- **No visible "next step" in the success page** encouraging them to go to the Studio app or take action.

**UX experience:** Purchase is complete, but the value activation is unclear. The user reads the success page, views the product once, but has no prompt to actually use it in-app or explore Creator Studio.

### Gaps

1. **Post-purchase dead end at `/academy/success`:** The success page is static; it does not offer an in-app deep link or next-best-action CTA.
2. **No in-app awareness of purchase:** If the user opens the Studio app after purchasing, there's no badge, banner, or card saying "You now have What To Say."
3. **No in-app product activation:** Even if the user navigates to Feed Planner or Maya, there's no suggestion or prompt to use the product they just bought.
4. **No Creator Studio upsell momentum:** The mini-product purchase is isolated from the in-app funnel. There's no in-app moment where the user is encouraged to upgrade to Creator Studio for more products or unlimited features.
5. **Email + in-app disconnect:** Post-purchase email is generic (Stripe receipt); it does not include an app link or "next step" copy.

### Suggested Surface

1. **Post-purchase modal/page enhancement (`/academy/success`):**
   - **Current:** Static page with links to products.
   - **Desired:** Add a prominent section:
     ```
     "Ready to use [Product]?

     [Open Studio App] → Go straight to [Feed Planner / Maya]
     Or [View Your Course] → Read the lessons
     ```
   - **Deep link:** Button targets `sselfiestudio://feed-planner?product=what-to-say` or similar deep-link format.

2. **In-app product purchase modal (if user buys while in-app):**
   - **After checkout redirect to `/academy/success?next=feed-planner`:**
   - **App intercepts the `next` parameter** and shows a modal:
     - **Header:** "You own [What To Say]!"
     - **Message:** "Great! Here's the best way to use it:"
     - **CTA:** "Start in [Feed Planner / Maya]" based on product type.
     - **Secondary CTA:** "[View Course]" or "[Skip]".

3. **Studio app Academy tab product badges (for mini-product owners):**
   - **In Academy tab, add a "Your Products" section at the top:**
   - **Cards for each purchased product:** "What To Say — [Start using in Feed Planner]"
   - **Data source:** Check `academy_course_purchases` table or `user_tags` for product ownership.

4. **In-app post-purchase email copy:**
   - **Subject:** "Your [Product] is ready — start using it now"
   - **Body includes:**
     - "You bought [What To Say]. Here's the best next step:"
     - "[Open Studio App]" → deep link to Feed Planner or Maya.
     - "You can also [upgrade to Creator Studio for unlimited content tools]" → membership checkout.

5. **Feed Planner / Maya contextual prompts (for product owners):**
   - **When user opens Feed Planner and owns "What To Say":**
     - **In-app banner:** "You have [What To Say]. Generate captions-focused styles: [Try Now]"
   - **When user opens Maya and owns "Show Up":**
     - **Maya first message:** "I see you have [Show Up]. Want me to help you plan your positioning and generate matching photos?"

## Cross-Funnel Pattern: Post-Purchase In-App Handoff

**Applied to:** All paid products (Paid Blueprint, Creator Studio, Academy mini-products).

### Current State
- User buys product on external checkout → redirected to success page → receives email → **no in-app follow-up or CTA**.
- If user happens to open app, there's **no signal** that they just purchased or what to do next.

### Desired Pattern
1. **Post-purchase email includes app deep link:** "Open Studio App → [next feature]"
2. **App intercepts post-purchase state** (via URL param or email tracking) and shows an **in-app modal or banner:** "You just purchased [Product]. Here's your next step: [CTA]"
3. **In-app modal includes:**
   - Confirmation of purchase.
   - **Contextual CTA** (e.g. "Start in Feed Planner" for caption products, "Plan with Maya" for strategy products).
   - **Secondary CTA** to dismiss or learn more.

### Benefits
- **Clear post-purchase momentum:** User doesn't leave the funnel after checkout.
- **Activation signal:** Purchased products are immediately visible and actionable in-app.
- **Funnel continuity:** Free → Paid Blueprint → Creator Studio → Academy products all have in-app next steps.
