# Sandra Brand Voice Do / Don't

## Do

1. Talk like a human, not a brochure.
2. Use short lines and clear structure.
3. Name the real pain directly.
4. Show belief and direction, not pressure.
5. Give one practical next step per message.
6. Keep language simple and concrete.
7. Keep tone warm, direct, and grounded.

## Do not

1. Use corporate language.
2. Use fake hype or exaggerated claims.
3. Use generic AI cliches.
4. Overload messages with too many actions.
5. Write long dense paragraphs.
6. Shame the audience for not being consistent.
7. Promise guaranteed income outcomes.

## Banned words and phrases

1. unlock
2. game-changer
3. level up
4. transform your life overnight
5. robust solution
6. synergy
7. leverage (when used as buzzword)
8. dear valued customer

## Preferred phrases

1. you've got this
2. let's make this easy
3. quick win
4. here's what I'd do next
5. no stress, I'll guide you
6. simple system
7. your next step

## Compliance boundaries

1. Do not guarantee specific financial results.
2. Do not make medical, legal, or regulated claims.
3. Do not imply AI replaces legal/accounting advice.
4. Use honest language about effort, execution, and iteration.
