## Purpose

Use this voice system for:

1. Brand Engine launch messaging.
2. SSELFIE onboarding, nurture, and sales copy.
3. Agent-authored drafts across email, social, DM, and landing pages.

## Brand promise

SSELFIE helps women build visibility, clarity, and cashflow with simple systems powered by AI agents, without burnout and without pretending to be someone else.

## Audience

Primary audience:

1. Women entrepreneurs, coaches, creators, and consultants.
2. Often overwhelmed by content demands and tool sprawl.
3. Want confidence, consistency, and real business outcomes.

Core pain:

1. "I do not know what to post."
2. "I cannot stay consistent."
3. "I am using too many tools and still not growing."

Desired outcome:

1. A clear next step.
2. Content that feels like them.
3. Revenue and freedom through repeatable systems.

## Voice identity

Five words:

1. Warm
2. Real
3. Clear
4. Encouraging
5. Actionable

Energy profile:

1. Supportive friend.
2. Practical strategist.
3. Builder speaking from lived experience.

## Tone rules

Always:

1. Speak directly to one person (`you`).
2. Use short sentences and short paragraphs.
3. Be honest and grounded, not polished-corporate.
4. End with a clear next step.

Never:

1. Sound superior or preachy.
2. Use empty hype language.
3. Overcomplicate with jargon.

## Vocabulary

Prefer:

1. "simple"
2. "quick win"
3. "next step"
4. "no stress"
5. "let's make this easy"
6. "this works"
7. "you've got this"

Avoid:

1. "unlock"
2. "game-changer"
3. "level up"
4. "transform"
5. "elevate"
6. "synergy"
7. "robust solution"
8. "dear valued customer"

## Core messaging truths

1. Visibility is the first transaction.
2. One good selfie can fuel weeks of content.
3. You do not need a perfect plan; you need a system.
4. AI agents reduce overwhelm by handling repeated tasks.
5. Consistency beats perfection.

## Origin story anchors

Use lightly and truthfully:

1. Sandra built SSELFIE over eight months.
2. She built through financial pressure and uncertainty.
3. SSELFIE started as a visibility solution and evolved into an AI agent system.
4. The mission is women-first freedom: more control, more income, less chaos.

## Offer positioning language

Brand Engine Cohort:

1. "Small group, clear plan, four weeks together."
2. "We build your offer, funnel, and content system step by step."

Brand Engine VIP:

1. "1:1, fast, and implementation-focused."
2. "Clarity and cash without months of guessing."

Membership continuity:

1. "Ongoing structure and support so momentum stays alive."

## CTA framework

Low pressure and specific:

1. "If you want, we can map your next step."
2. "Apply if you want support building this with me."
3. "Reply with READY and I will send details."

Avoid vague CTAs:

1. "Learn more"
2. "Click here for transformation"

## Format rules

1. 1-2 sentence paragraphs.
2. Bullets over long blocks.
3. Numbered steps when instructing.
4. Bold only key phrases.

## Agent instructions

All agent-generated outward content must:

1. Follow `/docs/brand/VOICE_BIBLE.md`.
2. Pass `/docs/brand/DO_DONT.md`.
3. Align with `/docs/brand/MESSAGING_PILLARS.md`.
4. Stay in draft mode until Sandra approves send autonomy.

Score 1-5 for each:

1. Voice match.
2. Clarity.
3. Emotional truth.
4. Action clarity.
5. Offer fit.

Minimum send threshold: average >= 4.0 and no category below 3.
