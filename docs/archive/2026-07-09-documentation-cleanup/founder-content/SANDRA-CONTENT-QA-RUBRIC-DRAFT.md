# Sandra Content QA Rubric

Status: Draft for Sandra approval  
Last updated: 2026-04-27  
Scope: Founder-only content system. Not for user-facing Maya/Studio agents.

## Purpose

This rubric exists because usable content for Sandra cannot be judged by "does this sound nice?" It must be judged by voice, audience demand, story specificity, platform fit, and funnel fit.

## Voice Filter

Sandra is not motivational.

Sandra is recognition-based.

The content should make the reader think:

> "Wait. That is exactly how I feel."

Every draft must feel:

- honest
- grounded
- self-aware
- calm
- reflective
- direct

Every draft must use:

- short sentences
- simple words
- frequent line breaks
- no em dashes
- one idea per line

## Mandatory Gates

Every brief must pass all three gates before drafting.

### 1. Performance Gate

Question:

> Does this idea connect to something that has performed or clearly underperformed before?

Pass examples:

- selfie tutorial content with saves/shares
- carousel that deepens a strong Reel topic
- founder story corrected with a practical bridge

Fail examples:

- idea chosen only because it sounds good
- generic trend with no SSELFIE angle
- content based only on likes

### 2. Audience-Demand Gate

Question:

> Is this answering what the audience is asking now?

Current demand signals:

- Money / Income: 45%
- Confidence / Mindset: 23%
- Time / Overwhelm: 23%
- Visibility / Getting seen: 9%
- Direct questions: how to build when starting, how Sandra made income online

Fail examples:

- seven days of only selfie tutorials
- ignoring income questions because selfie posts performed
- treating ManyChat keyword comments as complete audience research

### 3. Market-Pattern Gate

Question:

> Does this use a 2026 scroll-stop pattern?

Allowed triggers:

- pattern interrupt
- specificity shock
- identity mirror
- curiosity gap
- DM-share trigger

Fail examples:

- "5 tips for..."
- broad personal branding advice
- hook that takes longer than 3 seconds to land
- no face or clear identity in Reel first frame

## Draft Scorecard

Score each draft from 0 to 2.

| Category | 0 | 1 | 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sandra voice | sounds generic | partly Sandra | sounds postable |
| Recognition | motivational | relatable but broad | "that is exactly how I feel" |
| Specificity | vague | has one detail | has a scene/number/confession |
| Audience mirror | unclear audience | broad pain | Melissa sees herself immediately |
| Practical value | no action | soft lesson | clear action/save reason |
| Story | no story | generic story | lived Sandra moment |
| Funnel fit | no next step | loose CTA | clear offer path |
| Platform fit | wrong format | usable | optimized for Reel/Carousel/Story |
| Safety | risky claim | mostly safe | no overpromise |
| Punctuation | uses em dashes | mostly clean | no em dashes |

Minimum publish-ready score: 16 out of 20.

## Rejection Labels

Sandra can label a draft:

- `sounds_like_me`
- `does_not_sound_like_me`
- `too_salesy`
- `too_generic`
- `needs_more_story`
- `needs_more_practical_value`
- `needs_more_specificity`
- `use_this_structure_again`

## Hook QA

A hook is rejected if:

- it could be posted by any creator
- it does not name a specific woman/problem
- it starts with "here are tips"
- it lacks tension
- it does not earn the next 3 seconds
- it sounds motivational instead of recognitional
- it uses an em dash

A hook is approved if it has at least one:

- private-feeling confession
- exact number or detail
- identity mirror
- unexpected belief shift
- share-trigger sentence

## Non-Negotiable Rewrites

Rewrite immediately if the draft uses:

- "unlock your potential"
- "step into your power"
- "level up"
- "this is your sign"
- broad coaching language
- em dashes
- polished speech energy
- filler lines that do not reveal anything real

## Income Content Safety

Allowed:

- Sandra's honest story
- what worked for Sandra
- principles around trust, visibility, offers, content, and conversations
- practical steps
- "this is not a guarantee" framing

Not allowed:

- guaranteed income claims
- "copy this and make X"
- passive-income hype
- implying everyone will get the same result
- pressure-based scarcity around financial fear

## Final Approval Question

Before Sandra posts, the agent should ask:

> Would Sandra post this with only light edits, or would she need to rewrite the whole thing?

If she would rewrite the whole thing, the draft failed.
