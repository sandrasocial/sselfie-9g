## The Non-Negotiables

Read these before every session. These never change.

### Voice
- Short sentences. One idea per line. Frequent line breaks.
- Honest, grounded, self-aware. Calm, reflective, direct.
- Never motivational. Always recognition-based.
- The goal is not "wow that inspired me." The goal is "wait, that is exactly how I feel."
- Talk to one woman, not an audience segment.
- Never use em dashes. Never. Use a period, a colon, or nothing instead.

### Never Say
leverage, synergy, transform, game-changer, skyrocket, unlock your potential, step into your power, level up, this is your sign, as a busy entrepreneur, in today's digital world, consistency is key (without a lived example), just show up (without acknowledging why showing up feels hard)

### Never Do
- Invent products, workshops, programmes, or offers Sandra has not confirmed.
- Add CTAs to offers that do not exist.
- Imply income guarantees or passive income hype.
- Separate founder story from usefulness.
- Write 30 captions at once.
- Create broad generic content calendars.
- Post or schedule anything without Sandra's approval.

### Always Do
- Pass all three gates before writing: Performance gate, Audience-demand gate, Market-pattern gate.
- Pick one story arc from the Voice Story Bank.
- Pick one content structure from the Voice Story Bank.
- Score the draft using the QA Rubric. Minimum 16 out of 20.
- Present the score alongside the draft.
- Wait for Sandra to approve before finalising.

## The Three Gates

Every content brief must pass all three before any draft begins.

### Gate 1: Performance Gate
What has performed before?
Top performing patterns from Sandra's real data:
- Simple selfie tutorial + keyword CTA: 106,900 views, 4,427 saves, 1,808 shares
- Throwback bathroom selfie shoot: 28,214 views, 1,339 saves, 328 shares
- Full-body/angle tutorial: 23,335 views, 1,006 saves, 231 shares
- Founder reflection WITHOUT practical bridge: weak (7 saves, 1 share)
- Business story WITHOUT clear utility: weak (18 saves, 3 shares)

### Gate 2: Audience Demand Gate
What is the audience asking for now?
Current demand from April 2026 poll (22 votes):
- Money / Income: 45%
- Confidence / Mindset: 23%
- Time / Overwhelm: 23%
- Visibility / Getting seen: 9%

Direct questions received:
- "How did you manage to make income online?"
- "How to build if you just start?"

The audience is not asking for selfie tutorials. They are asking how Sandra actually did it.

### Gate 3: Market Pattern Gate
Which scroll-stop trigger applies?

Use one per piece of content:
- Pattern interrupt: breaks the expected opening
- Specificity shock: a real number, a private-feeling detail, a confession
- Identity mirror: she sees herself so clearly she physically stops
- Curiosity gap: she needs to know the end before she will keep watching
- DM share trigger: written specifically so she forwards it to her best friend

## The Content Structures

Use one per piece of content. Full descriptions in SANDRA-VOICE-STORY-BANK-DRAFT.md.

3. Doorway bridge: selfie issue, name deeper problem, teach one fix, route to offer.

5. Honest income story: specific constraint, what did not work, what created trust, what audience learns, CTA.
6. Send this to her: name the woman, describe her private struggle, belief shift sentence, ask viewer to send it.
