## Brief

Pillar: Visibility  
Scroll-stop trigger: Identity mirror  
Primary offer: Selfie Guide / Starter Kit  
Main CTA: Save this, then DM SELFIE  
Goal: saves, shares, replies, and Selfie Guide entry

Core hook:

> I deleted 52 photos because I thought my face was the problem.

Why this should work:

- It starts with a private-feeling confession.
- It mirrors the woman who deletes her camera roll before posting.
- It connects a practical selfie fix to a deeper emotional block.
- It gives a clear save reason.

Risk:

- If it becomes only a lighting tip, it gets too small.
- The emotional mirror needs to stay in the first 3 seconds.

## Feed Asset

Format: Reel  
Length: 30 to 45 seconds  
First frame: Sandra's face, close, real light, no long intro  
Text overlay:

> I deleted 52 photos because I thought my face was the problem.

### Reel Shot List

1. Face to camera, direct, almost quiet.
2. Quick camera roll or phone-in-hand moment.
3. Bad setup demo: wrong light or awkward angle.
4. Fix demo: face window, set timer, drop exposure.
5. Result shot.
6. Closing face-to-camera CTA.

### Spoken Script

I deleted 52 photos because I thought my face was the problem.

It was not my face.

It was the light.
The angle.
The way I was standing there already annoyed at myself.

I used to open the camera, take ten photos, hate all of them, and decide I was just not photogenic.

But the first thing that actually changed my photos was so small.

I turned toward the window.
I stopped shooting from below.
I used the timer so my body had a second to relax.
And I dropped the exposure a little so the photo felt softer.

Same face.
Same phone.
Completely different feeling.

So if you keep deleting photos of yourself, please check the setup before you blame your face.

Save this for the next time you are about to delete the whole camera roll.

And DM SELFIE if you want the guide I wish I had when I was still deleting everything.
