# Sandra Content Strategy Playbook

Status: Draft for Sandra approval  
Last updated: 2026-04-27  
Scope: Founder-only content system. Not for user-facing Maya/Studio agents.

## Strategy Thesis

Sandra's content should not be a selfie tutorial account.

The strongest position is:

> I help women turn their real face, real story, and real life into visibility, trust, and income-ready content.

Selfie content is the first proof point. It is not the whole brand.

## Voice Positioning

Sandra is not motivational.

Sandra is recognition-based.

The content should make Melissa think:

> "Wait. That is exactly how I feel."

It should feel like:

- a late-night truth
- a message Sandra did not plan to say
- a woman telling the truth in real time
- calm, reflective, and direct

It should not feel like:

- coaching
- a lesson
- a performance
- a polished speech

## Audience Demand Order

Based on Sandra's recent poll:

1. Money / Income: 45%
2. Confidence / Mindset: 23%
3. Time / Overwhelm: 23%
4. Visibility / Getting seen: 9%

Direct question signal:

- "How to build if you just start?"
- "How did you manage to make income online?"

This means the content system must answer income and building questions every week, not only photo confidence.

## Content Pillars

### 1. Selfies As Confidence Tools

Core message:

> Your face is your brand. If people cannot see you, they cannot buy from you.

Job:

- connect selfie tips to visibility
- connect visibility to trust
- connect trust to income
- make the selfie feel useful, not vain

Topics:

- light direction
- camera settings
- bathroom/car/selfie setup
- one selfie into multiple content assets
- profile photo confidence
- "you are not unphotogenic" belief shifts

Hook examples:

- "I deleted 52 photos because I thought my face was the problem."
- "I deleted 52 photos before I posted the one that changed everything."
- "The photo that got me 300 DMs was taken in my bathroom."

Tutorial system:

- Hook in the first 0 to 3 seconds.
- Show the wrong way or the relatable mistake.
- Demo the fix with pose, angle, light, or phone setting.
- Explain one teaching point per beat.
- Show the glow-up from bad selfie to brand-level result.
- End with one CTA: save, send, DM SELFIE, or DM KIT.

Repurposing rule:

Every strong selfie tutorial should become:

1. original Reel
2. carousel from screenshots 2 to 4 weeks later
3. Story sequence
4. caption SEO post
5. Selfie Guide or Starter Kit asset
6. offer bridge into Strategy, Masterclass, or Studio

Source system:

- `docs/founder-content/SANDRA-SELFIE-TUTORIAL-REPURPOSING-SYSTEM-DRAFT.md`

- build trust without pretending to be finished

- starting with limited money

Hook examples:

## Weekly Content Rhythm

### Minimum Weekly Rhythm

- 1 Reel for reach: identity mirror or specificity shock.
- 1 Carousel for saves: deeper breakdown of the same topic 3 to 5 days later.
- Daily Stories for trust: real life, proof, polls, replies, and subtle pitch.

### Recommended Launch Week

| Day | Format | Pillar | Hook | CTA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | Reel | Visibility | "I deleted 52 photos because I thought my face was the problem." | Save this |
| Wednesday | Reel | Money / Income | "I made money from my phone while my kids were at school. Here's the honest version nobody posts." | Comment TELL ME MORE |
| Friday | Carousel | Visibility + Strategy | "The photo that got me 300 DMs was taken in my bathroom. Here's what I did differently." | Share this |
| Daily | Stories | Trust + Sales | real-life scenes, polls, proof, subtle pitch | Reply / DM |

## Topic Selection Rule

Every week must include:

- one Money / Income post
- one Visibility / Selfie post
- one Confidence or Time / Overwhelm post
- one Story or Proof sequence in Stories

If the week only includes selfie tutorials, the system has failed.

## Hard Rules

- No links in captions.
- No em dashes.
- No motivational coach language.
- No generic "5 tips" opener.
- No off-niche random posts during launch week.
- No income guarantee.
- No trend unless Sandra can make it more specific than the original.
- No post without a share/save reason.
- No content brief unless it passes performance, audience-demand, and market-pattern gates.
- No content that sounds like Sandra is trying to prove something.
- Recognition before inspiration.
