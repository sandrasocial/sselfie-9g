## Executive Decision

The next content focus should be **income-ready visibility**, not selfie tutorials alone.

Selfie utility is still the easiest doorway into Sandra's audience, but the deeper buying signal is money, freedom, and "how do I build something online that fits my real life?" The content system went wrong by treating top-performing selfie tutorials as the whole strategy instead of using them as proof of one entry point.

The corrected strategy is:

1. Use selfie/visibility posts to create saves, shares, and trust.
2. Use founder story to create emotional belief and authority.
3. Use income/motherhood/real-life posts to answer the actual audience demand.
4. Bridge all three into the funnel: Selfie Guide -> Starter Kit -> Brand Strategy Pack -> Masterclass/Studio.

## Where The First Workflow Went Wrong

The first canvas failed because it did three things:

1. **It overweighted historical post metrics.** The top selfie tutorial content matters, but it only tells us what creates reach and saves. It does not fully explain what the audience wants to buy or learn next.
2. **It underweighted fresh audience intent.** The story poll clearly shows Money / Income as the strongest current demand, and the direct question responses ask how Sandra started and made income online.
3. **It skipped competitor/creator pattern matching.** Successful creators in this niche are not only posting tutorials. They are posting identity-mirror hooks, founder story, money story, behind-the-scenes proof, objection handling, and "send this to someone" content.

The corrected workflow must require three gates before any content brief is approved:

- **Performance gate:** what past posts got saves, shares, comments, and reach.
- **Audience-demand gate:** what people are asking for now in polls, replies, comments, DMs, applications, and emails.
- **Market-pattern gate:** what similar successful creators are using today: identity mirror, specificity shock, pattern interrupt, curiosity gap, and DM-share content.

## Content Rules For The Agent

Every draft must include:

- one audience signal
- one feed asset
- one Story sequence
- one Sandra story or lived moment
- one practical takeaway
- one funnel objective
- one CTA
- one scroll-stop trigger: pattern interrupt, specificity shock, identity mirror, or curiosity gap
- one share/save reason
- one reason this should work
- one risk or caveat

## What To Stop Doing

- Do not produce 30 captions at once.
- Do not lead with abstract "personal branding" advice.
- Do not separate founder story from usefulness.
- Do not rely on ManyChat keywords as the only audience insight.
- Do not sell Masterclass with income claims before grounding the buyer in visibility, clarity, and practical selling assets.
- Do not let selfie tutorial metrics override current audience demand for income, time, confidence, and real-life business building.

## What To Double Down On

- Selfie tutorials with a specific result.
- Device/settings/lighting/angle content.
- Carousels that name the fear and give a concrete action.
- Founder story posts that include a practical lesson.
- Income story posts that tell the honest version without guarantees.
- Real-life motherhood/ADHD/time posts that show why the business had to be built differently.
- Share-trigger hooks written for "send this to your friend" behavior.
- DM-first CTAs for warm conversation.
- Save/share-first formats before harder sales CTAs.
