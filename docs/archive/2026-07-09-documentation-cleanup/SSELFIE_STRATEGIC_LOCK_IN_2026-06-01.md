# SSELFIE Strategic Lock-In

Date: 2026-06-01
Status: Source of truth for SSELFIE positioning, revenue ladder, and 90-day focus

## The Decision

SSELFIE is a visual identity transformation company.

The public sentence:

> Selfie to Brand Shoot.

The deeper economic engine:

> Sandra's taste system: visual direction, aesthetic certainty, and identity translation for women who want to become visible online.

The product is not the prompt. The prompt is the mechanism.

The product is the relief of finally knowing how to make yourself, your content, and your brand look elevated.

## What SSELFIE Is

SSELFIE helps women turn one ordinary selfie into elevated personal brand images using AI, Sandra's visual taste, and a clear transformation path.

The customer is not only trying to create images. She is trying to become visible, confident, recognizable, and visually aligned with the next version of herself.

## What SSELFIE Is Not

SSELFIE is not a prompt company.

SSELFIE is not a software-first company.

SSELFIE is not a productivity company.

SSELFIE is not a generic creator education company.

SSELFIE is not a planning/dashboard business.

SSELFIE should not keep launching disconnected products with different identities.

## Validated Signals

Last-year data shows:

- Studio generated the most revenue because it felt closest to a complete transformation system.
- Prompt Vault and AI prompt content generate the strongest current attention and demand.
- Free prompt users copy prompts heavily, which proves interest in the mechanism.
- Low-ticket prompts alone have not proven enough revenue depth to carry the company.
- Studio/Maya had purchase willingness, but activation and retention were weak.
- The audience consistently rewards visual transformation more than organization, planning, or generic education.

The market signal:

- Women want aesthetic certainty.
- They want content that finally looks elevated.
- They want personal brand visuals without a studio, photographer, or perfect setup.
- They trust Sandra's eye more than they trust another tool.

## Core Transformation

Before:

- I do not know how to look good online.
- I do not know what aesthetic fits me.
- I do not know what to post.
- My content does not look like the woman I want to become.
- I feel invisible or visually inconsistent.

After:

- I can turn one selfie into a brand shoot.
- I know which visual world fits me.
- I have elevated images I can use for my content, profile, offers, and next chapter.
- I feel visible again.
- I have Sandra's taste system guiding the look.

## Revenue Ladder

### 1. Free Front Door

Free AI photoshoot prompts.

Purpose:

- Capture demand from Instagram, TikTok, ManyChat, and email.
- Let women test the transformation with one selfie.
- Segment the AI photoshoot audience.

Public language:

- Free photoshoot prompts.
- Start your first brand shoot.
- Turn one selfie into elevated brand images.

### 2. Low-Ticket Qualifier

Prompt Vault, $27.

Purpose:

- Qualify buyers.
- Prove which aesthetics create purchase intent.
- Give immediate access to full photoshoot collections.

Positioning:

- Not "prompt collection."
- Yes: full AI photoshoot collections, newest and future drops, Sandra-tested visual worlds.

### 3. Core Automated Product

Working name:

Selfie to Brand Shoot System.

Target price:

$197-$297.

Purpose:

- Main scalable product.
- Package the complete transformation using existing assets.
- Create the passive-income engine Sandra originally wanted, without relying on a $27 product to carry the whole company.

Should include:

- Prompt Vault.
- Source selfie guidance.
- First-result walkthrough.
- Aesthetic direction.
- How to choose the right visual world.
- How to create a usable mini brand shoot.
- How to use the images for profile, content, offers, and visibility.
- Selected pieces from existing courses, guides, presets, and workbooks.

Should not become:

- A huge new portal.
- A complex app rebuild.
- Another disconnected course.
- A software-first experience.

### 4. Recurring Offer

Working name:

SSELFIE Visual Identity Club.

Target price:

$47-$97/month.

Purpose:

- Recurring revenue.
- New drops, seasonal shoots, visual prompts, content-use ideas, and an archive of Sandra's taste.

Build only after the core product is clear enough and buyer activation is working.

### 5. Premium Offer

VIP / 1:1 / private visual identity work.

Target price:

$1,500-$3,000+.

Purpose:

- Cash flow.
- Proof.
- Deep customer insight.
- Premium transformation for women who want Sandra's direct eye.

## Existing Products: New Role

### Prompt Vault

Keep. It is the entry transformation and buyer qualifier.

### Free Prompt Pack

Keep. It is the best current demand capture tool.

### Selfie Guide

Keep as support content. Reframe as source-photo improvement for better AI results.

### Starter Kit

Stop treating as the main next step. Repackage useful parts into the core system if they help the Selfie to Brand Shoot outcome.

### Masterclass / Branded by SSELFIE / Editing Masterclass

Audit and merge useful lessons into the core system. Do not promote as separate main identities unless Sandra explicitly chooses to revive them.

### Studio / Maya

Protect existing users. Do not sell cold prompt traffic into Studio as the primary next step.

Maya/Studio can later become backend infrastructure or a premium guided layer, but only if activation is fixed.

### Feed Planner

Keep for existing paid users. Do not make planning the front-door promise.

### Presets / Workbooks

Use as bonuses or support assets inside the core system if they help the visual transformation.

## 90-Day Focus

### Month 1

Consolidate.

- Lock the new positioning.
- Keep Vault selling as the front door.
- Audit existing assets into the Selfie to Brand Shoot System.
- Create the core product outline from existing assets.
- Stop promoting disconnected product names.

### Month 2

Package.

- Build the simplest possible core product.
- Use existing access systems where possible.
- Add clear onboarding and first-result activation.
- Add proof and objection handling.
- Sell through email, ManyChat, stories, and checkout upsells.

### Month 3

Scale.

- Add membership only if activation and buyer demand support it.
- Add premium/VIP path for customers who want Sandra's direct eye.
- Use AI to generate briefs, audits, content plans, and customer support without adding product complexity.

## How AI Helps From Today

AI should be used to move faster by consolidating and repurposing what already exists, not by inventing more products.

Use AI to:

- Audit all existing lessons, prompts, guides, workbooks, presets, and pages.
- Map every asset to the Selfie to Brand Shoot transformation.
- Rewrite product copy under the new positioning.
- Create lesson summaries and checklists.
- Generate onboarding emails and support responses.
- Analyze buyer objections.
- Identify winning aesthetics from prompt copy and content data.
- Maintain a daily Morning Board with what Sandra does and what Codex fixes.

Do not use AI to:

- Create ten new offers.
- Rebuild a giant app.
- Keep changing the business identity.
- Add more dashboards before the offer is clear.
