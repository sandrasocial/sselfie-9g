## Product Promise

Turn one selfie into a small, elevated personal brand shoot you can use for your profile, content, offers, and next chapter.

Short public promise:

> Selfie to Brand Shoot.

Emotional promise:

> Your content can finally look like the woman you are becoming.

## Target Buyer

She is a woman building or rebuilding her online identity.

She may be a creator, coach, founder, beauty/lifestyle entrepreneur, service provider, or woman entering a new visible era.

She does not want another complicated dashboard.

She wants:

- better personal brand images,
- a clear aesthetic direction,
- content that looks elevated,
- guidance on which selfie to use,
- prompts that actually produce usable images,
- confidence that the images still look like her,
- a simple way to use the images in content.

She is not primarily buying prompts.

She is buying relief from visual uncertainty.

## Product Structure

### Module 1: Start With One Selfie

Buyer outcome:

She knows which selfie/photo to upload so the AI result has a better chance of looking real, elevated, and like her.

Steps:

1. Choose the best source photo.
2. Check light, clarity, angle, expression, and face visibility.
3. Understand what AI can and cannot fix.
4. Take a better source selfie if needed.

Existing assets:

- Selfie Guide.
- Starter Kit posing guide.
- Confidence Camera Hack.
- High Quality Selfies lesson.
- Selfie Guide settings/walkthrough visuals.

Missing mini-assets:

- Source selfie checklist.
- Good / okay / poor source photo examples.
- One-page "before you upload" guide.

Visual requirements:

- Side-by-side source photo examples.
- Clean editorial phone/selfie visual.
- No abstract stock image.

### Module 3: Create The AI Brand Shoot

Buyer outcome:

She creates her first AI brand shoot in ChatGPT using one source selfie and one selected visual world.

Steps:

1. Open ChatGPT.
2. Upload the source selfie.
3. Copy the recommended opening shot prompt.
4. Generate the first result.
5. Continue the shoot sequence if the first result works.
6. Use troubleshooting if it looks fake or off.

Existing assets:

- Prompt Vault prompts and copy buttons.
- Free Prompt Pack preview prompts.
- Prompt Vault buyer sequence.
- AI prompt nurture sequence.
- Collection instructions.

Missing mini-assets:

- First-result walkthrough.
- "Try this prompt first" recommendation.
- ChatGPT setup note.
- Fake-looking result troubleshooting.

Visual requirements:

- One clear example flow: source selfie -> opening shot -> full shoot.
- Images should be real/current SSELFIE visual worlds.

### Module 4: Pick The Images That Look Like You

Buyer outcome:

She knows which AI images to keep, which to fix, and which to discard.

Steps:

1. Check likeness.
2. Check face, hands, expression, hair, outfit, and body realism.
3. Decide whether the image feels premium or fake.
4. Choose winners for profile, content, and offer use.
5. Save the best shots.

Existing assets:

- Sandra's visual taste notes.
- Editing Masterclass.
- Branded by SSELFIE aesthetic lessons.
- Prompt Vault examples.

Missing mini-assets:

- Keep / fix / discard rubric.
- Examples of believable vs too-AI.
- "What makes this look expensive?" checklist.

Visual requirements:

- Visual comparison cards.
- Minimal copy.
- No scary or overly technical language.

### Module 5: Turn Them Into Content

Buyer outcome:

She knows how to use the generated images in real content instead of leaving them in her camera roll.

Steps:

1. Choose one image for profile or bio.
2. Choose one image for a reel cover.
3. Choose one image for a story sequence.
4. Choose one image for offer/sales context.
5. Create a simple 7-day content plan from the shoot.

Existing assets:

- Masterclass content system lessons.
- Starter Kit caption/content PDFs.
- CEO Reels Launchpad.
- Selfie to CEO Instagram Planner.
- Feed Planner as optional legacy support.

Missing mini-assets:

- "Use this image as..." guide.
- 7-day content plan from one AI brand shoot.
- Simple caption prompt pack tied to the images.

Visual requirements:

- Mock Instagram use cases.
- Reel cover / story / profile examples.
- Keep this section practical, not dashboard-like.
