# Prompt Vault Proof And Objection Tracker

Date: 2026-06-01
Status: Active for the 7-day Prompt Vault value certainty test

## Purpose

For the next 7 days, do not only watch sales.

Collect the proof and objections that explain sales.

This tracker exists so SSELFIE does not keep guessing whether the issue is:

- product value
- product clarity
- buyer confidence
- ChatGPT usage friction
- wrong source selfie
- price resistance
- weak proof
- wrong next offer

## Daily Rule

Every day, collect:

1. At least one proof signal.
2. At least one objection signal.
3. One story sequence that shows the transformation instead of only selling the Vault.

## Objection Tags

Use one or more tags for every reply.

| Tag | Meaning | What It Tells Us |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `will_it_work_for_me` | She is unsure it will work with her face/photo | Need more proof, source-selfie guidance, before/after examples |
| `how_do_i_use_it` | She does not understand the workflow | Need clearer first-result path or tutorial |
| `is_it_only_prompts` | She thinks prompts alone are not valuable | Need stronger packaging, examples, and "full shoot" framing |
| `price_pause` | $27 feels too high or uncertain | Need stronger value proof, not discount first |
| `chatgpt_friction` | She is not confident using ChatGPT image generation | Need beginner walkthrough or mini video |
| `too_many_options` | She feels overwhelmed inside the Vault/free preview | Need stronger "start here" and fewer first choices |
| `wants_personal_help` | She asks Sandra to help choose/fix prompts | Signal for AI Brand Shoot Sprint or 1:1 |
| `wants_more_styles` | She wants more aesthetics/collections | Signal for future drops or Vault Club |
| `needs_better_selfie` | Her original photo is weak | Bridge to Selfie Guide/Starter Kit as support |
| `trust_or_proof` | She wants to see real users/results | Need social proof and buyer examples |

## Decision At End Of 7 Days

If proof is strong but sales are weak:

- packaging/bridge issue
- bundle into stronger Selfie to Brand Shoot Kit or sell a guided Sprint

If proof is weak and buyers do not use prompts:

- product/deliverable issue
- improve onboarding, source-selfie guidance, and result troubleshooting before more promotion

If sales improve and buyers send results:

- continue Vault push
- build the next rung from buyer behavior, not theory
