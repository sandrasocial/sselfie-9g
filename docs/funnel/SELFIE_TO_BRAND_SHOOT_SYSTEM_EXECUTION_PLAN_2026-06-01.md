## CEO Decision

SSELFIE is now organized around one transformation:

> Selfie to Brand Shoot.

The economic engine is Sandra's taste system: aesthetic certainty, visual direction, and identity translation for women who want to become visible online.

This is not a rebrand project.

This is not a new software build.

This is not another scattered product launch.

This is a consolidation sprint: package the strongest existing SSELFIE assets into one clear transformation, then sell that transformation through the existing audience, funnel, and product surfaces.

## What Is Proven Enough To Act On

### Internal data

- Studio generated the most recorded revenue because it felt closest to a complete transformation system.
- Studio/Maya did not fail because nobody wanted transformation. It failed because activation and retention were weak.
- AI prompts and Prompt Vault content generate the strongest current audience demand.
- Free prompt users copy prompts heavily, proving interest in the mechanism.
- Prompt Vault alone has not shown enough revenue depth to be the whole business.
- Planning/productivity surfaces do not create the same demand as visual transformation.

### Market proof

The market confirms the same direction:

- AI photoshoot products sell speed, output volume, and photoshoot economics, not "prompt education."
- Low-ticket AI photo/headshot products commonly sit around $29-$75.
- Creator subscriptions commonly sit around $26-$70/month when there is ongoing value.
- Premium AI photoshoot and visual identity offers sell from hundreds to thousands of dollars when they promise usable brand visuals and human taste.
- Creator businesses are moving toward owned product ladders: free audience capture, low-ticket entry, core product, recurring membership, and premium support.

Reference examples researched:

- Try it on AI: one-selfie AI headshots and creative studio.
- Aragon AI: $35-$75 AI headshot tiers.
- Fotium: $29-$59 AI photoshoot packs.
- GenMe Creators: $29.99-$69.99/month creator model plans.
- FUZE: $597 AI persona/personal brand masterclass.
- Hey Mary Studio: $97 course, $447 AI Photoshoot Lite, $2,495 full AI brand shoot.
- Selfmade: $10-$39/month women-founder membership.
- CREATE+: $297/month creator business membership.

## Revenue Ladder

### 1. Free Front Door

Offer:

Free AI Photoshoot Prompts.

Promise:

Start your first brand shoot from one selfie.

Role:

- Capture Instagram/TikTok/ManyChat/email demand.
- Let women test the transformation.
- Segment the AI photoshoot audience.

Must include:

- Simple opt-in.
- Real SSELFIE photoshoot examples.
- Free opening shots from current visual worlds.
- Clear ChatGPT steps.
- Strong bridge to Prompt Vault.

Do not build:

- A big free course.
- Another dashboard.
- Multiple competing lead magnets for the same audience.

### 2. Low-Ticket Qualifier

Offer:

Prompt Vault, $27.

Promise:

Full Sandra-tested AI photoshoot collections you can copy into ChatGPT today.

Role:

- Qualify buyers.
- Prove which visual worlds create purchase intent.
- Give immediate transformation.

Must include:

- Named photoshoot collections.
- Example visuals.
- Copy buttons.
- ChatGPT usage steps.
- "Which selfie to use" guidance.
- Sandra's note on why each look works.
- Tracking for access opens, prompt views, prompt copies, and collection demand.

Do not build:

- Prompt marketplace.
- Generic "500 prompts" positioning.
- Complex app experience.
- Community features.

### 3. Core Automated Product

Working name:

Selfie to Brand Shoot System.

Target price:

$197-$297.

Promise:

Turn one selfie into a usable mini brand shoot for your profile, content, offers, and next chapter.

Role:

This is the main scalable product. It is the product Sandra originally wanted a passive income business to have, but built from proven assets instead of random new features.

Required modules:

1. Start With One Selfie
   - Choose or take the right source photo.
   - Understand light, angle, expression, and clarity.
   - Pull from Selfie Guide and Starter Kit.

2. Choose Your Visual World
   - Pick an aesthetic that matches the woman/brand she is becoming.
   - Use Sandra's taste notes and Vault collection examples.

3. Create The AI Brand Shoot
   - Use Vault prompts.
   - Generate opening shots and full shoot sequences.
   - Troubleshoot fake-looking results.

4. Pick The Images That Look Like You
   - Decide what is usable.
   - Avoid over-AI results.
   - Choose profile, reel cover, launch, story, and post images.

5. Turn It Into Content
   - Use the images in profile, posts, offers, stories, and simple content plans.
   - Pull selected pieces from Masterclass, Editing Masterclass, Starter Kit, and Feed Planner only where they support the brand-shoot outcome.

Required buyer experience:

- Prompt Vault included.
- First-result checklist.
- First 24-hour activation email.
- Simple product home.
- Clear "start here" path.
- Proof examples by visual world.
- Objection handling for:
  - Will this work for me?
  - What selfie should I use?
  - Will it look fake?
  - Do I need ChatGPT paid?
  - What do I post with the images?

Do not build:

- A huge new portal.
- A new course from scratch.
- A software-first app.
- A full content planner.
- A community.
- A giant curriculum about all of personal branding.

### 4. Recurring Offer

Working name:

SSELFIE Visual Identity Club.

Target price:

Founding $27/month, later $47-$97/month.

Promise:

New Sandra-tested visual worlds every month so your brand never gets stuck in one version of you.

Role:

Recurring revenue, but only after activation is clearer.

MVP later:

- Monthly or biweekly drops.
- One named aesthetic per drop.
- Five to nine prompts per drop.
- Sandra-tested example.
- Best selfie to use.
- What not to ask ChatGPT.
- Best use case: profile, reel cover, launch photo, story, sales page.
- Archive page.
- Day 0 and Day 3 activation emails.

Do not build yet:

- Discord/community platform.
- Leaderboards.
- In-app submissions.
- Affiliate program.
- Annual plan.

### 5. Premium Offer

Working name:

VIP Visual Identity Sprint.

Target price:

$1,500-$3,000+.

Promise:

Sandra helps shape your personal brand visuals, AI photoshoot direction, and content identity for your next era.

Role:

Cash flow, proof, and customer insight.

Can be opened quietly before membership.

Required:

- Inquiry page or application.
- Async intake.
- Source selfie review.
- Visual direction/moodboard.
- Custom prompt set.
- Review of generated outputs.
- Final image-use plan.
- Testimonial/proof capture.

Do not build:

- A full agency.
- Ongoing done-for-you content management.
- Anything that makes Sandra the bottleneck for the entire business.

## AI Implementation Plan

Use AI to consolidate, not invent.

### Step 1: Asset Tagging

Scan all existing lessons, prompts, PDFs, workbooks, emails, and product pages.

Tag every asset as one of:

- source_selfie
- aesthetic_direction
- prompting
- first_result
- image_selection
- editing
- content_usage
- proof
- bonus
- legacy_only
- discard

### Step 2: Product Outline

Build the Selfie to Brand Shoot System outline from existing assets only.

No new modules unless a required transformation step has no existing material.

### Step 3: Public Funnel Alignment

Update core public messaging in this order:

1. Free Prompt Pack.
2. Prompt Vault.
3. Checkout page.
4. Prompt Vault delivery and buyer sequence.
5. ManyChat bridge copy.
6. Daily Briefing/Morning Board recommendations.

### Step 4: Core Product Shell

Use existing Academy/access infrastructure where possible.

Create the leanest possible buyer home:

- Start here.
- Choose selfie.
- Choose visual world.
- Create first brand shoot.
- Use your images.
- Bonuses.

### Step 5: Activation Layer

Before adding more content, add:

- Day 0: start with this one selfie.
- Day 1: create your first result.
- Day 3: fix fake-looking results.
- Day 5: turn your images into posts.

### Step 6: Proof System

Track:

- source selfie used.
- visual world chosen.
- first prompt copied.
- first result created.
- buyer replied/submitted result.
- objection category.
- next product interest.
