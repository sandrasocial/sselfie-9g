## Executive Decision

The active SSELFIE front-door audience is not asking for an AI app.

They are asking for Sandra's AI photoshoot prompts for ChatGPT.

Studio, Maya, and Feed Planner should no longer be treated as the main public value proposition for new cold traffic. ChatGPT can now do the image creation job directly. The new value is:

1. Sandra's taste.
2. Tested prompt systems.
3. Repeatable photoshoot collections.
4. Source-photo guidance.
5. Content usage after the image is created.
6. Human feedback and updates.

## Competitor Takeaways

What appears to work:

1. **Specific outcome over generic quantity.**
   "AI photoshoot prompts for your personal brand" is stronger than "500 prompts."

2. **Free taste, paid vault.**
   Buyers want to test before paying because prompt packs have a trust problem.

3. **Examples sell the prompt.**
   Every prompt should show a result image or expected output style.

4. **Updates create subscription logic.**
   A static prompt PDF is one-time. Monthly new collections, trend prompts, and seasonal drops create recurring revenue.

5. **Prompt buyers are skeptical.**
   The market is full of cheap, generic AI prompt packs. SSELFIE's moat is that Sandra shows real outputs, real taste, and a repeatable visual point of view.

6. **The upsell is not "more software."**
   The upsell is "help me make these work for my face, my brand, my content, and my next launch."

## New Buyer Persona

### The ChatGPT Photoshoot Buyer

She is already using ChatGPT or is willing to.

She saw Sandra's AI photo results and wants the prompt, not a platform.

She wants:

- beautiful brand photos without booking a shoot
- copy-paste simplicity
- prompts that keep her looking like herself
- examples she can copy from
- full shoot collections, not one random image
- content she can post immediately

She does not want:

- a complicated app
- another dashboard
- model training
- credits
- a vague education ladder
- "learn the whole selfie system first"

### 2. Prompt Vault Pro / Seasonal Vault — $47-$67

Status: hypothesis. Do not build until Prompt Vault sales and usage data show which collections buyers want more of.

Role: immediate order bump or next offer.

Promise: "More collections, launch-ready looks, and prompt formulas."

Possible packaging:

- 100+ extra prompts
- launch photoshoot prompts
- profile/header prompts
- carousel cover prompts
- product/offer photo prompts
- seasonal content drops
- "make your own prompt" framework

Why this fits:

The buyer has already said yes to prompts. The next thing she wants is more styles and more use cases, not a different product category.

### 3. Prompt-To-Post System — $97-$147

Status: hypothesis. Validate through buyer replies, prompt copy data, and support questions before building.

Role: replace the old Masterclass/Starter Kit bridge with a ChatGPT-native content system.

Promise: "Turn AI photos into actual content for your brand."

Includes:

- choose the right source selfie
- generate 10-20 usable AI brand photos in ChatGPT
- pick winners
- edit lightly
- write captions
- build a 7-day or 30-day posting plan
- turn one photoshoot into content themes

This can reuse pieces of Selfie Guide, Starter Kit, Masterclass, and Feed Planner, but it must be renamed and framed around the ChatGPT prompt buyer.

### 4. Prompt Vault Club — $19-$29/month

Status: hypothesis. Only build if buyers ask for ongoing drops or repeat collections.

Role: recurring revenue replacement for cold Studio subscriptions.

Promise: "New AI photoshoot collections every month, plus trend prompts and usage notes."

Includes:

- monthly prompt collection drops
- trend-based AI photo prompts
- seasonal/launch prompt packs
- buyer-only archive
- prompt fixes and updates when ChatGPT changes
- optional monthly live prompt lab

Why this replaces Studio:

It gives recurring value without asking buyers to leave ChatGPT or pay for an app they do not need.

### 5. AI Brand Photoshoot Sprint — $297-$497

Status: hypothesis. Validate with buyer replies and DMs before packaging.

Role: premium implementation offer.

Promise: "We build your AI photoshoot direction with you."

Includes:

- source-photo selection
- personal prompt direction
- brand/photo moodboard
- 20 prompt set customized to her brand
- review of generated outputs
- final content plan

Delivery can be async, workshop, or small cohort.

### 6. 1:1 With Sandra — $2,000+

Role: high-ticket back end.

Promise: "Sandra helps build the visual identity and content direction for your next era."

This should not be sold to cold prompt leads immediately. It is for buyers who have already purchased, used the prompts, and want Sandra's eye.

## What Happens To Studio / Maya / Feed Planner

### Stop Selling Publicly As The Main Offer

For this audience, Studio/Maya/Feed Planner are no longer the obvious next step. ChatGPT has absorbed the core image-generation job.

Do not use public prompt traffic to sell:

- custom model training
- AI generation credits
- Feed Planner
- "Maya does it inside our app"

Those are no longer the cleanest value proposition.

### Preserve For Existing Users

Keep Studio, Maya, and Feed Planner working for existing paying users.

Do not break paid-user access.

### Reposition Later

If Studio is kept, it needs a new job:

- prompt library and buyer hub
- saved prompt collections
- content planning from generated images
- Sandra's review layer
- asset organization

Studio should not compete with ChatGPT image generation unless it adds something ChatGPT cannot.
