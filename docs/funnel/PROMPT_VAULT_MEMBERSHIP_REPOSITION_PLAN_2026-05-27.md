## Executive Decision

Do not create ten new products.

The next recurring product should grow out of Prompt Vault behavior. The strongest direction is a recurring creative ecosystem for women who want to become different aesthetic versions of themselves using one selfie and ChatGPT.

Working positioning:

> SSELFIE Vault Club: new AI photoshoot transformations every week, seasonal collections, creative challenges, and Sandra's eye on what is working now.

This should replace the cold-traffic role that Studio used to play. Studio/Maya can still serve existing users, but for prompt traffic the recurring offer must not be sold as an AI app, model training, credits, or feed planner.

## Market Pattern Notes

The generic prompt-pack market is crowded and price-sensitive. The stronger recurring patterns are:

- Fresh drops: new prompts weekly or monthly.
- Trend discovery: what is working now.
- Community proof: prompt galleries, likes, votes, challenges, shared results.
- Creator identity: the buyer is not purchasing text; she is purchasing taste, direction, and an easier way to create.
- Distribution loops: affiliate, referral, or contributor programs work best after members have a visible result they want to share.

Relevant external examples reviewed:

- PromptMaster community emphasizes Discord, weekly challenges, contributors, and community voting.
- Creator's Copy Vault sells monthly prompt packs at a low recurring price.
- Banana Prompts uses an open gallery, creator contribution, likes, and trend discovery.
- 7Prompts uses daily prompt freshness and weekly winners.
- PromptDen and My Prompt Gallery show discovery, voting, sharing, and community prompt mechanics.
- Pen Pivot Pro sells a growing vault with weekly prompt additions delivered to inbox.
- Prompt Vault World positions around luxury lifestyle visual outcomes, not only prompt quantity.

## Core Reposition

### Old Studio Promise

"Join Studio to use Maya, train models, generate images, plan feed content, and access the SSELFIE ecosystem."

Problem:

For the Prompt Vault audience, ChatGPT now handles the generation job. Asking them to leave ChatGPT for an app is friction.

### New Membership Promise

"Every week, get new Sandra-tested AI photoshoot transformations for the version of you that wants to show up next."

This sells:

- Fresh aesthetic drops.
- Sandra's taste.
- A living archive.
- Weekly creative direction.
- Community momentum.
- Identity experimentation.
- Faster content creation from one selfie.

It does not sell:

- A dashboard.
- Credits.
- Prompt engineering education.
- Model training.
- Feed Planner as the main reason to join.

## Membership Contents

### 1. New Drops Weekly

Cadence:

- 1 flagship aesthetic drop per week.
- 5 to 9 prompts per drop.
- 1 free preview prompt becomes the next reel/freebie.
- Full collection stays member-only.

Examples:

- Dark Balcony City Girl.
- Winter Rich Girl Mirror.
- Soft CEO Hotel Morning.
- Paris Apartment Window.
- Red Lip Night Editorial.
- Coastal Rich Aunt.
- Launch Day Founder Portraits.

Each drop must include:

- Cover result image.
- 5 to 9 copy-paste transformations.
- Source selfie guidance.
- What to post it as.
- Best use case: reel cover, profile photo, sales page, carousel cover, story, launch teaser.

### 2. Seasonal Collections

Cadence:

- 1 larger seasonal collection per month.
- 15 to 30 transformations.
- Built around content moments members actually need.

Seasonal examples:

- Summer Main Character.
- Fall Founder Era.
- Holiday Glam Content.
- New Year Rebrand.
- Valentine's Soft Luxury.
- Spring Clean Girl Brand Refresh.
- Black Friday Sales Editorial.

Seasonal drops are important because they create urgency and a reason to stay subscribed.

### 3. Creator Challenge System

Goal:

Turn passive buyers into people who use the prompts, share results, and emotionally attach to their new identity.

Weekly challenge format:

1. Sandra posts the weekly aesthetic.
2. Members create 1 image from one selfie.
3. Members submit or tag their result.
4. Sandra features 3 to 5 transformations.
5. Winner gets a custom prompt mini-set or one month free.

Challenge names:

- This Week's Transformation.
- One Selfie Challenge.
- Main Character Drop.
- Become Her Challenge.

What to measure:

- challenge starts
- challenge submissions
- shared results
- replies
- retained members who submit
- purchases after seeing member examples

### 4. Member Archive

The archive is the retention asset.

Structure:

- Newest drops first.
- Filter by aesthetic: dark feminine, luxury, glam, mirror, soft, coastal, founder, launch, seasonal.
- Filter by use case: reel cover, profile photo, carousel cover, sales page, story, launch.
- Prompt cards with examples and copy buttons.
- View/copy tracking on every prompt.

Do not overbuild. The first version can be a simple protected page or Academy access area.

### 5. Sandra's Notes

Every drop should include small notes from Sandra:

- Why this look works.
- Which selfie to use.
- What not to ask ChatGPT.
- How to make it look less fake.
- What caption angle fits the image.

This is the moat. The buyer can ask ChatGPT for prompts, but she cannot get Sandra's current taste, tested examples, and content instinct.

## Affiliate And Community Loop

### When To Add

Do not launch affiliate on day one.

Launch referral/affiliate after:

- 25+ Prompt Vault purchases.
- 10+ members in the recurring club.
- 5+ public member transformations Sandra would be proud to show.

### Customer Referral First

Start with a customer referral loop before a broad affiliate program.

Offer:

- Give a friend 20 percent off their first month.
- Get one free month when they join.

Why:

Existing buyers are more trusted than random affiliates. They can show their own result.

### Creator Affiliate Later

After proof:

- 30 percent recurring commission for 6 months.
- Affiliate must show examples or create content around a SSELFIE-style transformation.
- No generic coupon spam.
- Best affiliates are beauty creators, personal brand creators, Instagram coaches, digital product sellers, and women teaching ChatGPT/AI for creators.

### Community Loop

The loop should be:

Member creates transformation -> member shares result -> audience asks for prompt -> member shares referral link -> new buyer joins Vault or Club -> new buyer creates transformation.

This is stronger than:

Affiliate posts a discount code -> cold audience clicks -> maybe buys.

## Segment Strategy

The AI prompt audience should be treated as its own segment.

Segment name:

- AI Photoshoot Audience

Include:

- `/ai-prompts` opt-ins.
- Prompt Vault buyers.
- Prompt Vault access openers.
- Prompt Vault prompt copiers.
- Checkout abandoners.
- ManyChat users from prompt reels.

Exclude from first membership push:

- legacy Selfie Guide only leads who never clicked AI prompts.
- Studio members unless messaging is framed as included/new member benefit.
- Starter Kit buyers unless they also entered AI Prompts.

Segment intent levels:

| Segment | Definition | Message |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Curious | opted into free prompts | "Try this week's free transformation." |
| Activated | copied a free prompt | "Unlock the full shoot." |
| Buyer | bought Prompt Vault | "Get new drops weekly." |
| Power user | copied 3+ vault prompts | "You are the best fit for Vault Club." |
| Abandoned | started checkout but did not buy | "Your transformation is waiting." |

## Validation Gates Before Building

Do not build the membership until at least two of these are true:

1. Prompt Vault reaches 25+ purchases.
2. 40 percent or more of buyers open access.
3. 50 percent or more of access openers copy at least one prompt.
4. At least 10 buyers copy 3+ prompts.
5. Dark Balcony or another aesthetic gets repeated comments/DMs asking to buy.
6. At least 5 buyers reply or DM asking for more prompts/drops.
7. Checkout recovery produces at least 1 recovered purchase.

If these are not true, keep improving the free-to-paid bridge and the Vault sales page.

## Existing Studio Reposition

Current Studio can become the backend infrastructure, but the front-door promise should change.

### Keep For Existing Members

Existing Studio members should not lose access.

### New Public Framing

Possible future framing:

> SSELFIE Studio is becoming the home for your AI photoshoot vault, weekly aesthetic drops, and content direction.

This makes Studio less about Maya and more about an ongoing creative system.

### Migration Path

1. Keep current Studio active for current members.
2. Build Vault Club as a new subscription product or membership tier.
3. Give existing Studio members included access to Vault Club during transition.
4. Measure whether Studio members use drops more than old Maya/Feed Planner features.
5. If usage confirms it, rename/reframe Studio around Vault Club.

## Biggest Strategic Risk

The biggest risk is building a membership before proving the buyer wants ongoing drops.

The correct sequence:

1. Sell Prompt Vault.
2. Watch which aesthetics people copy.
3. Add one new drop.
4. Measure whether buyers return.
5. Ask if they want weekly drops.
6. Then build the subscription.
