# SSELFIE 2026 Masterclass Income Module

Last updated: 2026-04-25

This module plan fills the current Masterclass gap: the audience is asking how to build and make income online, but the product should not promise guaranteed income.

## Compliance Position

Do not claim that this module guarantees income, sales, clients, or a specific financial outcome.

Approved promise:

> Build the offer, visibility system, content plan, and sales assets you need to start selling online with more clarity.

Avoid:

- guaranteed income
- “make X in Y days”
- “this will make you money”
- “copy this and get clients”
- exceptional testimonials without context

Suggested disclaimer:

> This training is educational and implementation-focused. Results depend on your offer, audience, consistency, pricing, market demand, effort, and timing. SSELFIE does not guarantee income or specific business results.

## Module Outcome

By the end, the customer should have:

- a clear personal brand offer
- a defined audience/problem
- a simple sales message
- 30 days of content direction
- DM and follow-up scripts
- a simple landing or bio-link pitch
- a weekly tracking system
- a workflow for using SSELFIE/Maya to execute faster

## Curriculum

### Lesson 1: What Are You Actually Selling?

Goal: turn vague “personal brand” desire into one simple offer.

Deliverables:

- Personal Brand Offer Map
- problem/result statement
- first-price recommendation worksheet
- “who this is for / not for” section

### Lesson 2: Why Would Anyone Trust You?

Goal: connect selfies, story, credibility, and proof.

Deliverables:

- credibility inventory
- story-to-content prompts
- proof post framework
- “why me” caption starter

### Lesson 3: Content That Leads Somewhere

Goal: create a content system that builds trust and moves people toward a conversation.

Deliverables:

- 4 content pillars
- 10 hooks based on the buyer’s story
- weekly content rhythm
- call-to-action bank

### Lesson 4: Your First Simple Funnel

Goal: create a simple path from content to offer.

Deliverables:

- bio link copy
- one-page offer copy
- DM conversation starter
- follow-up script
- objection response prompts

### Lesson 5: The 30-Day Revenue Readiness Sprint

Goal: help the customer implement consistently and measure traction.

Deliverables:

- 30-day income-ready content calendar
- daily/weekly actions
- conversation tracker
- offer-made tracker
- weekly review prompt

### Lesson 6: Use SSELFIE/Maya To Execute

Goal: connect education to the product.

Deliverables:

- photo generation workflow
- caption workflow
- content repurposing workflow
- weekly Studio routine

## Brand Strategy Pack Integration

Masterclass buyers should receive Brand Strategy Pack access first.

Recommended order:

1. Complete Brand Strategy Pack.
2. Open Masterclass income module.
3. Use strategy output to fill the Offer Map.
4. Generate 30-day content plan.
5. Implement weekly inside Studio/Maya.

## Required Product Changes Before Launch

- Add Brand Strategy Pack access to Masterclass entitlement behavior.
- Update Masterclass landing copy to describe the new outcome.
- Update checkout success to direct buyer to the right first step.
- Update Masterclass day-0 delivery email.
- Add income disclaimer to sales page and buyer home.
- Add tests for entitlement and email link behavior.

## Buyer Home Requirements

The Academy/Masterclass buyer home should show:

1. Start here: complete Brand Strategy Pack.
2. Then: build your offer map.
3. Then: create your 30-day content plan.
4. Then: execute inside Studio/Maya.

Do not drop buyers into a library with no order.

## Measurement

Track:

- Brand Strategy started by Masterclass buyer
- Brand Strategy completed by Masterclass buyer
- income module started
- offer map completed
- 30-day plan generated/downloaded
- Studio CTA clicked
- Studio subscription after Masterclass

## Launch Scope

First version can be lean:

- 6 short lessons
- one implementation workbook
- one 30-day content calendar
- one script bank
- one tracker

Do not wait for a large video library. The first useful version should help the customer do the work immediately.
