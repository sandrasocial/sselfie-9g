## FAQ
**Who is this for?**
For women building online who know they need to be more visible, but feel stuck on where to start, what to post, what their profile should say, or how to use AI without losing themselves.

**Will I know what to do first?**
Yes. The app starts by asking where you are, what you're building, and what feels most stuck. Then it gives you one clear first move. Not a long list.

**How much time do I need?**
Pick 2, 10, or 30 minutes. You don't have to do everything every day. You just keep coming back to one useful move.

**What if I don't feel ready to show my face?**
You don't have to feel ready first. SSELFIE starts small. One selfie. One profile fix. One story. One post. The confidence usually comes after you see yourself in a way that feels true.

**Is this an AI photo app?**
No. AI visuals are part of the method, but they're not the whole thing. SSELFIE helps you build the brand around your real face, story, message, content, and offer.

**Will the AI visuals look fake?**
The goal isn't to make you look like another woman. It's still-you visuals. The app helps you pick better source selfies, avoid fake-looking results, and use AI as direction around the real you. AI should not erase you. It should frame you.

**What if I miss days?**
Then you come back. That counts. The app is built for real life, not perfect streaks.
