## FAQ
**Who is this for?**
For women building something online who know they need to be more visible, but feel stuck on where to start, what to post, what their profile should say, or how to show up without it taking over their week.

**Will I know what to do first?**
Yes. You start with one selfie. The app asks where you are, what you're building, and what feels most stuck, then gives you one clear first move. Not a long list.

**How much time do I need?**
Pick 2, 10, or 30 minutes. You don't have to do everything every day. You just keep coming back to one useful move. That's what builds a brand.

**What if I don't feel ready to show up?**
You don't have to feel ready first. SSELFIE starts small. One selfie. One profile fix. One post. The confidence comes after you see yourself show up in a way that feels true, not before.

**Is this an AI photo app?**
No. It starts with one selfie, but it becomes your whole content and brand system: your message, your profile, your posts, your captions, and the offer it all leads to. The photos are one part of it, so you always have something that feels like you to post. The point is being seen and trusted, not just having nice pictures.

**Will I look fake?**
The goal isn't to turn you into someone else. It's to help you look like you, on a good day. The app helps you pick a better selfie to start from and use AI as direction, not a mask. AI should not erase you. It should frame you.

**What if I miss days?**
Then you come back. That counts. SSELFIE is built for real life, not perfect streaks.
