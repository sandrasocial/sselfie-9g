# Design Redesign: Risk Assessment & Priority Pages

## Risk Level Assessment

### ⚠️ HIGH RISK - Don't Do This Now:
**Full Redesign (Grids, New Photography, Complete Restructure)**

**Why it's risky:**
- Takes 1-2 weeks minimum
- Delays Brand Engine launch
- Could break responsive layouts
- Requires professional photoshoot
- High chance of bugs/issues
- Distracts from sales and marketing

**When to do it:**
- After you close your first 2-3 Brand Engine clients
- When you have time for professional photography
- After you've validated the offer works

---

### 🟡 MEDIUM RISK - Carefully Consider:
**Layout Structure Changes (Centered → Asymmetric, Adding Grids)**

**Why it's medium risk:**
- Changes HTML structure significantly
- Could break mobile responsive design
- Needs thorough testing
- Takes 1-2 days of work
- Might introduce visual bugs

**Impact:**
- Moderate visual improvement
- Still requires good photography to look editorial
- Won't fully achieve Pinterest aesthetic without other changes

**Timeline if you do it:**
- 2-3 days of work
- Must test on mobile/tablet/desktop
- Delays launch by a few days

---

### ✅ LOW RISK - Safe to Do Now:
**Typography & Overlay Quick Wins**

**Changes:**
1. Remove heavy dark overlays (or reduce to 0.3 instead of 0.5)
2. Increase headline font sizes (64-80px)
3. Add editorial numbers (01, 02, 03)
4. Adjust letter spacing and line heights
5. Use lighter font weights

**Why it's low risk:**
- Mostly CSS changes
- Doesn't change structure
- Easy to revert if something breaks
- Can be done in 1-2 hours
- Won't break responsive layouts

**Impact:**
- Noticeable improvement
- Feels more editorial
- Doesn't achieve full Pinterest aesthetic, but moves in that direction

---
