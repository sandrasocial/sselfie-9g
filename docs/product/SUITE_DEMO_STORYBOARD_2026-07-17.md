## Recording direction

- Use the real product for every interaction and result.
- Use the editorial Calendar mockup only as the opening or closing frame. Label it “illustrated walkthrough” if it appears outside the landing page.
- Keep the cursor slow. One action at a time.
- Zoom into the exact area Sandra is talking about. Do not show the whole interface when the important action is small.
- Let one real result stay on screen long enough to inspect.
- Do not hide corrections, waiting states, or the review step. They make the demo more believable.
- Do not show fake followers, fake customer accounts, fake messages, or made-up results.
- Do not promise income, perfect likeness, automatic posting, or a fixed number of finished photos.
- Keep captions on screen. Many women will watch without sound.
