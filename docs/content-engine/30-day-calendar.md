## METRICS TO TRACK (Weekly)

### Content Performance
- Reach (unique accounts who saw content)
- Engagement Rate: (Likes + Comments + Saves + Shares) / Reach
- Save Rate: Saves / Reach (target >5%)
- Profile Visits from posts
- Website clicks (link in bio)

### Business Impact
- DM inquiries about Brand Engine
- "Comment BRAND" responses
- Link clicks to sales page
- Brand Engine enrollments
- Revenue attributed to Instagram

### Audience Growth
- Follower count (+/- per week)
- Follower quality (engaged vs. ghost)
- DM conversations started
- Story replies and engagement

### Weekly Review Questions
1. Which post got highest saves? (Most valuable content)
2. Which post got most shares? (Most relatable content)
3. Which post drove most profile visits? (Best hook)
4. What comments/DMs revealed about audience needs?
5. Which content took too long to create? (Optimize next week)
