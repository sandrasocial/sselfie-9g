## ⚡ QUICK REFERENCE

### Posting Schedule (Mon-Fri)
- **Monday:** Carousel (Educational)
- **Tuesday:** Personal Story (Vulnerability)
- **Wednesday:** Reel (Tutorial or Storytelling)
- **Thursday:** Before/After or Carousel
- **Friday:** BTS or Reel

### Best Posting Times (Test in Week 2)
- 8 AM (morning coffee scroll)
- 12 PM (lunch break)
- 7 PM (evening wind-down)

### Content Pillar Mix
- 40% Educational (tutorials, tips, how-tos)
- 30% Personal Story (vulnerability, journey)
- 20% Social Proof (results, testimonials)
- 10% Engagement (questions, BTS, community)

### Hashtag Strategy
- Use 25-30 hashtags per post
- Mix: 5 big (1M+) + 12 medium (100K-1M) + 10 niche (10K-100K) + 2 branded
- Rotate 3 sets weekly (avoid spam detection)
- Full hashtag sets in `30-day-calendar.md`

## 🎨 DESIGN REQUIREMENTS

**All visuals must follow Scandinavian luxury aesthetic:**

### Colors (ONLY these 5)
- Obsidian: #0a0a0a (deep black)
- Porcelain: #ffffff (pure white)
- Pearl: #f5f5f5 (soft off-white)
- Smoke: #666666 (medium grey)
- Whisper: #e5e5e5 (light grey)

### Fonts (ONLY these 2)
- **Headers:** Cormorant Garamond (200-300 weight, UPPERCASE)
- **Body:** Inter (300-500 weight)

### Layout Rules
- 48px minimum padding all sides
- Asymmetric balance (not centered)
- Generous white space
- Mobile-first (test at 375px width)

**Complete design specs in** `10-carousel-concepts.md`

## 💬 BRAND VOICE REMINDERS

### Always
- Warm, conversational (like texting a friend)
- Short sentences. Breathable.
- Contractions (you're, don't, it's)
- "Let me be really honest..." "Here's the thing..." "Wild, right?"

### Never
- Marketing jargon (leverage, synergy, transform)
- All caps (except in graphics)
- Emojis overload (2-3 max per caption)
- Corporate speak
- Buzzwords (game-changer, next level, crushing it)

**Complete voice guide in** `caption-templates.md`
