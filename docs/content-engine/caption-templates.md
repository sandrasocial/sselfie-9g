# CAPTION TEMPLATES FOR INSTAGRAM
## Reach, Nurture, Convert Goals

**Voice Guidelines:** Every caption must sound like Sandra (warm, conversational, authentic)
- Short sentences. Breathable. Like texting a friend.
- Contractions always (you're, don't, it's)
- "Let me be really honest..." "Here's the thing..." "Wild, right?"
- No marketing jargon (leverage, synergy, transform, game-changer)
- End with engagement question

## REACH TEMPLATES (Algorithm Boost + New Audience)

**Goal:** Stop the scroll, drive saves/shares, attract new followers
**Best for:** Educational content, tutorials, myth-busting, controversial takes

### REACH TEMPLATE 1: Pattern Interrupt

```
[UNEXPECTED STATEMENT THAT BREAKS EXPECTATIONS]

I know what you're thinking. [Address their assumption]

But here's the thing. [Your reality/truth]

[2-3 sentences explaining why this matters]

[Personal example or proof]

[Actionable takeaway they can use today]

Swipe → / Watch / Save this if [specific benefit]

[Engagement question]
```

```
Your brand photos don't need to cost thousands.

I know what you're thinking. "But Sandra, professional photography is an investment."

Sure. So is showing up consistently for 6 months while saving for that shoot.

I spent €1,200 on professional photos last year. Used maybe 3 of them. Needed more content two weeks later. Couldn't afford another shoot.

This year? 400+ professional photos. €47/month. AI tool. My iPhone. Done.

Your phone + AI = brand photos in 20 minutes. No savings account required.

Save this if you're tired of the "I don't have content" excuse.

What's been holding you back from consistent content? 💭
```

## CAPTION LENGTH GUIDELINES

### Short Captions (50-100 words)
**Best for:**
- Reels (let video do the talking)
- Strong visual content (before/after)
- Simple announcements
- Quick tips

### Medium Captions (150-250 words)
**Best for:**
- Educational carousels
- Tutorial content
- Myth-busting posts
- List-based content

### Long Captions (250-400 words)
**Best for:**
- Vulnerability stories
- Transformation narratives
- Sales/launch content
- Personal journey posts

**Instagram allows 2,200 characters** — use them when the content calls for it, but respect your audience's attention.

## CALL-TO-ACTION (CTA) FORMULAS

**Every caption needs ONE clear CTA. Don't ask for multiple actions.**

## CAPTION WRITING WORKFLOW

### Step 1: Know Your Goal (Choose One)
- REACH: Stop scroll, attract new audience
- NURTURE: Deepen trust, build connection
- CONVERT: Drive action, make sales

### Step 2: Choose Template
- Pick template matching your goal
- Fill in brackets with your content
- Keep Sandra's voice (warm, real, conversational)

### Step 3: Write Hook
- First line must stop the scroll
- Use one of 15 proven hook formulas
- Make it specific, not generic

### Step 4: Deliver Value
- Educational: Teach something actionable
- Story: Share vulnerable truth
- Social proof: Show real results

### Step 5: Add CTA
- One clear action only
- Match CTA to goal (engagement/growth/conversion)
- Make it easy to do

### Step 6: Edit for Voice
- Read out loud — does it sound like Sandra?
- Remove jargon, corporate speak, buzzwords
- Add contractions (you're, don't, it's)
- Break into short, breathable sentences
- End with engagement question

### Step 7: Format for Mobile
- Add line breaks every 2-3 sentences
- Use emojis sparingly (2-3 max)
- Check "...more" cutoff (first 125 characters must hook)
- Preview on phone before posting
