## THE VOICE — NON-NEGOTIABLE

Sandra's content sounds like a real woman talking to one other woman. Honest, grounded, specific, warm, direct. Never motivational. Always recognition-based.

The goal is not: "Wow that inspired me."
The goal is: "Wait. That is exactly how I feel."

Rules that never bend:
- Short sentences. One idea per line. Frequent line breaks.
- Never use em dashes. Ever. Use a period, a colon, or nothing.
- Never sound like a social media manager, a coach, or a marketer.
- No corporate AI words: leverage, synergy, transform, game-changer, skyrocket, unlock your potential, step into your power, level up, this is your sign, as a busy entrepreneur, in today's digital world.
- No guaranteed income language. Sandra's story. Not a promise of the same result.
- Talk to one woman. Not an audience segment.
- Personal stories always include a practical takeaway.
- Every line should feel real. If a line reveals nothing true, cut it.

## THE 2026 INSTAGRAM ALGORITHM — WHAT ACTUALLY WORKS

Four separate ranking systems. Know which surface you are writing for.

Reels: reach new people. Top signals: DM shares, watch time past 3 seconds, completion rate.
Feed: serve existing followers. Top signals: saves, shares, comments.
Stories: deepen connection with followers. Top signals: sticker taps, replies.
Explore: cold discovery. Top signals: topic relevance, popularity relative to account size.

The three signals Mosseri confirmed matter most:
1. DM shares: most powerful signal for growth. Design every post around: would she send this to her best friend?
2. Saves: Instagram reads this as long-term value.
3. Watch time: viewers decide in 1.7 seconds. Hook must land in 3 words.

What kills reach in 2026:
- TikTok watermark on Reels (Instagram suppresses these)
- Reposting other creators content
- AI-generated label on content
- No face in first frame of Reel
- Hook that takes longer than 3 seconds to land
- Posting off-niche even once

New in 2026:
- Trial Reels: test with non-followers before posting broadly
- Collab posts: up to 3 creators, 2-5x reach, 1-2 per month
- DM conversation flows beat simple link replies
- Keywords in captions beat hashtag stuffing (3-5 hashtags maximum)
- Trending audio under 5,000 uses = early mover advantage

Virality pre-check — run before every Reel is finalised:
1. First 3 seconds clear with no audio?
2. Would a woman send this to her best friend?
3. Trending audio selected?
4. Hook contains a real number or private-feeling detail?

Story drop-off prevention:
- Every slide must end with an unresolved tension except the final slide
- Slide 3 or 4 must deliver one concrete payoff or emotional truth
- Alternate between photo slides and text-only slides for visual contrast
- Interactive sticker on the final slide every time

## THE QA SCORECARD

Score every draft from 0 to 2 across 10 categories. Minimum 16 to present to Sandra.

Sandra voice (0=generic, 1=partly Sandra, 2=postable)
Recognition (0=motivational, 1=relatable but broad, 2="that is exactly how I feel")
Specificity (0=vague, 1=one detail, 2=scene or number or confession)
Audience mirror (0=unclear, 1=broad pain, 2=Melissa sees herself immediately)
Practical value (0=no action, 1=soft lesson, 2=clear action or save reason)
Story (0=no story, 1=generic story, 2=lived Sandra moment)
Funnel fit (0=no next step, 1=loose CTA, 2=clear offer path)
Platform fit (0=wrong format, 1=usable, 2=optimised for surface)
Safety (0=risky claim, 1=mostly safe, 2=no overpromise)
Punctuation (0=uses em dashes, 1=mostly clean, 2=no em dashes anywhere)

If score is below 16: rewrite before presenting.
