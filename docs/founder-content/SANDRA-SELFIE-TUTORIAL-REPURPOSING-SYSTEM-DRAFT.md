# Sandra Selfie Tutorial And Repurposing System

Status: Draft for Sandra approval  
Last updated: 2026-04-27  
Scope: Founder-only content system. Not for user-facing Maya/Studio agents.

## Where Selfie Tutorials Fit

Selfie tutorials are the **visibility engine**.

They are not the whole strategy.

They do three jobs:

1. Create fast saves and shares.
2. Prove Sandra's method in public.
3. Bring new women into the funnel through Selfie Guide and Starter Kit.

The bigger content strategy still needs money, story, AI, behind the scenes, confidence, and selling.

Selfie tutorials are the front door.

## Core Tutorial Promise

> Your face is your brand. If people cannot see you, they cannot buy from you.

Every selfie tutorial should connect:

1. selfie
2. visibility
3. confidence
4. trust
5. income

If it only teaches a pose, it is too small.

## Reel Format

### 1. Hook: 0 to 3 seconds

Start with a bold, curiosity-driven opening or a wrong-way demo.

Goal:

- make her recognize the problem instantly
- make the first frame impossible to skip

Examples:

- "You've been doing mirror selfies wrong."
- "Still taking selfies like this?"
- "I deleted 52 photos because I thought my face was the problem."
- "I deleted 52 photos before I figured this out."

### 2. Visual Demo

Show the exact fix.

Examples:

- pose shift
- angle shift
- back camera
- 5x lens
- Portrait Mode
- Exposure at -1.0 or -0.3
- window light direction
- phone timer
- mirror demo
- screen recording

### 3. Teaching Points

Use text overlays or voiceover to explain why it works.

Keep it simple.

One teaching point per beat.

Examples:

- "Face the window."
- "Drop exposure."
- "Use the back camera."
- "Step back before you zoom."
- "Timer gives your body time to relax."

### 4. The Glow-Up

Show the transformation.

Structure:

- THIS: the bad selfie
- THAT: the brand-level result

This is the proof moment.

### 5. CTA

Use one specific next step.

Examples:

- "DM SELFIE for my free guide."
- "DM KIT for the Selfie Starter Kit."
- "Save this before your next photo."
- "Send this to the friend who deletes every photo."

## Caption Template

Use the same emotional arc:

1. Relatable truth
2. The shift
3. Result
4. Clear CTA

### Example Structure

Relatable truth:

> I used to think I just looked bad in mirror selfies.

The shift:

> Then I realized I was standing in the worst light possible.

Result:

> Same face. Same phone. Completely different photo.

CTA:

> Save this for the next time you are about to delete the whole camera roll.

## Tutorial Rules

- Keep tutorials under 30 seconds when possible.
- Make the fix feel achievable in 5 to 10 minutes.
- Repeat high-performing hooks with fresh visuals.
- Always include Sandra in the frame.
- Use face, mirror, hand, phone, room, or result as proof.
- Do not chase novelty before repeating what works.
- Do not let the tutorial become only technical. It must connect to confidence or visibility.

## Repurposing System

Every selfie tutorial can become at least six assets.

## Production Tools

Sandra's local HTML tools are the production layer for approved briefs:

- `SSELFIE-Story-Creator.html` creates 9:16 Story overlays after the daily story angle is approved.
- `SSELFIE-Carousel-Creator.html` creates saveable carousel overlays after the Reel topic has been validated or chosen for deeper teaching.
- The tools should not be used to invent the strategy. They should receive approved hooks, slide beats, CTAs, and photo notes from the content intelligence workflow.
- Default overlays inside both tools should stay current with the active launch sprint, not old campaign copy.

Production order:

1. Pick the brief from the weekly strategy.
2. Confirm the daily package includes one feed asset and one Story sequence.
3. Approve the hook, story angle, CTA, and funnel bridge.
4. Open Story Creator or Carousel Creator.
5. Upload the photos.
6. Edit only the slide text needed for the exact post.
7. Export and post.
8. Record saves, shares, replies, DMs, opt-ins, and sales movement.

Daily package rule:

- Feed asset creates reach, saves, shares, or comments.
- Story sequence deepens the same topic and creates replies.
- Both assets should point to the same funnel bridge or manual DM route.

### Asset 1: Original Reel

Use for:

- reach
- non-followers
- save/share signal

Structure:

- hook
- wrong way
- fix
- glow-up
- CTA

### Asset 2: Carousel From Screenshots

Post 2 to 4 weeks later.

Use screenshots from the Reel and turn the tutorial into a saveable breakdown.

Structure:

1. Hook slide
2. Wrong way
3. Why it looks wrong
4. The fix
5. Exact setting or pose
6. Before/after
7. Why this matters for visibility
8. CTA

Best CTA:

- "Save this."
- "Send this to the friend who deletes every photo."
- "DM SELFIE."
- "DM KIT."

### Asset 3: Story Sequence

Use the same tutorial as a 5 to 7 slide Story.

Structure:

1. "I see women do this all the time."
2. wrong-way clip
3. poll: "Do you do this too?"
4. fix
5. glow-up
6. resource pitch
7. reply prompt

### Asset 4: Selfie Guide Lesson

If the tutorial performs, save it as guide material.

Use for:

- free Selfie Guide updates
- Starter Kit bonus
- email nurture
- FAQ content

### Asset 5: Caption SEO Post

Turn the tutorial into a caption-first post.

Use natural search phrases:

- how to look good in photos
- mirror selfie tips
- selfie confidence
- how to take better photos with your phone
- how to show up online

### Asset 6: Offer Bridge

Use the tutorial to bridge into a paid offer.

Examples:

- Selfie Guide: "start here"
- Starter Kit: "get the setup and first week of content"
- Brand Strategy Pack: "now you need to know what to say"
- Masterclass: "visibility is the start of income-ready content"
- Studio: "use this every week"

## Reuse Rules

Reuse a tutorial if it has one of these:

- high saves
- high shares
- high comments
- strong DMs
- strong opt-ins
- strong "I needed this" replies

Do not reuse only because it got likes.

## Repurposing Timing

Recommended rhythm:

1. Post Reel.
2. Share Stories same day.
3. Save best replies and questions.
4. Post carousel 2 to 4 weeks later.
5. Use the carousel topic in Stories again.
6. Add the best version to the guide, email, or Starter Kit.

## Tracking Fields

Track each tutorial by:

- tutorial topic
- original hook
- format
- date posted
- saves
- shares
- comments
- DMs
- opt-ins
- carousel reuse date
- best viewer question
- next offer bridge

## What The Agent Should Do

When a selfie tutorial performs, the agent should suggest:

1. a carousel version
2. a Story sequence
3. a caption SEO version
4. a paid offer bridge
5. a new hook variation

The agent should not treat every tutorial as new.

The system should repeat the same working ideas until the data says they are tired.
