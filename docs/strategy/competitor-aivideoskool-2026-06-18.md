## The content engine (what's actually working)
**Two lanes:**

2. **Reels = reach via trends.** Cinematic hooks ("HOW MUCH does a high-end ad cost" over a diamond snake), money/income shots (cash + sunglasses), and he's **riding the toyification/Barbie wave** right now (Barbie-doll faces, toy-aisle scenes).

**The carousel formula (his best move — copy this):**
- **Cover:** big white bold caps, 2 lines, naming the aesthetic ("PHOTOGRAPHIC PRINTS / POSTER PROMPTS") + `@handle` watermark bottom-left.
- **Slides 2–6:** the SAME prompt applied to different famous faces (Julia Roberts, Cillian Murphy, The Rock) + a small "source photo" inset. Celebs = instant recognition + "look, it kept their face" proof.
- **Caption formula (near-verbatim worth modeling):**

  - **No hashtags.** He doesn't rely on them — the keyword-comment + follow CTA carry it.

## The 5 things to steal (ranked)

1. **Numbered prompt keywords.** He uses "Comment **220**" instead of a word. Why it's smarter than our SELFIE/PROMPT: it's unique per post (we can track exactly which prompt drove demand), it implies a huge library ("there are at least 220 of these"), and it feels collectible. **Move:** number our Vault prompts/drops and use the number as the ManyChat comment keyword (e.g. "Comment 14 for the Café Window prompt"). ⚠️ Wire each number in ManyChat or it silently delivers nothing (our known typed-keyword leak).

2. **Demo one aesthetic across multiple faces — but use REAL WOMEN, not celebrities.** His proof is "it kept Julia Roberts' face." Ours is stronger for our buyer: show one prompt on Sandra + 2–3 real women (members, with permission; varied ages/skin tones). 3. **Name every aesthetic + run a cover system.** Each prompt post is a named look with a serif cover + watermark. That's literally our Vault-collection-drop strategy: every new collection ships with a named-aesthetic carousel demo.

4. **The caption formula.** Hook+CTA first line → one-idea-per-line → "high save rate / endless variations" → repeat CTA → follow CTA. Save-worthy and frictionless. Adopt the skeleton, keep her warm voice.

5. **Proof-stacked highlights + bio.** His WIN/CASES/Students'/Reviews. Ours: member before/afters, "she built it" wins, "real women, real faces."

## Turning his visuals into OUR prompts (Vault/SUITE)

His best, most stealable aesthetic is the **"Photographic Prints Poster" / Polaroid-collage portrait.** Here it is rewritten in our shipped Vault anatomy — No-Fake, keeps-your-face, ChatGPT-paste ready:

### VAULT PROMPT — "Polaroid Story" (collage portrait)
**Title:** Polaroid Story
**When to use:** when you want one striking, save-worthy brand image that feels like a story, not a single headshot. Great for a profile photo refresh or an "about me" post.
**Mood:** nostalgic, editorial, warm.

**Prompt (paste into ChatGPT with your selfie attached):**
> Use the attached photo as the exact identity reference. Keep my real face, features, skin texture, and proportions exactly as they are — same nose, jaw, eyes, and natural skin. Do not beautify, slim, or change my face.
>
> Create a single portrait of me that is composed of many overlapping Polaroid-style photo prints, as if my face and shoulders are built from a scattered stack of memories. Each print shows a slightly different angle or moment of the same me. A pair of hands holds the largest print at the center. Background is a soft, dark, out-of-focus setting so the prints stand out.
>
> Style: realistic editorial photography, natural film grain, soft directional light, true-to-life skin (no plastic smoothing, no glassy skin). Keep it elegant and warm.
>
> Body and proportions: natural, realistic body proportions that match the reference. Do not lengthen, slim, or alter my body.
>
> Avoid: changing my facial features, a different person, waxy or airbrushed skin, extra fingers, distorted hands, text, logos, heavy filters, oversaturation.

**Visual brief for the IG carousel that sells it:** cover "POLAROID STORY" white serif + watermark; slides = the same prompt on Sandra + 2 real women (with permission); last slide "Comment [number] and I'll send you the prompt." Keyword wired in ManyChat.
