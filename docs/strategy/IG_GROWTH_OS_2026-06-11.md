## 3. The SSELFIE Viral Content OS

### Content pillars (weighted)
1. **Selfie tutorials, 40%.** The proven 1.8M engine. Phone tricks, posing, lighting, editing. Keyword: SELFIE.
2. **AI photoshoot prompts demonstrated on YOU, 30%.** One selfie → editorial result, honest craft explanation, anti-waxy-skin tips. Keyword: PROMPT. This is the Vault funnel.
3. **Build-the-brand story, 20%.** Your single-mum-to-founder story, client results, "why visibility pays". This converts followers into buyers and makes you a person, not a prompt account. Soft CTA or none.
4. **Objection killers, 10%.** Android tutorials, "is AI fake?" talks, "I hate my face on camera". Each one answers a real comment theme.

### The non-negotiable reel checklist (all five, every time)
- [ ] Transformation or promise visible in the first 2 seconds (before/after, or result first)
- [ ] About HER ("your selfie", "your outfit", "your business"), not about you
- [ ] Steps she can steal, numbered, text on screen
- [ ] One comment keyword, said in the video AND the caption
- [ ] Honest AI language: "keeps your face", "still you, just elevated". Never "no one will know"

### The repost engine (proven: 319k, 526k, 581k from ONE old reel)
Every 8 to 12 weeks, re-run a proven winner with: new cover, new first line, current year in the hook ("still works in 2026"). Rotate through the winner library. New followers haven't seen it; the algorithm treats it as new content.

### Cover text system (your grid is your storefront)
Winners had serif editorial covers with 2 to 4 words: "PROMPT MY SELFIE", "Car SELFIE", "SUMMER SELFIE TUTORIAL". Keep that exact style. Every cover: WHAT it is + that it's a tutorial. No vague poetry on tutorial covers; save that for story posts.

## 9. Weekly testing system (stop guessing)

One variable per week, everything else held constant:
- **Week test slots:** Monday reel = control (proven format). Wednesday reel = the test.
- **What to test, in order:** hook line → cover text → length (15s vs 35s) → location → CTA phrasing. Never two at once.
- **Decision rule (7 days after posting):** views ≥3x baseline = promote to the winner library (repost candidate). Views ≤ baseline AND saves low = kill the variant, log why.
- **Metrics that matter, in order:** DM keyword count (money), saves+shares (algorithm fuel), views (reach), likes (vanity, ignore).
- Log every reel in the tracker (date, pillar, hook, keyword, views d7, comments, DMs triggered, verdict). The automation below does this for you.
