# Your Personal AI Team Delivery Contract

Status: CURRENT PRIVATE OFFER CONTRACT

Owner: Sandra

Price: EUR 2,000 paid in full

Length: six weeks

Capacity gate: accept two cleared buyers, verify delivery quality and Sandra's real workload, then decide whether to open the remaining three places.

## The one problem

Her business still depends on her for too much. The research, plans, writing, organizing, preparation, and repeatable work wait for her brain and her time.

She has tried ChatGPT or another AI tool, but it does not know the business well enough to become reliable help. She keeps starting over and explaining the same context again.

## The buyer

She already has a real business, something people buy, clients or customers she understands, and recurring work she wants help carrying.

This is not for a woman starting from zero or choosing her first business.

## The promise

Build and train a personal AI team around her business, customers, offers, voice, decisions, and way of working.

The team prepares the work chosen during intake. She keeps her client relationships, judgment, and every final decision.

## What she receives

1. One Business Brain with her business, customers, offers, voice, proof, decisions, visual direction, and boundaries.
2. One founder workload map that identifies the three recurring areas where trained AI support can genuinely help.
3. Three personal AI roles chosen around her real workload. They may support research, planning, content, writing, organizing ideas, or repeatable preparation.
4. Three repeatable workflows using her real business work.
5. One 30-day working plan showing what to give each role and when.
6. Four private 45-minute calls with Sandra.
7. Training and handover so she can keep using and improving the team.

## The six weeks

### Week 1: Learn the real business

- Confirm what she sells, who she helps, and how the business currently works.
- Map the work that keeps coming back to her.
- Choose the three areas the team will support.

### Week 2: Business Brain

- Build the shared source of truth.
- Remove guesses and conflicting claims.
- Get the client's approval on her offers, customers, voice, decisions, and boundaries.

### Week 3: Build and train the team

- Configure three personal AI roles for the chosen work.
- Connect every role to the same Business Brain.
- Test each role on real examples from her business.

### Week 4: Use it on real work

- Run live research, plans, drafts, or repeatable preparation through the team.
- Check that the output is useful before it reaches the client.
- Remove generic language and unnecessary steps.

### Week 5: Refine from use

- Review what the client used, changed, ignored, or did not trust.
- Simplify each workflow.
- Retrain only the parts that failed the real work.

### Week 6: Handover

- Finish three repeatable workflows.
- Give the client her 30-day working plan and update instructions.
- Confirm what each role handles and what the client still owns.

## Business Brain minimum

- Business and current offers
- Best customers and their real problems
- Client language, questions, proof, and objections
- Voice examples and visual direction
- Recurring work and current priorities
- Important decisions and working preferences
- Claims that are true
- Claims the team must never make
- Final decision and relationship owner

## Quality gate

Every role and workflow must be specific to the client's real business, useful without Sandra rebuilding it from zero, and clear about what the client does next. Generic outputs, invented proof, hidden automation, and vague strategy do not count as delivery.

## Boundaries

This is not whole-business automation, CRM implementation, customer service, inbox access, automatic publishing, lead generation, or AI replacing employees. It does not promise clients, income, growth, or a business that runs without her.

No agent may expand the promise without Sandra's exact approval.
