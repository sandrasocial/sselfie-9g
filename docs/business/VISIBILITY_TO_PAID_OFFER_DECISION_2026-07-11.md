# Visibility To Paid Sprint — Locked Offer Decision

Date: 2026-07-11  
Owner: Sandra  
Status: Locked from the live Work With Me implementation; no new implementation spec is needed.

## The offer

**Visibility To Paid Sprint** is Sandra's warm-audience, private service for a woman who already has
something real to build from but cannot yet make it clear online.

- Format: two weeks of Sandra-led research and first-version building, followed by a private
  four-week sprint.
- Price: **€2,000**, or **2 × €1,100** on the payment plan.
- Entry: `/visibility-to-paid` and `/work-with-me` use the same application.
- Sale: application, personal review, short fit call, then an attended payment link only when the fit
  is clear. No passive checkout and no payment at application.
- Delivery: private, not a group beta and not a course.

## Who it is for

She already has at least one real starting point: a skill, service, offer, story, expertise, business,
or idea people ask her about. She needs help making her identity, message, content, visual direction,
and first paid path understandable.

It is not for someone seeking guaranteed income, a passive template pack, generic social-media
management, or a fully outsourced business with no real expertise or idea to build from.

## What Sandra builds before the sprint

1. Niche and demand research.
2. A profile and visibility diagnosis.
3. The first version of her one-line identity and buyer transformation.
4. A clear first-offer hypothesis.
5. A starter set of brand-photo direction and images.
6. Captions and a practical weekly content direction to react to on the first call.

## What the four-week sprint completes

1. Identity, buyer, and before/after language.
2. The first offer and the next paid step.
3. Profile direction and content pillars.
4. The first seven posts or story directions.
5. Selfie/AI visual direction that still feels like her.
6. A clear CTA and follow-up path.
7. A repeatable working system inside her own SSELFIE SUITE account.

## Fit and sales rules

- Qualified means she has something real to build from, a clear visibility gap, and willingness to
  consider the €2,000 private sprint.
- Every qualified application routes to a fit call. DMs never receive an automatic payment link.
- The pipeline remains: applied → qualified/contacted → call booked/completed → offer sent → won/lost.
- Sandra records the lost reason and follows up personally. Automated agent sends remain off.
- Proof capture after delivery includes before/after profile direction, offer sentence, content
  direction, testimonial, and permission status.

## Success outcome

The client leaves able to explain who she is, what she helps with, what she can sell first, what to
post next, and how to keep showing up without rebuilding the plan every week. This is a clarity and
execution outcome, not an income promise.
