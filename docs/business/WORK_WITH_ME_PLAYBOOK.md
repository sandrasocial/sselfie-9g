# Work With Me — Playbook (private 4-week sprint, €2,000)

Reusable system for every Work With Me client. Built 2026-06-23 (first client: Harmony Steingrebe, luxury real estate, Tahoe/Reno).

**Design principle:** turn "all of it" into ONE goal + a few bounded deliverables, with a written container, so Sandra over-delivers on the *result*, never on hours. The agreement is the anti-burnout AND anti-disappointment tool.

**Framing (important):** sell it as a focused **sprint to build her foundation + a system she can run**, with SUITE as the ongoing layer after. 4 weeks to build the engine, then she drives it. (4 weeks alone reads as "rushed" for high-ticket; the sprint + SUITE framing fixes that.)

---

## The flow
1. Application (already a mini-intake) → Sandra replies (below) with the payment link.
2. She pays (standing €2,000 Stripe Payment Link).
3. **Welcome packet** email lands (below) → 4 steps: okay the plan · fill the intake · start now (Masterclass + photos) · book the first call.
4. Kickoff call → set the ONE goal + scope from her intake → map the 4 weeks.
5. 4 weekly 45-min calls + bounded async support.
6. Handoff → she leaves with her system → continues on SUITE (€97/mo).

## 3. The agreement / one-page plan (the burnout guardrail)
*Warm and clear, not legal. Both sides agree by starting. Fill the goal + deliverables at kickoff.*

**Work With Me — your four weeks**

**The goal.** [Set at kickoff. Harmony example: a presence that brings in qualified buyer and seller leads on a few minutes a week.] Everything we do serves this one goal.

**What we'll build together (3–4 things, set at kickoff).** Harmony example:
- Your message + brand photos (the recognizable Tahoe luxury name)
- One simple lead-capture offer and where it lives
- A light weekly content rhythm + your first batch
- DM and follow-up scripts that turn inquiries into conversations

**Not in this round (great next steps, after).** Anything beyond the goal above. We park it on purpose so you're never buried, and you keep going on SUITE.

**How we work.**
- Four weeks. One 45-minute call a week, booked through [scheduling link].
- Between calls, questions go in [email / one channel]. I answer within one business day, during working hours.
- I build the agreed pieces. New ideas are welcome and become a next-round or SUITE thing, so we both stay focused.

**What I need from you.** Show up to the calls, do the small steps between them, and share what I need (logins, photos, info) on time.

**Investment.** €2,000, one time, paid up front. Because this is private time set aside for you, it's non-refundable once we begin.

**Done looks like.** You leave with your message, your offer, your content system, and a clear next move you can run yourself, or keep running with me inside SUITE.

*By starting, we're both agreeing to this plan.*

## 4. The intake (so the first call is productive)
Recommended: use the existing **Brand Strategy tool** (bundled in Masterclass) as the intake — it captures most of this AND gives her a strategy result (instant value). If a plain form is easier, these are the questions:
1. Your business in one line. Who do you help, and with what?
2. Your market. Where, and what makes it specific? (e.g. luxury Tahoe/Reno)
3. Where do you show up online now? Drop your handles + website.
4. If these four weeks were a win, what would be true at the end?
5. Time reality: how many minutes a week can you realistically give to this?
6. What do buyers and sellers usually ask you, or choose you for?
7. What feels hardest about being online?
8. Anything you've already tried that didn't work?
9. Links to any photos, logos, or assets you have.

## 5. Setup Sandra does once (the two external pieces)

### A. The €2,000 payment link (Stripe — 60 seconds)
Stripe Dashboard → **Payment Links** → **New** → one-time → amount **€2,000 EUR** → name "Work With Me · Private 4-Week Sprint" → (optional) after-payment confirmation message "You're in. Check your inbox for your welcome from Sandra." → **Create** → copy the link. Paste it into the reply (§1) and reuse it for every applicant.
*(Or ask Codex to create it via the Stripe API for a permanent, tracked link. Either works.)*

### B. The scheduling link (Calendly or Cal.com — free)
Create one event type: **"Work With Me Session" · 45 min.** Set availability to **specific windows only** (e.g. Tue + Thu afternoons). The limited windows ARE the boundary that protects your week — don't open your whole calendar. Copy the booking link into the welcome packet (§2, step 4). Cal.com (free, generous) or Calendly (free tier) both work.
