**Investment.** €2,000, one time, paid up front. Because this is private time set aside for you, it's non-refundable once we begin.

**Done looks like.** You leave with your message, your offer, your content system, and a clear next move you can run yourself, or keep running with me inside SUITE.

_By starting, we're both agreeing to this plan._

## 4. The intake (so the first call is productive)

Recommended: use the existing **Brand Strategy tool** (bundled in Masterclass) as the intake — it captures most of this AND gives her a strategy result (instant value). If a plain form is easier, these are the questions:

1. Your business in one line. Who do you help, and with what?
2. Your market. Where, and what makes it specific? (e.g. luxury Tahoe/Reno)
3. Where do you show up online now? Drop your handles + website.
4. If these four weeks were a win, what would be true at the end?
5. Time reality: how many minutes a week can you realistically give to this?
6. What do buyers and sellers usually ask you, or choose you for?
7. What feels hardest about being online?
8. Anything you've already tried that didn't work?
9. Links to any photos, logos, or assets you have.
