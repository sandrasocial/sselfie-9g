business authority. Model names and versions are replaceable tools; roles stay stable.

Do not revive “one commercial experiment,” automatic Work With Me routing, a low-ticket-only
funnel, or an old launch because it appears in memory. Do not invent current audience, email, MRR,
sales, or conversion numbers; pull the live source and attach the verification date.
