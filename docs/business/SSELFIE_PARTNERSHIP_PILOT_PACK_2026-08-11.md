## The one offer

### SSELFIE Tutorial Partnership — working pilot floor EUR 3,000

Sandra teaches one useful, visually clear transformation using the brand's product in the way her
audience already knows her: selfies, phone photography, hair and beauty, editing, design, and
personal-brand content.

The pilot contains:

- one original tutorial concept tied to a real customer use case;
- one finished short-form tutorial published on one agreed primary Sandra channel;
- one review round for product facts, not a rewrite of Sandra's opinion or voice;
- organic brand reposting for 30 days with credit and a link to the original;
- a short result note after the agreed reporting window.

The EUR 3,000 floor does not include paid advertising, whitelisting, raw footage, exclusivity,
perpetual usage, additional versions, guaranteed views, or guaranteed sales. Those require a
separate written scope and fee. Payment in full reserves production; 50 percent to reserve and 50
percent before publication is the procurement fallback. Working founder-time ceiling: one concept,
one filming block, one edit, and one factual review round.

This is an unvalidated rate hypothesis, not booked revenue. A buyer-confirmed brief and budget can
change the final quote. Product-only gifting never buys valuable tutorial usage.
