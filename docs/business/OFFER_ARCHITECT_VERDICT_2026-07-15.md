## 1. Launch verdict: LAUNCH WITH TWO NAMED GUARDS (not hold, not blind go)

**Composition, frame, and $97 price are defensible. The event data does not indict them.**

- **Composition** — a done-for-you campaign kit (6 photos, 3 posts, 1 carousel, 2 Story
  sequences, 5-day plan, 1 reel) for one promotion is coherent: it completes one job rather
  than piling output. It sits correctly between the $42 own-face photo pack and the $99/mo DFY
  content tier (MARKET_INTEL_OFFER_AND_MEMBERSHIP_2026-07-15.md §1). FACT: no advertiser was
  found running this exact combined SKU under five search phrasings — it is a genuinely novel
  shape, so it is a HYPOTHESIS, and the founding batch is the only valid proof. Sandra's
  skepticism ("I wouldn't buy 5 AI posts for $97") is correct in kind — which is why the page
  must read as "your promotion, planned, produced, human-checked," itemized line by line,
  never "N AI posts" (CAMPAIGN_COPY_POLISH_2026-07-15.md B2.1). That guard is already in the
  copy contract.
- **Frame** ("For the woman who knows what she's building and freezes when it's time to
  post") — INFERENCE: well-supported. The behavior addendum shows specificity, not category,
  predicts payment: women who articulated what they're building were 8 of 12 typed payers and
  generated 5–40x more (MARKET_INTEL §3b). The frame selects for exactly that trait. Keep it.
- **$97** — defensible by adjacency (one-tenth the human floor, one month of the DFY tier).
  FACT: a real $97 buyer already exists in stripe_payments; the event's 5 bundle buyers paid
  $97 without resistance. The risk is frame, not number.

**Why not "hold":** the offer is implemented, fail-closed, and QA-gated at ≤10 orders.
Holding costs a month and learns nothing; the founding batch is the cheapest possible truth
test.

**The two guards (both already preconditions in ONE_SELFIE_WEEK_OUTCOME_TEST_2026-07-16.md):**
1. **Qualified traffic, not free-prompt collectors.** Running this on prompt-collector traffic
   produces a false negative (that segment converts 1.5%, SSELFIE_TRUTH_INVESTIGATION Part 2).
   The launch must ship with its own building-something hooks.
2. **Founding batch capped ≤10 with Sandra QA on every order**, redo-rate tracked from order
   one. This is the differentiator vs the $29–59 commodity and the only defense on the #1
   market complaint (likeness).

## 2. The honest $200K ARR map

$200K ARR ≈ **$16,667 MRR**. A $97 one-time is not recurring, so it reaches ARR only two
ways: (A) sustained repeat-purchase run-rate, or (B) feeding a recurring SUITE. Both were run
against our observed rates.

**Observed anchors (FACT, close-night + docs):**
- Bundle event: 5 buyers / $485 over 48h — *below* the pre-committed 6-buyer floor.
- Email money-ask: ~6,600 delivered, 46–47% open, ~1% click; free-content clicks historically
  17–34%.
- Confirmed qualified landers per send ≈ 24 (ONE_SELFIE_ATTRIBUTION_VERDICT_2026-07-14.md).
- Page→buy ≈ **7–10%** of confirmed landers (5 buyers off ~50–70 landers across the event) —
  healthy, at or above the 2–3% gate.
- Instant annual-SUITE upsell at delivery: **0 of 21 viewers** (FACT).
- Baseline recurring: 8 members, ~$393 net MRR (June 11 figure; re-verify live before acting
  on Scenario B math); first €697 annual July 2.

**Scenario A — one-time only:** $200K / $97 = 2,062 sales/yr = **~40/week, every week**. At
8% page→buy that needs ~500 qualified landers/week; at ~24 landers per email send, ~20
sends/week to the same 6,600 list — impossible without new traffic. Best flash ever = 18
Vault sales over 4 days. **The one-time front door alone cannot reach $200K at observed
throughput. It plausibly contributes $15–30K/yr at one-event-per-month cadence.**

**Scenario B — recurring-carried:** hold ~**159 members at €97** (or ~265 annual at €697) to
equal $16.7K MRR — a 20x on today's 8. RevenueCat 2026 (verified in TRUTH_INVESTIGATION Part
4): AI monthly plans retain ~11% at one year. Even at a generous 8%/mo churn, holding 159
sheds ~13/month, so ~13–27 gross member-adds are needed **every month just to stand still** —
and the earned-SUITE conversion mechanism has produced 0 of 21 so far. To net those adds at
even a 10% earn rate needs ~130–270 campaign buyers/month = 30–60/week — the same
top-of-funnel wall as Scenario A, plus an unproven conversion step.

**Both roads dead-end at one constraint: qualified-buyer throughput on money asks.** The page
converts; the offer is coherent; the wall is getting dozens of qualified humans to the page
per week. That is the historical broken link (money-ask clicks ~1% vs free 17–34%), and no
document has closed it.

**Plain conclusion: $200K ARR in 12 months is NOT plausible from this $97 front door on
current evidence.** What would feed it: (1) a repeatable acquisition engine converting the
819K-reach IG audience or paid traffic into dozens of qualified landers/week — the one thing
that has never worked, gated behind the ads criteria (20 organic buyers, 2–3% sustained);
(2) an earned recurring layer with proven ≥25% repeat and <5–8% churn; realistically
(3) higher-priced repeat/annual. A defensible 12-month target from current evidence is closer
to **$40–80K blended run-rate** if repeat and SUITE earn modestly — a real business, not
$200K.

**Benchmarks (fresh, verified 2026-07-15):**
- **PhotoAI (Pieter Levels)** — ~$105K/mo (~$1.26M ARR), Mar 2026; solo; own-face AI photos;
  one-time packs + subscription. REPORTED, HIGH credibility (founder publishes revenue).
  Lesson: scale came from a global self-serve/SEO/viral front door, not a 6,600 warm list.
- **Coffee & Contracts** — $74/mo DFY content membership (FACT, pricing verified), durable
  6-yr business, price raised $19→$74 with grandfathering. Lesson: recurring content is
  durable at ~$74 when it's DFY + human/community; €97 SUITE is near the solo ceiling but
  survivable only if delivery is real.
- **Socinova** — $99/mo DFY multi-format content (FACT, verified). The content half of the
  kit prices as a *subscription* elsewhere — reinforces "earn recurrence, don't sell 5 posts
  once."
- **Meta-finding (INFERENCE):** well-documented solo AI businesses cluster <$100K or >$1M;
  verifiable ~$200K-exact solo cases are thin, and every one that scaled ran on an acquisition
  engine, not a warm email list. (The "Sarah Chen $420K" listicle is LOW credibility — do not
  lean on it.)

## 3. Top 3 risks + kill criteria

1. **Market-unproven combination; the event's $97 ask fell below floor.** Counter-reading
   (real): the bundle was a different product (learning tools + pass, not a done-for-you
   outcome); a 2-hour DB outage overlapped the last-call window; the list ≠ the IG audience.
   Weak evidence about the campaign offer specifically. **Kill criterion (contract gate):**
   campaign purchases ≤3 from the first 200 qualified landers = promise/segment/price failed;
   4–5 = uncertain; 6+ = proceed.
2. **Throughput wall — the money-ask click gap.** Even a perfect offer starves at ~1% clicks /
   ~24 landers per send. **Kill criterion:** if organic launch cycles cannot deliver ≥200
   qualified landers, the front door cannot scale without paid traffic — and ads stay gated
   until 20 organic buyers + 2–3% page→buy sustained.
3. **Manual fulfillment + likeness quality.** Likeness failure is the market's #1 complaint;
   Sandra QAs every order. **Kill criterion:** redo/refund-request rate approaching the
   industry ~1-in-3 identity-drift baseline, OR per-order QA >10 minutes → pause the batch and
   fix automation before continuing.
   - Sub-risk: the SUITE-earn mechanism is unproven (0/21 instant upsell). Do not model any
     ARR on SUITE conversion until the repeat gate reads.

## 4. Founding-batch measurement plan — 5 numbers from the first ≤10 orders (30-day decision)

1. **Qualified landing → paid** (page→buy %). Decides *scale*: sustained ≥2–3% with the offer
   front-loaded = the page works; the fight moves entirely to traffic volume.
2. **Redo / refund-request rate** vs the ~1-in-3 industry baseline. Decides *reprice*: well
   under 1-in-3 earns $97 and a future $149; at/above it, the still-you promise isn't holding.
3. **Sandra-minutes per order + % fully automated.** Decides *scale vs die*: >10 min/order
   means a validation-only concierge, not a business.
4. **% who publish something within 7 days** (gate ≥40%). Decides *usefulness*: downloads
   without posting = the kit isn't finishing the job.
5. **% who buy a SECOND campaign within 30–45 days** (gate ≥25%). Decides *recurring vs
   episodic*: ≥25% = the earned-SUITE case has money behind it; <10% = keep it one-time (a
   real episodic business, but ARR must come from volume, not subscription).

**Stop-list for launch (from the contract, endorsed):** no price range (one $97 denominator),
no second competing offer on the page, no "months of content" claims, no income promises, no
ads, no SUITE upsell reframed as the front door, no batch beyond 10 until numbers 2 and 3
pass.
