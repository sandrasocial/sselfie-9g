# Market intel — the $97 campaign offer and the membership (2026-07-15)

Status: EVIDENCE MEMO (market-intel skill, first run). Triggered by Sandra's challenge:
"Is the deliverable something people will buy? Do we have evidence the price matches the
outcome? I would not buy 5 AI-generated posts for $97. People churned because it was buggy
and confusing, not because they don't want it."
Sources: two parallel web-research sweeps 2026-07-15 (live pricing pages, Meta Ads Library,
review platforms, ChartMogul Sept-2025 retention report, founder interviews). Strength
labels per finding in the underlying research; this memo keeps only load-bearing items.

## 1. Direct answer: would people pay $97 for the campaign kit?

**The components are proven. The combination is not.**

- Photos built from her OWN selfie, one-time, with a redo guarantee: proven demand at
  $30-93 (Etsy listing with 1,200+ verified purchases at ~$42; volume thins sharply near
  $93 — only 44 reviews there). Aragon runs always-on Meta ads for $18-75 headshots.
- Multi-format done-for-you content (posts + captions + strategy + calendar): proven at
  **$99/MONTH recurring** (Socinova), not one-time. The content half of our kit prices
  like a subscription elsewhere.
- The exact combined SKU (photos + posts + carousel + Stories + plan, one-time): **zero
  advertisers found running it under five different search phrasings.** Nobody has
  ad-spend-validated this bundle. It is a genuinely novel offer = hypothesis, not a
  category. Sandra's skepticism is therefore correct in kind: there is no direct market
  proof for this shape. The founding-batch test IS the proof mechanism, and it must stay
  small and measured.

**Price verdict:** $97 is defensible by adjacency (above the photo-only ceiling, one-tenth
of the human-service floor of ~$1,000, equal to ONE MONTH of the recurring DFY tier). The
risk is not the number — it is the FRAME. At $75+, a likeness/redo guarantee is now table
stakes (free at the $18-75 tier: Aragon, HeadshotPro, BetterPic all offer it). What the
market shows justifies $97-297: a visible STRATEGY layer, MULTIPLE formats itemized, and
HUMAN review. If the page reads as "N AI posts," it competes with $8-25 template packs and
loses. If it reads as "your promotion, planned and produced, human-checked" with a
line-item list, it sits correctly between the $42 photo pack and the $99/mo service.

## 2. What the offer must contain/say to clear the bar (changes adopted)

1. **Itemized "what you get," line by line** (mirror the $99/mo tier's list format), never
   a count of posts. Lead with the strategy + human layer: "planned for what you're
   promoting · written in your voice · checked by Sandra · delivered in 48 hours."
2. **Ground the generator in proven patterns and say so.** Sandra's own corpus (156-reel
   viral analysis; hook patterns at 40-180x baseline in this niche) becomes a generator
   input, and the page may then honestly claim "built from patterns proven in this niche"
   — a claim template-pack sellers cannot make. (Spec note for CAMPAIGN-OUTCOME:
   generator grounding + a traceability note per campaign.)
3. **The metric that settles $97:** redo/refund-request rate vs the industry's ~1-in-3
   identity-drift baseline. If our still-you pipeline holds redo requests well under that,
   the premium is earned and provable; track it from order one.
4. Guarantee stays Sandra's approved line ("If you don't recognize yourself in a photo,
   we redo it") — necessary, no longer sufficient; never the only proof point.

## 3. The membership: the evidence sides with Sandra

- **Coffee & Contracts is now $74/mo**, raised $19→$34→$54→$74 over six years, always
  grandfathering — you only get to do that with durable demand. What survives churn there:
  done-for-you-ness + coaching + community. Never asset volume.
- **ChartMogul (Sept 2025, ~3,500 companies): AI-native subscriptions under $50/mo retain
  at 23% GRR** — buyers treat them as trials. $50-249/mo retains ~2x better (45%/61%).
  **This contradicts the earlier "$39-49 Monthly Drop" assumption**: a cheap drop is
  structurally the leakiest shape in the market. SUITE's €97 tier is the survivable band.
- Churn causality: the one documented AI-product churn fix (Interior AI, 30%→20%) came
  from product value, not price cuts. No public unused-vs-quality breakdown exists for
  this comp set — Sandra's "they churned because it was buggy" hypothesis is undisproven
  externally and now testable internally (fixed product + delivered month + posted loop).
- Buyer language: the objection is never "AI" — it is "doesn't look/sound like me" and
  "generic." AI-content preference collapsed 60%→26% (2023→2026); 98% of 30k surveyed say
  authentic images are essential. The still-you position sits exactly on the fracture.
- Retention levers with evidence: annual billing (+10-20 NRR points), grandfathered price
  increases, pause options (industry-pattern inference), compounding product value.

**Membership implications (feed into the postmortem decision):**
1. Keep the €97/mo tier; do NOT introduce a cheap drop tier. The path is C&C's: deliver
   monthly (CALENDAR-UPGRADE-01 + MEMBER-RECAP-01), grandfather loyalists, raise price
   later only on evidence.
2. Annual plan emphasis once delivery is real (the €697 founding annual already exists).
3. Pause-instead-of-cancel: evidence-supported lever, awaiting Sandra's yes.
4. **The one honest gap vs the proven model: coaching/community.** C&C's churn-survivors
   stay for the human layer. SSELFIE has Maya but no live/community layer. Candidate
   (Sandra's call, not urgent): one monthly Sandra-led live "post with me" session for
   members — small, human, unfakeable. Decide after the delivered month ships.

## 3b. Behavior addendum (added later 2026-07-15 — the layer job titles can't give)

Joining declared business type × generation behavior × subscriptions × real payments
(n small — 12 typed payers total — treat as strong directional signal, not proof):

| Segment (self-declared) | n | ever generated | avg images | real payers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| unknown (left it empty) | 130 | 6 (5%) | 2.0 | 1 |
| "other" (wrote something SPECIFIC in her own words) | 101 | 47 (47%) | 82.3 | 8 |
| coach | 47 | 37 (79%) | 58.7 | 2 |
| creator (self-labeled) | 54 | 9 (17%) | 9.8 | 1 |
| service pro | 8 | 3 | 39.6 | 0 |

**The commercial signal is SPECIFICITY, not category.** Women who can articulate what they
are building — whatever words they use — engage 5-40x more and account for 8 of 12 typed
payers. Coaches are the strongest NAMED segment (79% ever generated). Self-labeled
"content creators" barely engage (17%) — the label correlates with browsing, not building.
The 130 empties are commercially inert today (1 payer): whatever they are, they are not a
segment to target; they are a nurture pool. This corrects the earlier read that "creators
are the plurality → creators are the segment."

Positioning consequence (adopted): brand-level — "For women building a personal brand who
want to show up consistently, with content that still looks and feels like them." Campaign
sharpening — "Everything you need to promote what you're building, starting with one
selfie." The qualifier word is BUILDING: it selects for direction and specificity without
requiring an existing offer, matches the Constitution ("build something of your own"), and
matches who actually engages and pays. Instrumentation gap found: `suite_intent_detected`
events carry no intent label (360 events, all unlabeled) — small Codex fix so "what
members ask Maya for" becomes queryable.
