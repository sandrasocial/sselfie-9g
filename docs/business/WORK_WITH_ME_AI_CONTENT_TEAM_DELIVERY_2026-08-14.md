# Your AI Content Team Delivery Contract

Status: CURRENT PRIVATE OFFER CONTRACT

Owner: Sandra

Price: EUR 2,000 paid in full

Length: six weeks

Capacity gate: accept two cleared buyers, verify delivery quality and Sandra's real workload, then decide whether to open the remaining three places.

## The one problem

She should not have to run her business and be the entire marketing team too. Every post, email, idea, and research task still depends on her starting from a blank page.

## The buyer

She already has a real business, a service people buy, clients she understands, and a reason to keep showing up. She has tried ChatGPT or another AI tool, but it felt generic, confusing, or became one more thing she stopped using.

This is not for a woman starting from zero, choosing her first business, or looking for guaranteed leads.

## The promise

Build and train a personal AI content team around her business, customers, offers, voice, visual direction, and real weekly workflow.

The team helps research, plan, write, and repurpose. She keeps final judgment and publishing control.

## What she receives

1. One Business Brain with her business, customers, offers, voice, proof, visual direction, and boundaries.
2. One research assistant that finds useful questions, themes, category shifts, and source material.
3. One content director that turns priorities into a focused weekly plan.
4. One writer and repurposer that turns an idea or voice note into drafts that sound like her.
5. One repeatable weekly flow from source idea to review-ready marketing.
6. Her first 30 days of marketing drafted through the system.
7. Four private 45-minute calls with Sandra.
8. Training and handover so she can keep using the team.

## The six weeks

### Week 1: Kickoff and intake

- Confirm the one weekly marketing burden to remove.
- Confirm her existing offer, customer, channels, capacity, and approval boundaries.
- Collect real voice, visual, offer, proof, and customer source material.

### Week 2: Business Brain

- Build the shared source of truth.
- Remove guesses and conflicting claims.
- Get the client's approval on voice, customers, offers, and boundaries.

### Week 3: Build and train the team

- Configure the research assistant.
- Configure the content director.
- Configure the writer and repurposer.
- Test each role against the same Business Brain.

### Week 4: First real marketing week

- Start with one idea or voice note.
- Produce research, a focused plan, and review-ready drafts.
- Sandra and the client remove generic language and unnecessary volume.

### Week 5: Refine from use

- Review what the client used, ignored, changed, or did not trust.
- Simplify the flow.
- Retrain only the parts that failed the real work.

### Week 6: Handover

- Finish the first 30 days.
- Give the client the weekly runbook and safe update instructions.
- Confirm what the team does, what the client still owns, and what is outside scope.

## Business Brain minimum

- Business and current offer
- Best customer and her real problem
- Client language, questions, proof, and objections
- Sandra-approved or client-approved voice examples
- Visual direction and examples
- Weekly content needs and channels
- Claims that are true
- Claims the team must never make
- Final approval and publishing owner

## Weekly workflow

1. Client gives one source idea, voice note, lesson, customer question, or current priority.
2. Research assistant adds useful context and source links.
3. Content director chooses one angle and the smallest useful weekly plan.
4. Writer creates drafts using the Business Brain.
5. Client reviews. Nothing publishes automatically.
6. The team saves useful corrections for the next week.

## Quality gate

Every deliverable must be specific to the client's real business, usable without Sandra rewriting it from zero, and clear about what the client does next. Generic drafts, invented proof, hidden automation, and vague strategy do not count as delivery.

## Boundaries

This is not whole-business automation, CRM implementation, customer service, inbox access, automatic posting, lead generation, or a promise of clients, income, growth, or competitive advantage. It is not generic AI education, offer creation, or a photo and content package.

No agent may expand the promise without Sandra's exact approval.
