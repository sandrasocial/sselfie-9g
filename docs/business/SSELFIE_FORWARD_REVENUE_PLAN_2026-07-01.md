# SSELFIE Forward Revenue Plan

## Executive Decision

SSELFIE should stop treating every part of the audience as if they are ready to buy the same thing.

There are two different jobs now:

1. Make the top of funnel sell a simple, obvious paid result.
2. Give the warmer audience a clear path into help that can change their online reality.

The cold Instagram audience is telling us:

> Help me take a better photo. Help me turn one selfie into AI photos that still look like me.

The warmer story, DM, email, and application audience is telling us:

> I want to build online, but I do not know what to post, what to say, what I can sell, or how to make people understand me.

Both are real.

They need different offers.

## Product Separation Lock

Do not collapse the selfie education funnel and the AI prompts funnel into one product.

- **Free Selfie Guide -> Selfie Starter Kit** is for the woman who wants to take better iPhone photos and better selfies.
- **Free AI Prompts / PROMPT funnel -> Selfie To AI Photos Kit** is for the woman who wants to turn one clear selfie into AI photos that still look like her.

The existing `starter_kit` product, `/starter-kit`, `/checkout/starter-kit`, `/access/starter-kit/[token]`, and the `KIT` keyword remain the **Selfie Starter Kit** path.

Do not send people who comment `KIT` to the AI Photos Kit.

The Selfie To AI Photos Kit needs its own approved product/checkout path before it is sold. Until then, it belongs to the Prompt/AI funnel strategy, not the live Starter Kit checkout.

## The Plan Going Forward

### 1. Top Of Funnel Offer

Build a simple paid front-door product:

> Selfie To AI Photos Kit

This is for the 2 million monthly-view audience that wants the fastest, clearest next step from selfie content and AI transformation reels.

Its job is not to explain the whole SSELFIE ecosystem.

Its job is:

- turn viral attention into more low-ticket buyers
- help women get one clear selfie and use it for AI photos
- create a cleaner bridge into the Vault and SUITE
- give Sandra a concrete product to sell from selfie tutorials, AI reels, and ManyChat

### 2. Warm-Audience Offer

Create the immediate warm-audience offer:

> Visibility To Paid Sprint

This is for women who already feel the deeper problem:

- they have skills, a story, a service, expertise, or lived experience
- they want to build online
- they do not know what to say
- they do not know what to sell first
- they do not know how to make their profile, visuals, and content make sense

This should not be positioned as generic business coaching.

It should be:

> I help you take what you already have and make it visible, clear, and easy for people to understand why they should work with you.

### 3. Recurring Core

Make SUITE / Studio the monthly creation system after the first result.

SUITE should not be sold only as AI image access.

It should be framed as:

> The monthly workspace where you keep creating visuals, captions, content ideas, and brand assets that help people recognize, trust, and remember you.

### 4. Keep Prompt Vault In Its Correct Role

Prompt Vault works.

It should stay as a low-ticket buyer activation product.

But it should not be expected to carry the whole business.

Its job is to turn interest into buyers and move those buyers toward SUITE or the warm-audience offer.

### 5. Stop Leading With Selfie To Brand Shoot As A Standalone Sale

Selfie To Brand Shoot has strategic value, but the live data does not support making it the primary public push right now.

Use it as:

- a bonus
- an onboarding path
- a guided support asset inside SUITE
- a future proof-backed launch when we have stronger case studies

Do not make it the main paid CTA until the data changes.

## Why This Is The Right Direction

### Data Support

From the latest revenue truth audit:

- Prompt Vault is the strongest one-time buyer activator.
- SUITE is the real recurring revenue engine, but activation needs work.
- Selfie To Brand Shoot had checkout interest but no completed sales in the measured window.
- Work With Me demand was not being persisted in the database until now, so the next stage needs a measurable human sales process.

From Instagram:

- The strongest public content is selfie and AI transformation content.
- Saves and shares are strongest when the viewer gets a simple, practical photo result.
- Comment keywords show cold demand around SELFIE, PROMPT, and KIT.

From older story and poll signals:

- The warm audience is asking about money, starting online, confidence, time, overwhelm, and what to build.
- That is not a different business. It is the next layer after the photo gets her attention.

## Offer 2: Visibility To Paid Sprint

### Purpose

Create the immediate warm-audience offer for women who are already asking for deeper help.

This is not for cold scrollers who only want a better selfie.

This is for the woman who has started to realize:

> I do not just need better photos. I need to know how to show up, what to say, and what I can sell.

### Buyer

She has one or more of these:

- a service
- expertise
- a business idea
- a story
- life experience
- a skill people ask her about
- a small existing audience
- offline credibility but weak online presence
- a desire to make money online but no clear first offer

She is stuck because:

- her profile does not explain what she does
- her content feels random
- she does not know what to say
- she does not know what people would pay her for
- she feels invisible online
- she does not have time to figure it out alone

### Promise

Make what you already have visible, clear, and easy for people to understand so you can start creating trust, content, and a first paid path online.

### Format Options

Pick one simple first version.

Recommended first version:

> Small-group beta sprint

Price options:

- $197 for a low-friction beta
- $497 for a more serious small group
- $2,000 private version only for qualified applicants

Do not start with a large complex program.

### Deliverables

Minimum valuable sprint:

1. Your simple "what I help with" sentence.
2. Your buyer and before/after.
3. Your first offer idea.
4. Your profile direction.
5. Your content pillars.
6. Your first 7 posts or story ideas.
7. Your selfie/AI visual direction.
8. Your simple CTA and next step.

### What It Should Not Be

Do not make it:

- generic business coaching
- a big course
- a manifestation program
- an Instagram growth hack
- a broad "build online" promise with no specific output
- a passive payment-link flow

### Sales Path

Warm content path:

1. Stories and emails about starting online from what she already has.
2. Ask:
   - "Do you already have a skill, service, idea, or story, but no clear way to show it online?"
3. CTA:
   - Apply or reply WORK.
4. Human sales flow:
   - personal reply
   - short fit call
   - clear diagnosis
   - payment link only after fit is clear
   - 3-touch follow-up

## Final Operating Rule

From this point forward, every SSELFIE funnel decision should ask:

> Is this for cold attention, warm trust, or paid activation?

If the answer is unclear, the offer will feel unclear.

Cold iPhone/selfie attention gets the Selfie Starter Kit.

Cold AI-photo attention gets PROMPT and the planned Selfie To AI Photos Kit path.

Warm trust gets Visibility To Paid.

Paid activation gets SUITE.

Prompt Vault stays the proven low-ticket buyer bridge.
