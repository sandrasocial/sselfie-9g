# One Selfie, One Week of Content — the outcome pivot and its test

Status: DECISION CONTRACT. Drafted 2026-07-14 during the One Selfie event, from two independent
audits (Claude 2026-07-13, Codex 2026-07-14) that converged on the same diagnosis. Sandra approves
before build. Supersedes leak-patching as the operating focus. The Brand Constitution still governs
all copy and ethics.

## The diagnosis both audits agree on

A year of evidence, one sentence: SSELFIE sells access to a machine (subscription, courses,
prompts) to an audience recruited by free-result content, when the proven demand — internal and
market-wide — is for a finished, identity-safe outcome bought once.

Evidence (verified, sources in parentheses):

- Every offer at every price converts at roughly a tenth of normal warm-list rates: money-ask email
  clicks 0.7-1.8% vs free clicks 17-34% (funnel truth memory); Vault ~1% capture-to-purchase;
  SUITE trial 0/50 ever converted; Masterclass ~never sold; Founding Annual sold 1; Work With Me 0
  applications (audits 2026-06/07).
- What does sell is one-time and outcome-shaped: Vault 29 payments / $873 in 30d (Codex),
  June flash 18 sales/$496 with 56% on deadline day.
- Paying members mostly do not operate the tool: 3 of 9 never generated; cancelled members' median
  stay 61 days; 0 of 13 mature trial creators returned days 8-14 (Codex). The job completes; the
  subscription doesn't.
- Market demand for the outcome is proven externally: InstaHeadshots ~300-400K customers,
  PhotoAI ~$150K/mo, commodity price $29-59 one-time (ads research 2026-07-13). The unowned
  position: identity-safe ("still you") full personal-brand content for women 35+, above the
  commodity headshot.
- Costs: ~1,414 images/30d ≈ $85 API (Codex). The $1,000-1,500/mo burn is tools/subscriptions →
  invoice-level audit required.
- One Selfie event (as of 2026-07-14 afternoon): ~40 real landing views, 2 checkout starts,
  0 sales. At the 2-3% target, 40 views predicts ~1 sale. Verdict available only on traffic, not
  on product. Final read after close.

## The product to test (next commercial experiment after the event closes)

**One Selfie, One Week of Content** — an automated, done-FOR-you outcome. One-time purchase.

Customer provides: one selfie, what she sells, what she wants to promote this week.

She receives, DELIVERED (email + buyer page), within **48 hours**:

1. A clear visual direction (her world, chosen by Maya).
2. A finished set of brand photos (still-you guaranteed).
3. Seven content ideas mapped to her offer.
4. Captions/talking points and calls to action connected to what she sells.
5. One recommended first post.

Price to test: **$97-129 once.** Not access. Not credits. Not "log in and create." The delivery
must be complete enough that a non-technical buyer posts the first piece the day it arrives.

Hard product laws:

- DONE-FOR-YOU IS THE LINE. If the buyer must learn or operate anything to get the deliverable,
  the test is invalid — that rebuilds the tool with a new label, which is the exact failure mode
  this pivot exists to end. Human QA pass on every order at first (10 min/order, also trains the
  likeness quality loop).
- **48-hour delivery promise** and **still-you guarantee** ("it looks like you or I fix it") are
  part of the offer. These are the differentiators vs the $29-59 commodity and the synthetic wave.
- SUITE is repositioned as the continuation for repeat buyers ("want this every month?"), offered
  only after a delivered result. It stops being the front-door ask.
- Prompt Vault stays the proven $37 self-serve entry, but the outcome product is also sold
  directly; the ladder is not mandatory.

## Qualified traffic is part of the test (the gap in the Codex plan)

The current content engine recruits free-prompt collectors. Running the test on that traffic
measures the wrong audience and will produce a false negative. The test ships with its own content
plan: hooks addressed to women who SELL something ("you have an offer and no photos of yourself",
"your website still has no picture of you", "she posted once and got a client"), Stories carrying
the warm sell, feed staying cold-reach. Buyer-intent qualification question at checkout or delivery
("what do you sell?") — required for fulfillment anyway, doubles as segment truth.

## Gates (Codex's, adopted — they separate marketing problems from product problems)

Over a 14-day test window:

- ≥200 qualified landing visitors (tracked, post-fix — see below).
- ≥10% checkout start.
- ≥2-3% purchase (4-6+ buyers minimum signal; interpret small samples with humility).
- ≥50% open/download their delivery; ≥25% buy or request a second week within days 8-14.
- Refunds <5%.

Readings: fewer than ~4 buyers from 200 qualified visitors → change promise or segment. Buyers who
don't return → change the product. Buyers who return → scale it, then earn the right to make it
recurring.

## Preconditions (Thursday 2026-07-16, before any build)

1. **Event postmortem** with final numbers (traffic by source, starts, sales, ManyChat BUNDLE
   volume, Stories link taps). One honest page; no quiet repeat without learning.
2. **Fix click attribution**: campaign email recorded 99 clicks vs 23-24 attributed landing
   visits. Determine Apple-prefetch inflation vs UTM/redirect loss. No conversion conclusions are
   trustworthy until this is explained.
3. **Invoice-level expense audit**: cancel anything not directly supporting payments, customer
   access, Maya's essential experience, email delivery, analytics, or proven acquisition.
4. Codex implementation spec written from this contract (fulfillment pipeline = existing Maya /
   Shoot Studio machinery + delivery email + buyer page + QA queue).

## The stop-list (30 days, from the Codex audit — adopted verbatim in spirit)

No new bundles from existing courses. No new broad "visibility system". No 1:1 as the revenue
strategy. No new Maya features without proven repeat use. No expensive recurring offers to cold
audiences. No ads until: tracking fixed, one offer has 20+ organic buyers, page-to-purchase ≥2-3%
sustained, fulfillment cost + refunds known — then retargeting first at $10-20/day. No describing
SSELFIE as an income system before income outcomes are measurable.

## What is protected

Prompt Vault ($37 entry, proven). The "one selfie" starting point. Sandra's story, taste, and the
No-Fake/still-you position (the moat). Maya — pointed at finished outcomes, not more capabilities.
The Brand Constitution and honesty guardrails: real deadline, real proof, no income promises.
