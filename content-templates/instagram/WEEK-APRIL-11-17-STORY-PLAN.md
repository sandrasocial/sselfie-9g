## THE FRAMEWORK: "SOAP OPERA SEQUENCE" � STORYBRAND

This week uses two proven storytelling systems layered together:

### FRAMEWORK 1: StoryBrand (Donald Miller)

Your follower is the HERO. You are the GUIDE.

- The hero has a **problem** (she can't show up consistently online, feels invisible, no professional photos)
- She meets the **guide** (you � who has been exactly where she is)
- The guide gives her a **plan** (SSELFIE + your Selfie Guide)
- She **takes action** (buys / signs up)
- She **avoids failure** (staying invisible, losing opportunities)
- She **achieves success** (visible, consistent, professional, growing)

**Every story slide should be: her problem ? your proof ? the path forward.**

---

### FRAMEWORK 2: The Soap Opera Sequence

Each day is a chapter. Each chapter ends with a reason to come back tomorrow.
The emotional arc across the week:

```
Saturday  ? THE HOOK (create curiosity + relatability)
Sunday    ? THE STRUGGLE (vulnerability builds trust)
Monday    ? THE TURNING POINT (the discovery that changed everything)
Tuesday   ? THE PROOF (social proof � show it working)
Wednesday ? THE OFFER (soft sell, value-first)
Thursday  ? THE URGENCY (FOMO + deadline energy)
Friday    ? THE WIN (transformation story + close)
```

---

### FRAMEWORK 3: Per-Slide Structure (AIDA)

Each individual story slide uses:

- **A** = Attention (first slide: hook, bold statement, question, or confession)
- **I** = Interest (build the story, make her nod along)
- **D** = Desire (show the transformation / outcome)
- **A** = Action (link sticker, DM trigger, swipe-up CTA)
