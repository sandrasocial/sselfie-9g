## WHAT'S WORKING ?

### 1. The 7-day arc is genuinely elite
The Soap Opera Sequence across 7 days is exactly what the research validates. Each day builds on the last and makes viewers come back. This is not common � most creators post randomly. You have a system.

### 2. DM-first strategy is correct
1 in 5 stories gets a DM. Story replies are the highest-intent signal on Instagram. The plan already leans into this � we just needed to automate it properly with ManyChat keywords.

### 3. V3 copy is doing the right things
Micro-confessions, scenes, unfinished thoughts � this is exactly what drives replies. The research confirms: "She's saying what I haven't been able to put into words" = the conversion trigger.

### 4. One CTA per day
Correct. Research confirms: multiple CTAs per session confuse viewers. One clear ask per day is the right approach.

### FIX 2: Slide Count � 5 ? 7 per day
**The problem:** Research shows reach peaks between slides 6�13. At 5 slides/day, you're leaving up to 6� potential reach on the table.

**The fix:** Add 2 slides per day:
1. A **"messy moment" slide** (already in the V2 list � now make it mandatory)
