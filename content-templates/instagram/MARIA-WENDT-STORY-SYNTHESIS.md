## WHAT MARIA WENDT ACTUALLY TEACHES

From her courses, templates, and public content — here is the complete distilled framework:

### THE CORE PRINCIPLE
> "Never nurture on Instagram Stories. Every story session should have a subtle sales intention."

This is the single biggest shift. Most creators use stories to warm people up for weeks before selling. Maria Wendt says this is wrong. Instead:
- Every session has **at least one subtle pitch**
- You don't need permission to sell — you already have their attention because they **chose** to watch your stories
- Story viewers are already pre-sold — they know you, they like you, they clicked on your face

---

### THE 5 MARIA WENDT STORY TYPES

These are her named story formats from the Viral Content Treasure Chest:

#### TYPE 1 — RESOURCE PITCH
**What it is:** Give a free resource (guide, tip, checklist, template) and ask people to comment/DM a keyword to receive it.
**What it does:** Creates a DM conversation instantly. Warms them into the funnel. The resource builds trust AND positions you as the expert.
**How to use it:** "I made a free [thing] — reply SSELFIE and I'll send it straight to your DMs."
**When to use:** Day 1, Day 3, Day 7 — when you want to build the list and start conversations.

#### TYPE 2 — SUBTLE BRAGGING
**What it is:** Casually show a result, a DM, a milestone, or a win — without framing it as a testimonial ad.
**What it does:** Social proof without sounding like you're selling. Builds desire by showing what's possible.
**How to use it:** "Got this DM today ??" + screenshot. Or: "just hit [milestone]… not gonna make a big thing of it but wanted to share."
**When to use:** Day 2, Day 4, Day 6 — especially on vulnerability/struggle days to add credibility.

#### TYPE 3 — INSANE VALUE
**What it is:** Give your absolute best tip for FREE. The kind of tip that makes people think "wait, she just gave that away?"
**What it does:** Positions you as THE expert. Makes the paid product feel like a no-brainer ("if the free stuff is this good…")
**How to use it:** One specific, actionable tip. Show the result. Don't hold back.
**When to use:** Day 3, Day 5 — on value/turning point days.

#### TYPE 4 — WORK WITH ME PITCH
**What it is:** A direct, clear, unapologetic offer. "Here's what I have. Here's who it's for. Here's how to get it."
**What it does:** Converts the people who were already warm. No dancing around it.
**How to use it:** Short, clear, name the product, state the outcome, give the CTA.
**When to use:** Day 5 (main sales day), Day 7 (closing week).

#### TYPE 5 — SOCIAL PROOF STORY
**What it is:** A DM screenshot, a before/after, a client win — shown as a story, not a testimonial ad.
**What it does:** Builds trust through others' results. The viewer sees herself in the client story.
**How to use it:** "She was exactly where you are 3 weeks ago." + result.
**When to use:** Day 4 (The Proof day), but also pepper into Day 2, Day 6.

---

### HOW MARIA SEQUENCES THESE

She doesn't have a rigid weekly order — she runs these types **within each story session**, typically in this order:

```
Within each session:
1. Story/Hook (emotional, personal, relatable)
2. Value OR Proof (builds trust)
3. Subtle Pitch (Resource Pitch or Work With Me — always present)
```

The key: **the pitch is ALWAYS there.** Even on vulnerability days. Even on story days.
She just keeps it subtle enough that it doesn't feel jarring.

## THE COMBINED FRAMEWORK

Merge the emotional arc WITH Maria's "always be pitching subtly" principle.

**The Rule:** Keep the Soap Opera arc for the first 4–5 slides. Add a Maria Wendt Type slide as the FINAL slide of each day's session.

```
SESSION STRUCTURE (every day):
Slide 1: HOOK (Soap Opera — emotional, personal)
Slide 2: STORY (Soap Opera — scene, confession, vulnerability)  
Slide 3: INSIGHT (Soap Opera — the realization)
Slide 4: ENGAGEMENT (Poll / Question sticker)
Slide 5: STORY CONTINUATION (Soap Opera — next beat)
Slide 6: WENDT SLIDE (Resource Pitch / Subtle Brag / Social Proof)  ? NEW
Slide 7: CLOSE/TEASE (Soap Opera — come back tomorrow)  ? NOW SLIDE 7
```

This gets you to 7 slides (in the research sweet spot) AND adds the Maria Wendt pitch layer every day.

## THE WENDT RULE (print this out)

> **Before posting each story session, ask:**
> "Where is the pitch in today's session?"
>
> If you can't find it — add a Resource Pitch slide at the end.
> It doesn't have to be a big pitch. It just has to be there.
>
> Viewers who want to buy will act on it.
> Viewers who aren't ready will ignore it.
> Neither group is harmed by it being there.
