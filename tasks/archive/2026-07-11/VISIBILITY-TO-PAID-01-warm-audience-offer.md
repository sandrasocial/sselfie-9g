## Goal

Create the immediate warm-audience offer for women who are past the "better selfie" moment and are asking for the deeper path:

> I want to build online, but I do not know what to post, what to say, what I can sell, or how to make people understand me.

## Strategic Role

This is the offer for warm trust, not cold attention.

It should use the existing Work With Me path first.

Do not create a separate complicated funnel unless the current Work With Me infrastructure cannot support the offer.

## Offer Name

Recommended:

> Visibility To Paid Sprint

## Buyer

She has something real:

- a skill
- a service
- a business idea
- expertise
- a story
- lived experience
- offline credibility
- a small audience

But online, it feels unclear.

She is thinking:

- "I do not know what to post."
- "I do not know what to say."
- "I do not know what I can sell."
- "My profile does not explain me."
- "I have something to offer, but people cannot see it yet."
- "I need someone to make this simple."

## Promise

Make what she already has visible, clear, and easy for people to understand so she can start creating trust, content, and a first paid path online.

## Recommended Format

Start with one simple version.

Recommended:

> Small-group beta sprint

Possible price points:

- $197 low-friction beta
- $497 serious small group
- $2,000 private version for qualified applicants

Do not begin with a giant course.

## Deliverables

The sprint should help her leave with:

1. A simple "what I help with" sentence.
2. Her buyer and before/after.
3. A first offer idea.
4. Profile direction.
5. Content pillars.
6. First 7 posts or story ideas.
7. Selfie/AI visual direction.
8. A simple CTA and next step.

## Sales Process

This must not be a passive payment-link flow.

Use:

1. Application or reply WORK.
2. Personal reply from Sandra.
3. Optional Loom or voice note.
4. 15-minute fit call.
5. Clear diagnosis.
6. Payment link only after fit is clear.
7. 3-touch follow-up.

## Existing Infrastructure To Use

Start with:

- existing Work With Me page
- existing Work With Me form/application
- `brand_engine_applications` persistence
- admin lead scoring
- application confirmation email

Do not build a second application system.
