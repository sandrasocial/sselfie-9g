Before You Start
I used to think I needed a photographer, a studio, perfect lighting, and two hours I didn't have — just to get a decent photo for Instagram.

Then I started using AI. And I realised the photos I was getting from my phone and a few specific words were better than anything I'd ever paid €300 for.

The problem isn't your phone. The problem isn't your face. The problem is that most people just type "nice photo" and wonder why it looks generic.

The prompt is everything. The right words tell SSELFIE exactly what kind of woman to show — her vibe, her environment, her energy, what she's wearing, and how the light falls.

These 50 prompts are the ones that actually work. Across 10 different brand scenarios, they'll give you a full month of content from a single selfie upload.

Your phone is enough. You just needed the right words.

— Sandra
How To Use This
Step 01 — Choose Your Scenario

Pick the scenario that matches what you need this week. Morning routine, workspace, outfit, event — there's a scenario for every type of content.

Step 02 — Open SSELFIE Studio

Go to the Maya tab. Upload one clear selfie. Paste the prompt. Hit generate.

Step 03 — Customise If You Want

Add your brand colours, location, or mood to any prompt. The words in [brackets] are the ones worth personalising.

01
Scenario One

Morning
Routine

The "morning content" slot is one of the highest-performing for women entrepreneurs. It signals discipline, intention, and realness — before the rest of the world is online.

  • Prompt 01 A woman sitting at a clean white marble desk with a [gold/rose] coffee mug and a journal open beside her laptop. Soft morning light through sheer curtains. Minimal, intentional, editorial. Personal brand portrait. Copy
  • Prompt 02 Close-up portrait of a woman in a white bathrobe, holding a ceramic cup, looking out a bright window. Peaceful, premium, morning ritual energy. Soft bokeh background, natural skin tone. Copy
  • Prompt 03 A woman in a [neutral linen / soft grey] outfit sitting cross-legged on a bed with a notebook. Warm morning light, fresh flowers in the background. Calm, focused, intentional energy. Copy
  • Prompt 04 Editorial portrait of a woman standing at a kitchen counter, espresso machine beside her, golden hour light. She's dressed simply but stylishly. Lifestyle brand photo, magazine quality. Copy
  • Prompt 05 A woman in a oversized cream sweater, hair natural, outdoors on a terrace with city view and morning coffee. Authentic, unfiltered morning vibe with premium editorial framing. Copy
Sandra's Tip

For morning content, keep the background simple. Your face should be the focus. A blurred background and soft light beats a cluttered "aesthetic" every time.

  • Prompt 10 A woman standing at a standing desk by a large window, reading notes. Minimal office setup, evening golden light. Professional brand portrait with architectural background. Copy
03
Scenario Three

Brand
Statement

These prompts are about one thing: presence. You don't need a context or a story. Just you, looking like you mean business.

  • Prompt 11 A confident woman in a [black blazer / tailored jacket], standing against a clean white wall. Direct eye contact. Professional headshot with personality — not corporate, but commanding. Copy
  • Prompt 12 Editorial portrait of a woman in a sophisticated [cream / beige / stone] outfit, minimal accessories, neutral background. Magazine cover energy. Clean, precise, elevated. Copy
  • Prompt 13 A woman sitting on the edge of a marble counter, one leg crossed, looking directly at the camera. Confident posture. Luxury brand editorial. Studio lighting, sharp focus. Copy
  • Prompt 14 Close-up portrait, direct eye contact, natural light from the left. No distracting background. Just her face, her skin, her story. Raw, real, and completely her own brand. Copy
  • Prompt 15 A woman in an oversized [white shirt / structured blazer] standing in the centre of an empty, minimalist room. Wide shot. Power stance. This is what "I built something" looks like. Copy
04
Scenario Four

Behind The
Scenes

People buy from people they trust. Behind the scenes content builds that trust faster than any polished post ever will.

  • Prompt 16 A woman at a messy creative desk — notes, mood board, open laptop — looking up mid-thought. Candid energy, warm light. "I'm in the middle of building something" aesthetic. Copy
  • Prompt 17 A woman packing orders or organising products, hair up, casual outfit, focused. Behind-the-scenes brand content. Real, relatable, energetic. Shallow depth of field. Copy
  • Prompt 18 Portrait of a woman on a video call, screen glow, notebook open, headphones around her neck. Working with clients from home. Real and polished at the same time. Copy
  • Prompt 19 A woman reviewing a printed document at her desk, pen in hand, glasses if applicable. Detailed work energy. The business version of "the devil is in the detail." Clean, focused. Copy
  • Prompt 20 A woman walking through a busy market / city street with a tote bag and phone in hand. Mid-movement, candid, energetic. Shows she has a life outside her screen. Vibrant and real. Copy
05
Scenario Five

Lifestyle &
Personal

People follow brands. They stay for people. These prompts show who you are when the laptop is closed.

  • Prompt 21 A woman in a park or green outdoor space, natural light, relaxed expression. Wearing [casual but intentional outfit]. Lifestyle content that says: "I take care of myself." Clean and genuine. Copy
  • Prompt 22 A woman at a restaurant table, glass of wine or sparkling water, laughing or looking relaxed. The luxury of time and good food. Feminine, elegant, real. Not staged. Editorial spontaneity. Copy
  • Prompt 23 A woman reading a book on a couch, natural afternoon light, throw blanket, a cup of tea nearby. Calm domestic scene. "I protect my energy" energy. Warm tones, soft focus. Copy
  • Prompt 24 Portrait of a woman at the gym or in activewear — confident, post-workout, hair up. Not "fitness content." The message is: "I show up for myself the same way I show up for my business." Copy
  • Prompt 25 A woman walking on a beach, coastal or lakeside setting, looking ahead. Natural light, movement, freedom. The visual equivalent of: "I built this so I could have this." Wide shot, filmic. Copy

  • Prompt 29 Portrait of a woman at a conference or event — badge visible, professional outfit, a crowd or venue background blurred. "She shows up in the room" energy. Aspirational, credible, real. Copy
  • Prompt 30 A woman looking directly at camera with a knowing half-smile — the look of someone who has figured something out and is about to share it. This is the "caption says it all" visual. Close-up, direct. Copy

Scenario Seven

Outfit &
Style

You don't need to be a fashion account to use outfit content. Getting dressed with intention is a message. "I care how I show up" is always on-brand.

  • Prompt 31 Full-length portrait of a woman in a [trench coat / tailored trousers / clean minimal outfit] on a city street. Natural light, slight movement. Editorial fashion meets personal brand. Her style is her brand. Copy
  • Prompt 32 A woman in a [bold colour / pattern] outfit that makes her the only thing in the frame. Clean background — white wall, neutral street. The outfit speaks. Confident, intentional, on-brand. Copy
  • Prompt 33 Close-up of a woman's neck, collarbone, and jewellery. Soft natural light. The details tell the story. Premium, personal, feminine. This is what product detail photography looks like when the product is you. Copy
  • Prompt 34 A woman sitting on steps, looking away from camera. Three-quarter shot. Casual but considered outfit, natural environment. Thoughtful, introspective, quietly powerful energy. Copy
  • Prompt 35 A woman in her walk-in wardrobe or getting-ready space, mirror reflection, beautiful natural morning light. Getting ready content. The ritual of dressing as an act of self-respect. Copy
08
Scenario Eight

Results &
Milestones

Social proof doesn't have to be a screenshot. The right photo shows people that something good is happening. Pair these with your wins.

  • Prompt 36 A woman with a laptop open, looking at the screen with a surprised and delighted expression — the "just got great news" face. Real, spontaneous energy. Caption: "She just checked her metrics." Copy
  • Prompt 37 Portrait of a woman toasting with champagne or juice, laughing, celebratory. "We're celebrating something" energy. Warm light, blurred background, joy. The visual that goes with a win announcement. Copy
  • Prompt 38 A woman writing in a journal, pen moving, deep in reflection. Journaling as a business practice. Intentional. "I track my growth" energy. Peaceful, focused, morning light. Copy
  • Prompt 39 A woman standing outside a nice restaurant, hotel, or venue, dressed up and smiling. "Client dinner" or "sold-out event" energy. Subtle luxury, real celebration. Soft evening light. Copy
  • Prompt 40 Close-up of a woman's face — genuine, calm, clear-eyed. The look of someone who has been through something hard and come out the other side. No big smile needed. Just: "I made it." Copy
09
Scenario Nine

Story &
Vulnerability

The photos that make people stop are the ones where something real is happening. These prompts create the visual space for your most honest captions.

  • Prompt 41 A woman sitting alone at a window, looking out, thoughtful. Soft rain or grey light outside. Interior warmth. "I've been thinking about something" energy. For your most reflective posts. Copy
  • Prompt 42 A woman curled up on a couch with a blanket, phone in hand, looking tired but okay. The "I had a hard week and I'm recovering" photo. Real. Relatable. This is the post that gets the most DMs. Copy
  • Prompt 43 Portrait of a woman mid-laugh — not posed, not perfect. Genuine joy, messy happiness. This is the photo that reminds people you're a human, not a brand account. Use it sparingly. It lands hard. Copy
  • Prompt 44 A woman standing at a crossroads, looking left and right, or walking away from the camera down a long path. The "I was at a turning point" visual. Used for transformation stories, before/afters, pivots. Copy
  • Prompt 45 Close-up portrait, no makeup or very minimal, natural light, direct gaze. The "this is actually me" photo. It takes confidence to post this. That confidence is the whole point. Copy

Scenario Ten

Seasonal &
Timely

Tying your content to a season, month, or moment keeps it feeling relevant. These prompts are built to be adapted to whatever is happening right now.

  • Prompt 46 A woman in an autumn / fall setting — warm tones, leaves, a coffee cup. Soft golden light. "New season energy" for business planning posts, quarterly reflection, or fresh starts. Copy
  • Prompt 47 A woman in a bright summer setting — sun, pool, terrace — working or relaxing. "This is my life in [month]" energy. Shows freedom, flexibility, the life that the business funds. Copy
  • Prompt 48 A woman in a cosy winter setting — fireplace, heavy knit, candlelight, warm interior. "The world slows down but I keep going" energy. For December content: reflection, goals, quiet power. Copy
  • Prompt 49 A woman outside in spring bloom — light jacket, flowers in background, fresh colours. "New beginnings" energy. First quarter goals, spring launches, feeling of momentum returning. Copy
  • Prompt 50 A woman at a desk with a calendar, planner, or goal board visible. New Year planning energy. "Here's what I'm building" post. Clear, confident, directional. The photo that says: "I have a plan." Copy
