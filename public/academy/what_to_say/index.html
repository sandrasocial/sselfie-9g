A Note Before You Start
"I don't know what to say."

Those five words have cost you more than you probably realise. Not money. Time. Visibility. The opportunities that sit just beyond where people can actually see you.

I spent eight months staring at a blank caption box. Not because I couldn't write. But because I genuinely didn't believe I had the right to take up that space. I wasn't polished enough. I wasn't certified enough. I wasn't "influencer enough." And every time I thought about posting, my brain would just... freeze.

What changed wasn't that I suddenly became more qualified. It's that I stopped waiting for permission from someone else and gave it to myself.

This workbook isn't going to teach you marketing formulas or viral hacks. You don't need another framework. What you need is clarity on who you are, what you actually believe, and what that sounds like when you say it out loud.

And then you need proof that people want to hear it from you.

This is that proof machine.

— Sandra
How This Works

01
Exercise One

Who Are
You For?

You can't know what to say until you know who you're saying it to. Not their age or job title — the woman lying awake at night, thinking about her life. The more real she is in your mind, the more real your words become.

Question 01 — Who Is Your One Person?

Forget demographics. Describe a real woman. What's her situation right now? Is she newly separated? Building something for the first time? Scared of being visible? Tired of trying to be perfect?

Question 02 — What Does She Tell Herself?

What's the story running in her head when she feels stuck? The exact self-talk you know she's wrestling with — because you've wrestled with it too.

Question 03 — What's The 90-Day Transformation?

If she works with you, what becomes true for her in three months? Not a vague promise — a concrete shift.

Question 04 — Why Are YOU The Right Person?

Not because of credentials. Because of your story. What have you lived through that makes you the person who can help her?

Exercise Three

Your Positioning
Statement

One clear sentence about what you do. When you have this, you stop second-guessing every caption. You know immediately what to post and what to skip. People who need exactly what you offer find you way faster.

The Formula

I help [WHO] do / get / become [WHAT] even if / without [OBJECTION].

"I help women who feel invisible online build a personal brand that actually makes money, even without a big following or fancy equipment."

"I help divorced mums rebuild their confidence and their income by teaching them how to show up authentically on social media."

"I help solopreneurs who are tired of copying other people's strategies create a business model that actually fits their life and values."

do / get
even without
Your Positioning Statement

Read it out loud. Does it sound like something you'd actually say? If it feels stiff or salesy, rewrite it in your own words. This is your North Star for everything you post from now on.

Your System

The 5-Day
Rotation

Five days. Four buckets. Repeat forever. You'll never stare at a blank screen again.

M Monday — Story

Prompt: What Made You Start

Post about the moment you decided to build something. Not the perfect origin story. The real one. What were you running from? What were you running towards?

"My marriage ended on a Tuesday in November. By Thursday, I knew I had to build something that would let me control my own income. Not because I was angry. Because I was terrified. And terror is a really good motivator."

T Tuesday — Teach

Prompt: The One Thing Everyone Gets Wrong

Share one piece of advice that contradicts what everyone else is saying. What do most people get wrong? What have you learned the hard way?

"Everyone tells you to post every day. But here's what I've learned: it's not the frequency that matters. It's the clarity. One thoughtful post a week will outperform five posts where you're just filling space."

W Wednesday — Connect

Prompt: Ask Your People Something Real

Ask a question that makes them actually think. Not "What's your biggest goal?" — ask something that makes them reveal a struggle, a fear, an honest answer. Then reply to the comments.

"Real question: how many times this week did you almost post something but didn't because you were scared it would be judged? I think we're all living smaller than we have to."

T Thursday — Teach (Part 2)

Prompt: Show Them How You Do It

Walk through your actual process. Not the polished version. Show them something you're working on. Tell them what doesn't work. Give them the real, unfiltered version.

"Here's the inside of my writing process for captions: I write 10 versions. Nine are too salesy or too stiff. I delete all of them and go back to what I was thinking at 6am. That one's usually the one that works."

F Friday — Sell

Prompt: The Open Door

Make an invitation to your offer, program, or product. Not pushy. Just: this exists, here's what it does, here's who it's for, here's how to get it.

"The next Brand Engine cohort starts March 16. This is for women who are ready to stop being invisible and start making actual money. If that's you, the link is in my bio."
