SELFIE TO BRAND SHOOT
7-Day Content Plan

Use this after Module 5. The goal is not to make more images. The goal is to give your best images a job.

DAY 1 - UPDATE PROFILE IMAGE
Use the clearest identity image first.
Caption starter:
New visual chapter. I wanted my profile to feel more like the woman I am becoming, so I started with one clear image.
Action:
Upload the image, check the crop, and make sure your face is recognizable in the small circle.

DAY 2 - STORY INTRO
Share the source selfie, the result, and why this look feels like you.
Caption starter:
I started with a normal selfie and built this visual world from it. The goal was not perfect. The goal was recognizable.
Action:
Post 3-5 story frames: selfie, result, what changed, why it fits, and one reply keyword or link.

DAY 3 - REEL COVER
Post a simple transformation reel with one strong cover line.
Caption starter:
One selfie can carry more brand direction than you think when the style, light, and mood are clear.
Action:
Use the editorial cover image and keep the overlay to one short line.

DAY 4 - CAROUSEL
Teach the before, visual world, prompt, result, and what you kept.
Caption starter:
The difference was not just the prompt. It was choosing one visual world before creating the image.
Action:
Build 5 slides: before, visual world, prompt direction, result, and what you would repeat.

DAY 5 - OFFER IMAGE
Use one confident visual to point to your guide, Vault, offer, or service.
Caption starter:
If your visuals are starting to feel random, start with one world you can repeat. That is what makes the brand feel clear.
Action:
Pair the strongest authority image with one CTA. Do not explain everything.

DAY 6 - ABOUT-ME POST
Tell the story behind the woman your visuals are starting to show.
Caption starter:
For a long time I thought I needed a studio, a photographer, and perfect timing. I actually needed a clearer visual direction.
Action:
Use a softer identity image and write 5-7 lines about what this new visual chapter represents.

DAY 7 - FEED REFRESH
Pin or place the strongest images so the profile starts to feel cohesive.
Caption starter:
Your feed does not need to be perfect. But it should feel like the same woman lives there.
Action:
Choose your top 3 images and decide which one becomes profile, which one becomes cover, and which one becomes story proof.

FINAL CHECK
- I chose one profile / identity image.
- I chose one reel or carousel cover image.
- I chose one lifestyle or story image.
- I know which image points to my offer, guide, or next step.
- I have one 7-day posting plan instead of a folder of random images.
- My first week repeats the same visual world.
