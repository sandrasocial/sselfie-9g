SELFIE TO BRAND SHOOT
Course Workbook

Use this as you move through the course. You do not need perfect answers.
You need a clear starting direction you can repeat.

The goal:
Turn one clear selfie into your first AI brand shoot, then use those images to start showing up online with a more recognizable personal brand.


MODULE 1 - START WITH ONE SELFIE

My source selfie:

Why this is the photo I am starting with:

Source selfie check:
- My face is clear and sharp.
- My full face is visible.
- My eyes, nose, mouth, jawline, and hairline are not hidden.
- The photo is well-lit.
- The photo is not blurry.
- The photo is not overly filtered.
- I am the only person in the image.
- My face is not too far away.
- My head is not cropped.
- My face is not covered by sunglasses, hair, hands, or phone.
- My expression feels natural.
- My skin tone and hair color look close to real life.

Sandra's rule:
Clear beats pretty. AI can style you beautifully later, but it needs to understand you first.
