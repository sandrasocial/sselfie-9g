## Product Overview

**One-liner:** SSELFIE is the AI personal branding platform that knows your face, your voice, and your brand — so it can create professional photos, plan your feed, and write your captions on autopilot.

**What it does:** Users upload selfies, train a custom AI model on their face, and then generate unlimited professional brand photos without a photographer or studio. Maya, the AI, also plans their Instagram feed and writes captions in their voice. Everything runs inside one chat interface.

**Product category:** AI photo generation / personal branding SaaS. Customers search for "AI headshots," "AI brand photos," "Instagram content creation AI," "personal branding tools."

**Product type:** SaaS, subscription + one-time products

**Business model:**
- Studio membership: €97/month (200 credits, full Maya access, model training)
- Selfie Guide: €17 one-time (interactive course + AI access)
- Selfie Guide Bundle: €27 one-time
- Brand Strategy Pack: $19 one-time (AI-generated brand strategy)
- Feed Planner (Blueprint): ~$47 one-time (9-post grid + captions)
- Credit top-ups: available for free-tier users

**Current MRR:** ~€1,509 (15 Studio × €97)
**Target MRR:** €10,000
**Gap:** ~72 additional Studio subscribers needed (or equivalent mix)

## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem:** Personal brand builders need professional-looking photos and consistent content constantly, but photographers are expensive (€500–2,000 per shoot), slow (2–3 week turnaround), and don't understand their brand.

**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Canva / stock photos: Generic, not YOUR face, not your brand
- Expensive AI tools (Midjourney etc.): Too technical, don't know your face
- Real photographers: Expensive, slow, infrequent, can't iterate daily
- Hiring a VA/social media manager: €500–1,500/month and still needs photos

**What it costs them:**
- Time: 5–10 hours/week on content if done manually
- Money: €500–2,000/shoot × 6–12 shoots/year = €3,000–24,000/year
- Opportunity: Inconsistent posting = suppressed reach = slower growth

**Emotional tension:** "I know I need to show up more but I don't have time / money / energy to get a shoot done every time I need a new photo. I feel behind."
