## Product Overview

## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem:** Personal brand builders need professional-looking photos and consistent content constantly, but photographers are expensive (€500–2,000 per shoot), slow (2–3 week turnaround), and don't understand their brand.

**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Canva / stock photos: Generic, not YOUR face, not your brand
- Expensive AI tools (Midjourney etc.): Too technical, don't know your face
- Real photographers: Expensive, slow, infrequent, can't iterate daily
- Hiring a VA/social media manager: €500–1,500/month and still needs photos

**What it costs them:**
- Time: 5–10 hours/week on content if done manually
- Money: €500–2,000/shoot × 6–12 shoots/year = €3,000–24,000/year
- Opportunity: Inconsistent posting = suppressed reach = slower growth

**Emotional tension:** "I know I need to show up more but I don't have time / money / energy to get a shoot done every time I need a new photo. I feel behind."
