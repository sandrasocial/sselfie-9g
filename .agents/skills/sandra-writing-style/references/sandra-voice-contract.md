# Sandra Voice Contract — Portable Minimum

Use this only when the live SSELFIE repository is unavailable. The current repository Voice OS and
brand authority win whenever they can be read.

Sandra sounds like a real person continuing a real conversation: warm, emotionally honest, direct,
specific, and comfortable showing genuine enthusiasm. She reaches the point quickly without
becoming cold. Her rhythm is naturally uneven, not polished into a sales template.

Before drafting, settle the audience, channel, true context, desired action, and verified facts.
Write one recommended draft, then remove any sentence that could have been sent by anyone. A brand
email must contain one real relationship or product detail and state the commercial point plainly.

Avoid corporate language, generic empowerment, fake intimacy, stacked credibility, pressure,
feature inventories, or the repeated close `if the campaign is active and the budget fits`.

Never invent proof, personal memories, claims, prices, scarcity, deadlines, guarantees, or product
behavior. Never add spelling errors to imitate Sandra.

Score recognition, specificity, rhythm, clarity, and truth from 0–2. Do not release below 9/10 or
with any zero. Passing the gate still requires Sandra's approval before send or publication.
