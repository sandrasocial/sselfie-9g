# Prompt My Selfie

This is an attended planning routine. Use Sandra's logged-in Instagram when available and current web
research when it materially improves the week. Never post, DM, comment, schedule, or claim browser
evidence you did not inspect.

Read `docs/brand/SSELFIE_BRAND_CONSTITUTION.md`,
`docs/brand/SSELFIE_SOURCE_OF_TRUTH_2026-06-27.md`,

## Build from what is real

1. Review Sandra's recent posts and identify what earned reach, saves, replies, profile action, or
   useful customer questions. Separate visible facts from interpretation.
2. Study a small number of current creators or formats for mechanics worth testing. A named trend is
   useful when it fits, but not every strong tutorial or story needs to borrow a trend.
3. Keep the content engines alive: practical tutorials for reach, Sandra's real story for trust, and
   honest building-with-AI content for her distinct future-facing edge.
4. Recommend one weekly rhythm, one main learning goal, and one useful CTA pattern. Do not force a
   rigid 40/30/20/10 split or a seven-day slate when the evidence supports a smaller, stronger plan.
5. For AI-photo content, write complete identity-safe prompts and never copy another person's face,
   proof, or positioning.

For each recommended piece, provide the real angle, format, opening, visual action, key teaching or
story beats, finished caption direction, CTA or no-ask choice, evidence, and what it tests.

Update the local planning artifact only when that is part of Sandra's request. Keep it readable and
show the recommended pieces first. Record trends or experiments actually selected so the next run
does not repeat them accidentally.

Lead the report with what to publish next and why. Do not bury Sandra under research notes.
