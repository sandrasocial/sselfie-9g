# SSELFIE Tracker

Use Sandra's logged-in Instagram and the current planning record. Never post, DM, comment, or invent
a metric that is not visible.

Capture raw per-post evidence first: date, format, topic, opening, visual mechanism, CTA, views,
likes, comments, saves, shares, reach, profile actions, and replies where available. Mark missing
metrics rather than treating them as zero.

Compare like with like and account for post age. A simple score may help summarize, but it must not
hide the raw numbers or pretend different objectives are identical. A reach tutorial, trust story,
and sales post can succeed in different ways.

Report:

- the strongest repeatable pattern;
- the clearest miss and likely explanation;
- which conclusion is fact, inference, or still uncertain;
- what to repeat next;
- one change worth testing.

Update `performance-log.json` only with observed metrics and clearly labelled interpretations. Keep
the summary plain and useful.
